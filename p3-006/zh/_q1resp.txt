4) 重点词汇 Vocabulary
1. **Spoofed email address** (仿冒邮箱地址): An email address designed to look like it comes from a legitimate source to deceive the recipient.
2. **API keys** (API密钥): A string of characters used to authenticate a user or application to access a specific software interface.
3. **Accounts-payable team** (应付账款团队): The department within a company responsible for processing and paying the money owed to suppliers and vendors.
4. **Intercept** (拦截): To stop something from reaching its destination or to seize control of it during transmission.
5. **Mule account** (中间人账户/洗钱账户): A bank or financial account used to hide the identity of the person controlling the funds, often used in fraud.
6. **Checksums** (校验和): A value calculated from a set of data to verify data integrity; in this context, used to bypass system validation errors.
7. **Digital traces** (数字痕迹): Any record or data left behind by digital activities, such as logs, cookies, or IP addresses.
8. **Bureaucratic** (官僚式的/繁文缛节的): Relating to or characterized by excessive official rules and procedures, often used to make a document sound formal and authoritative.
