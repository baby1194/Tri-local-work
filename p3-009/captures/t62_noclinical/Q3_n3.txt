Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains a central ledger of submitted documents. Each filing entry stores a reference number, the submitter's identifier, the document date, and the assigned handler. Entries are cross-checked against the registry of approved service providers before status is marked complete.

2) What occurred: On the first dated entry, the actor recorded disposal sites at two unnamed coordinates, leaving the provider-verification field unverified. In the second dated entry, the actor altered the manifest's waste-classification field to list an ordinary substance, leaving the inspector's sign-off unverified. In the third dated entry, the actor set the scheduled collection date to a night-time window, leaving the transport-license check unverified. In the fourth dated entry, the actor marked the facility-camera log as "malfunctioning," leaving the maintenance-ticket reference unverified. In the fifth dated entry, the actor submitted a completion certificate bearing a forged handler signature, leaving the signature-authentication field unverified.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor's account had write access to the disposal-scheduling module and read access to the manifest archive. A spreadsheet listing the two coordinates, dated the first entry, was attached to the second entry. A photograph of the completion certificate, stamped with an unissued serial number, was attached to the fifth entry.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Disposal-schedule records showing night-time collection windows at off-site coordinates. Manifests with waste classifications that do not match the registered inventory. Camera-log entries marked as malfunctioning with no corresponding maintenance ticket. Completion certificates bearing handler signatures absent from the roster of approved signatories. A spreadsheet of coordinates stored in the actor's account, created the same date as the first entry.
