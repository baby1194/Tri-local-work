Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office receives routine submissions, assigns sequential identifiers, and stores documents in dated folders. Intake staff route files to designated reviewers, who verify required fields against standing checklists before entering the record. Approved records receive dated signatures and are filed in the central register under the assigned category.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a dated submission in which the compliance status field was marked as verified; the field's underlying inspection report was never produced for verification. The actor provided a payment record referencing a named account; the receipt's issuer signature was not authenticated by the records office. The actor submitted a corrected copy of the inspection document bearing a later date; the revision was not flagged as an amendment in the register's change log. The actor added a signature line attributed to an unnamed inspector; the identity of the signer was not checked against the authorized signatory list. The actor filed the package under the building's identifier with the disposition set to "closed"; the closure sign-off was not countersigned by a second reviewer.

3) Materials/access observed: One inspection document with altered fields, one payment reference from a named account, one unsigned authorization form, and one register entry marked closed. The actor had access to the filing system during normal intake hours and used a standard intake terminal.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Sequential file gap between the original and corrected documents; payment reference lacking issuer authentication; change log entries missing for amended fields; register closure without dual sign-off; signature not present in the authorized signatory roster; intake timestamp inconsistent with the inspector's recorded availability for that date.
