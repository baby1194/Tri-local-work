Forensic findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: Records offices maintain structured files with unique identifiers, audit trails, and version control. Changes to certified records require change logs with reason codes, timestamps, and reviewer attestations, and are filed with the original documents.

2) What occurred: The actor opened the airworthiness file for the aircraft and located the parts-transmission register. The actor created a backdated entry in the parts register showing the component's installation date preceding the actual installation date. The actor opened the component's serial-number record and changed the part-history field to reference a compliant part number. The actor submitted the modified record through the internal records channel to the certification office. The actor added a reviewer-attestation field bearing the name of a named account.

3) Materials/access observed: Parts-transmission register; component serial-number record; part-history field; installation-date field; part-number field; reviewer-attestation field; internal records channel; certification-office intake folder; a named account with reviewer privileges; a dated record entry.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Installation date earlier than the component's manufacture date; part-number field mismatch between the register and the physical component tag; reviewer attestation by an account that had no prior access to the file; backdated record lacking a change-reason code; channel submission timestamp inconsistent with the filed entry date; missing version-control entry for the altered record.
