- The actor registered each derivative document in a listing channel with a price field; the channel listing verification sign-off was not obtained.

- The actor applied a chargeback waiver code to the transaction record; the waiver approval field was left blank.
- The actor disabled the identity matching flag in the gate entry document; the identity verification sign-off was not obtained.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Duplicate serial values across seven document IDs; blank waiver approval field in the transaction record; disabled identity matching flag in the gate entry document; missing channel listing verification sign-off on all derivative documents; queue sequence timestamp preceding holder timestamp by 14 seconds.
