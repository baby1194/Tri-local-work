Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A records office receives routine paperwork for filing, assigns each document a sequential identifier, and stores the original in an indexed archive. Staff record the submission date, the submitting party, and the filing reference on the document itself, then route it through a single review checkpoint before the entry becomes part of the permanent record.

2) Unverified fields:

- The actor entered an unverified entry in the serial-number field of the component, and the cross-check against the manufacturer's serial registry remained unverified.
- The actor signed the maintenance log's completion field, and the sign-off's verification against the signed technician's license remained unverified.
- The actor transmitted the altered maintenance document to an aviation regulator's filing channel, and the channel's source-authentication check remained unverified.
- The actor submitted a second dated record amending the original part-history entry, and the amendment's approval sign-off remained unverified.

3) Materials/access observed: A maintenance-log document bearing a serial-number field, a completion sign-off field, and a dated part-history entry. A supplier certificate of conformity. A manufacturer serial registry reference. A regulator filing channel with a source-authentication check. A named technician account with license verification. A named airline maintenance account.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A part-replacement record whose serial number does not match the manufacturer registry entry.
