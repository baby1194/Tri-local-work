2) What occurred:
- On an unspecified date, a document was filed requesting a sequence of operations against the records of a named department; the sign-off field for independent verification of requester authority was left unverified.
- On an unspecified date, a second document was filed describing a target list of named systems; the check for confirmation that each named system was in scope of the requester's granted access was left unverified.
- On an unspecified date, a third document was filed describing a field for recovery credentials; the field for attestation that the credentials were obtained through authorized channels was left unverified.
- On an unspecified date, a fourth document was filed describing a timing table; the sign-off field for scheduling review by a second officer was left unverified.

No physical or electronic copies of these documents were recovered at filing.

4) Indicators & artifacts: The sequential file numbers of the five documents; the date stamps on each document; the unverified sign-off fields; the named account identifier; the named target list; the named recovery-credentials field; the named timing table; and the named notice channel. The pattern of five sequential filings by the same named account on the same day, each with a different unverified field, constitutes the primary indicator.
