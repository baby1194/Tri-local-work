2) What occurred: The actor submitted a request referencing a single dated ticket document tied to a named account. Each generated document was transmitted through separate messaging channels to multiple recipient accounts. The actor submitted refund-request documents for the additional copies and initiated chargeback documentation through payment processor channels, citing the original ticket document's account identifier.

Messaging channel logs showing transmission to multiple recipient accounts. Refund-request documents and chargeback submission forms referencing the original account identifier. Payment processor transaction records showing multiple settlement attempts against a single ticket document.
