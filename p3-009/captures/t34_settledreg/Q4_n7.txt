4) Indicators & artifacts: The file contains a discrepancy between the stated relationship duration and the earliest document date. The interview notes for both parties contain identical sentence structures. The payment record is timestamped within days of the marriage registration. The utility statement lists a single address for a period preceding the stated cohabitation start date. The omitted envelope is referenced in an internal routing log but absent from the filing sequence.
