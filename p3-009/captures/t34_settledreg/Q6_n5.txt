Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by maintaining dated document records, designated data fields, and approved submission channels. Each entry is indexed to a specific account, stamped with a date, and routed through authorized pathways for validation and storage.

2) What occurred: The actor created a modified component certificate of conformity dated to the original installation period, altering the serial number field to match a pre-existing inventory record. The actor then edited the maintenance log entry in the aircraft's technical log, replacing the original part installation field with the substituted serial and backdating the entry to the prior maintenance cycle. The actor submitted both documents via the maintenance data channel to the records account, and separately transmitted a corrected part history document to the regulatory submission portal account, marking the status field as airworthy.

3) Materials/access observed: A named component certificate document with a falsified serial number field; a dated technical log entry with modified installation and date fields; a part history document with altered provenance fields; access to the maintenance records account; access to the regulatory submission portal account; and a dated transmission record in the data channel showing sequential submission of all three documents.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A discrepancy between the component certificate date and the part history acquisition date; a serial number appearing in inventory records before the certified installation date; a maintenance log entry with an edit timestamp later than the recorded maintenance date; and a regulatory submission with an airworthy status field inconsistent with the underlying part certification record.
