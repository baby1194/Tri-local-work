1) Domain context:

The records office maintains a chronological index of administrative documents. Each intake is assigned a sequential identifier, a filing date, and a custodian reference. Documents are cross-referenced against standard checklists, signed by responsible parties, and archived in read-only binders after final disposition. No substantive review is performed during intake; the office only logs presence, sequence, and signature fields as submitted.
