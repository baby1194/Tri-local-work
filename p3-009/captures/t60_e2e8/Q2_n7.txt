Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office maintains paper and electronic files for routine submissions. Intake clerks log each submission with a unique reference number, a receipt date, and the submitting party's identifier. Files are assigned to a processing queue based on the service type indicated on the intake form.

2) What occurred: On a dated record, the actor submitted a service request form bearing a reference number, while the verification check for occupancy status remained unverified. On a subsequent dated document, the actor entered a payment reference in the settlement field, while the cross-check against the outstanding balance stayed unverified. On a later dated entry, the actor recorded an inspection outcome in the status field, while the supervisory sign-off stayed unverified. On the following dated record, the actor appended a correction note to the file index, while the audit log for modifications remained unverified.

3) Materials/access observed: A standard intake form with numbered fields, a payment processing terminal with a printed receipt, a physical file cabinet with a dated folder tab, a supervisory signature block, and an electronic audit log export.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Reference number mismatch between the intake form and the payment receipt, a payment timestamp preceding the inspection date, a status field altered after the supervisory signature date, and a correction note added outside the standard amendment window.
