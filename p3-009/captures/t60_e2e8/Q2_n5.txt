Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A records office files each submission in a dated register entry, links it to a named account, and routes it to the assigned verifier. Each verification step writes its own checkmark field on the document, and the file is sealed only when the final sign-off field carries the verifier's dated initial. Entries remain retrievable by date and account number.

2) What occurred: On a dated day, the actor opened a correspondence channel to the named inspector account and sent a message proposing a payment in exchange for a favorable report, leaving the inspector's acknowledgment field on that message unverified. On the next dated day, the actor produced a modified inspection report in which the violation fields showed a passed status; the original defect-list field on that report remained unverified against the site survey. On the third dated day, the actor submitted the modified report to the records office and recorded a passed status in the closure field, while the authenticity-check field on the intake form was never completed. On the fourth dated day, the actor logged a note in the status-history field describing the report as "routine corrections," while the edit-timestamp field on the prior entry remained unreviewed by any second verifier.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a named personal account with sender privileges on the correspondence channel, a draft document carrying an editable defect-list field, a dated inspection report with a passed-status field, the records-office intake form with an uncompleted authenticity-check field, and a status-history field with append-only logging. The actor held read access to prior register entries and write access to the closure and status-history fields.

4) Indicators & artifacts: An unverified acknowledgment field on a message in the correspondence channel proposing a payment to a named inspector account. A modified inspection report whose passed-status field is inconsistent with an unverified original defect-list field. An intake form with a populated closure field but an empty authenticity-check field. A status-history entry dated after the intake, marked as corrections, with an unreviewed edit-timestamp field on the preceding entry.
