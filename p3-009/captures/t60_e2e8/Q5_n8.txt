**Gate/entry sequence anomalies**
- Multiple documents derived from one original scanned at the same venue, within a short window

**Financial / refund-avoidance patterns**
- Payment routing through accounts with rapid open-close cycles
- Buyer accounts created, used once, and never reactivated

**Cross-channel correlation**
- Same device fingerprint, IP range, or session token across the seller, buyer, and gate-scan events
