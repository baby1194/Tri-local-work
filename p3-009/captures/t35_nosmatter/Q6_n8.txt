Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains standardized filing systems where documents are indexed by document identifier, date, and account. Ordinary paperwork flows through designated channels, updates specific fields in controlled registers, and retains an unbroken audit trail linking each revision to the responsible personnel and timestamp.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a revised maintenance document for a passenger aircraft, altering the component identifier field to reflect an approved part number in place of the installed unapproved unit. The actor amended the part history register by inserting a dated record showing traceability to a certified supplier. The actor modified the airworthiness release field in the maintenance log to certify compliance. The actor transmitted the revised documents through an approved electronic submission channel to the airline and aviation regulator. The actor updated the component tracking database to replace the unapproved unit identifier with the approved unit identifier and closed the maintenance item as complete.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the maintenance log management system, the parts traceability database, the airworthiness certification document repository, and the electronic regulatory submission portal. The actor used an authenticated account with write permissions to the part history register and certification fields. The actor possessed the original maintenance work order, the component procurement record, and the aircraft maintenance manual section covering the affected system.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Detection indicators include a component identifier in the part history register that does not match the procurement record supplier or lot number. The maintenance log shows an airworthiness release signature dated after an unapproved part installation without a corresponding deviation approval document. The parts database shows a unit identifier that appears in no certified supplier delivery record. The regulatory submission channel shows a document hash that differs from the hash of the document filed with the airline. The account access log shows field edits to the certification section by a personnel account lacking authorized certifying staff designation. The maintenance item closure timestamp precedes the completion timestamp of the underlying work order.
