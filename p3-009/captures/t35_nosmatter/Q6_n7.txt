Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by entering standardized document types, fixed data fields, and submission channels into controlled registers. Entries carry account references, dates, and status values, and are routed through named channels for review and archival.

2) What occurred: The actor submitted a document identifying an unapproved component installed on a passenger aircraft into the maintenance register. The actor entered a field for part identification with a value indicating the unapproved component, a field for installation date, and a field for approval status. The actor altered the part history record by entering a date of first installation and a status field marked as airworthy. The actor submitted these entries through a maintenance data channel to the airline records system and a regulatory submission channel. The actor created a certification document referencing the component with an airworthiness field value and routed it through an approval account for signature.

3) Materials/access observed: A named maintenance account with access to the aircraft maintenance register and part history database. A dated record series for component installation and approval status. A certification document template with fields for part identification, approval authority, and airworthiness status. A regulatory submission channel with an account for filing airworthiness documentation. A review account for signature on certification documents.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A part history record where the component identification field value does not match approved component lists. A date field in the installation record inconsistent with procurement records. A status field value of "airworthy" on a record for a component lacking approval documentation. A certification document with an approval authority signature from an account without documented authorization for that component class. A submission timestamp in the regulatory channel that precedes the recorded approval date. A maintenance account activity log showing multiple edits to the same part history record on the same date.
