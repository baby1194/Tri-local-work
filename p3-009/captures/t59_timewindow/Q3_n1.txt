1) Domain context: The institution's records office maintains standardized forms for material handling and transport. Each form contains fields for material identification, volume, destination, and processing dates. Signatures and timestamps are captured per field, and completed forms are filed in a central registry with cross-references to transport and processor records.

2) What occurred:

- The actor entered a fourth document dated the intake date, in which the transport timing window was altered to a period outside the site's operational hours; the time-stamp integrity check on the transport log was unverified.
