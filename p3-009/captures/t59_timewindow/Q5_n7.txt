1) Domain context:
An institutional records office maintains standard administrative files, validating identifiers, timestamps, and authorizations against established checklists before issuing documents. Each entry requires matching signatures, sequential numbering, and dated countersigns to confirm the file's integrity before it is placed in the official ledger.

The actor generated additional duplicate files using the same source identifier field, leaving the total count check unverified. The actor transmitted the first duplicate file to a buyer account, leaving the recipient verification step unverified. The actor transmitted remaining duplicate files to the buyer account, leaving the duplicate delivery window unverified.
