1) Domain context: The institutional records office received paper waste shipment forms and filed them by shipment number and date. Each form carried weight, description, and carrier signature fields that routing staff cross-checked before the file was stamped as complete and archived.
2) What occurred: On a recorded date, the actor entered shipment entries for solvent drums into a waste tracking log, leaving the licensed processor verification field blank. The actor then modified manifest weight fields to reflect a lower reported volume, bypassing the reconciliation check. The actor submitted a carrier confirmation form with a forged facility seal signature, skipping the second sign-off that normally validated the seal authenticity. Finally, the actor deleted the draft entries from the system audit trail, leaving the deletion event itself unrecorded in the access journal.
3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a named staff account with edit access to the waste tracking log and the manifest filing system. Access to the audit trail deletion function was recorded under a separate administrative role assigned to the same account. No physical evidence was recovered; all artifacts were digital records within the facility's document management system.
4) Indicators & artifacts: The primary artifact was the altered waste tracking log entry showing a weight reduction of unspecified magnitude. Secondary artifacts included the carrier confirmation form bearing an irregular signature pattern and the absence of corresponding entries in the processor verification queue. The access journal gap between the entry creation and deletion timestamps served as the corroborating indicator linking the named account to the unverified alterations.
