Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context:
The institutional records office maintains a permanent ledger of all ticket transactions, attendee identifiers, and payment references. Entries are indexed by event date, ticket serial, and purchaser profile. The system requires a single valid identifier per seat, with all transfer, entry, and refund events logged against that specific record for audit trail integrity.

2) What occurred:
The actor generated multiple duplicate ticket serials linked to the original valid ticket identifier.
The actor transmitted the generated serials to multiple external buyer accounts via a direct messaging channel.
The actor presented the first generated serial at the physical entry point, bypassing the single-use verification check.
The actor initiated chargeback requests on the original ticket purchase record using the buyer payment references.
The actor modified the attendee name field on the original ticket to match a different registered profile.
The actor disabled the automated refund notification for the original ticket account.

3) Materials/access observed:
A valid digital ticket containing a unique QR code and seat assignment.
A named account associated with the original ticket purchase.
A dated payment reference for the ticket transaction.
External messaging channels used to transmit duplicate ticket data.
A physical entry point terminal with verification access.
A chargeback dispute form template.
A refund status indicator field within the ticketing platform.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Multiple QR code scans recorded at the same seat location within a short time window.
Payment disputes referencing the original ticket serial number.
Attendance logs showing multiple unique identifiers for a single seat.
Modification timestamps on the original ticket record indicating name or status changes after initial sale.
Communication logs showing the distribution of identical ticket data to multiple recipients.
Refund suppression flags activated on the original transaction record.
