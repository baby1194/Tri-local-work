Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office stores routine paperwork as dated, indexed documents. Each file item holds fixed fields and standard sign-off markers, and staff reference them by neutral identifiers during normal processing. Records remain linked to the originating account and processing date, with fields kept distinct for later audit.

2) What occurred:
On a dated record, the actor entered a ticket document number into the document field; the validity check field remained unverified.
On a subsequent dated entry, the actor copied the barcode data into a second document field; the uniqueness sign-off stayed unverified.
On a further dated record, the actor prepared the copied barcode data in the output file field; the integrity check remained unverified.
On a later dated entry, the actor referenced a buyer account in the buyer field; the account verification sign-off stayed unverified.
On a following dated record, the actor placed the copied barcode data into a transfer message field; the channel authorization check remained unverified.
On a later dated entry, the actor recorded a transfer timestamp in the date field; the synchronization check stayed unverified.
On a further dated record, the actor entered a payment amount in the amount field; the reconciliation sign-off remained unverified.
On a subsequent dated entry, the actor marked the sale as complete in the status field; the final review check stayed unverified.
On a following dated record, the actor referenced a refund request in the refund field; the refund approval sign-off remained unverified.
On a later dated entry, the actor noted a dispute in the dispute field; the dispute resolution check stayed unverified.
On a final dated record, the actor referenced identity data in the identity field; the identity verification sign-off remained unverified.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed one valid ticket document containing a document number, barcode data, and validity check field. The actor accessed two or more secondary documents containing copied barcode data in output file fields. The actor accessed a buyer account record with an account identifier in the buyer field. The actor accessed a transfer message with a channel authorization check field. The actor accessed dated records with date, amount, status, refund, dispute, and identity fields.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Multiple documents sharing the same ticket document number across distinct dated records.
Barcode data present in secondary documents where a uniqueness sign-off field remained unverified.
