On a final dated record, the account attached a payment-pressure memorandum, its authorization field unverified and its routing field marked untraced.

A rollout plan document bearing a dated record was filed in the administrative register. A schedule field specifying propagation sequencing was documented. A memorandum bearing a payment-pressure field and a routing field marked untraced was observed in the same file.
