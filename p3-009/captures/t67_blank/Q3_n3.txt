Each document carries a unique identifier, a dated receipt field, and a designated custodian sign-off line. The actor submitted a disposal receipt with a generic facility descriptor, leaving the manifest cross-reference field blank. The actor archived the original paper forms without attaching the required custody transfer slip, leaving the seal verification field unsigned.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a shared intake terminal account and a portable scanner to digitize the modified forms. The actor accessed the scheduling module with a standard clerk credential and viewed the camera coverage map document. The actor printed a duplicate manifest draft on the office copier and retained the carbon copy sheet.

4) Indicators & artifacts: The system log shows a manual override of the processor validation check on the recorded date. The manifest checksum field contains a null value where a cryptographic hash was expected. The transport timestamp falls outside business hours, contradicting the standard operating procedure. The custody transfer slip is absent from the archive box index, creating a gap in the sequential document numbering. The intake scan metadata indicates the file was edited after initial submission.
