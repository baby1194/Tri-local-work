Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by indexing each document in a ledger, stamping it with a serial number and date, recording the submitting account, and routing it through sequential verification stages before final closure.

2) What occurred:
- The actor generated a document duplicating the fields of the valid digital ticket (identifier, holder name, event date, seat assignment). The original validation checksum was not verified against the issued record.
- The actor distributed the duplicate documents through an off-platform channel to multiple recipient accounts. The recipient account ownership was not verified against the primary holder.
- The actor submitted a transfer record listing the recipient as the first entry for gate admission. The seat assignment uniqueness was not verified against the original valid ticket.
- The actor filed a refund-suppression document referencing the original transaction. The transaction status was not verified by the issuing institution.
- The actor recorded an identity-obfuscation entry in the recipient contact list. The recipient identity was not verified against the account holder.

3) Materials/access observed:
- Original valid digital ticket document with issuer serial number
- Duplicate ticket documents with altered metadata fields
- Off-platform messaging channel logs
- Payment transfer records showing multiple recipient accounts
- Refund suppression document with transaction reference
- Contact list entries with masked identifier fields

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Multiple documents sharing the same issuer serial number with differing timestamps
- Transfer records with sequential recipient accounts registered within a short interval
- Gate admission entry not matching the original seat assignment
- Refund suppression document filed after multiple transfer records
- Contact list entries with incomplete identifier fields lacking verification status
