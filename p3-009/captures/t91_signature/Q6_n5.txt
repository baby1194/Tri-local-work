Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains paper and electronic filing systems for standard correspondence. Staff process incoming forms using predefined fields, standard dates, and designated signature lines. Filed materials are indexed by account and date, with routine checks applied before records are placed in the active archive.

2) What occurred: On a dated record, the actor entered an unapproved component identifier into a part-history field, leaving the original approval verification unverified. On a subsequent dated record, the actor altered a maintenance log entry in a service-date field, leaving the inspection sign-off unverified. On a third dated record, the actor modified an installation certificate in a component-serial field, leaving the certification authority stamp unverified. On a fourth dated record, the actor updated an airworthiness release document in a compliance-status field, leaving the releasing inspector's signature unverified. On a fifth dated record, the actor adjusted a parts-traceability document in a supplier-authorization field, leaving the supplier verification code unverified.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a named account on the maintenance record system to access dated log files. Electronic forms with dated entries and signature blocks were observed. A paper maintenance logbook with handwritten corrections was observed. A parts inventory spreadsheet with modified cells was observed.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Altered service-date fields in dated maintenance logs. Modified component-serial fields on installation certificates. Changed compliance-status fields in airworthiness release documents. Updated supplier-authorization fields in parts-traceability records. Absence of verification stamps or signatures in the modified fields. Dated records showing sequential entries without corresponding independent verification checks.
