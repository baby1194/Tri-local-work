1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office receives immigration filings, indexes each submission by applicant identifier, and files supporting documents in chronological order. Each form entry, attachment, and correspondence is catalogued with receipt dates, cross-referenced against prior filings, and retained for audit review.

2) What occurred:

- The actor compiled a list of potential partners in a separate document; the consent form field for each listed individual remained unsigned and unverified.
- The actor produced correspondence, photographs, and dated communications simulating a relationship; the authenticity check for each dated record showed no independent verification.
- The actor drafted interview preparation notes for both parties; the training log field was not submitted for review.
- The actor recorded payment terms in a separate document; the disclosure field in the main filing remained blank.
- The actor submitted the primary application; the declaration field regarding payment was not marked or initialed by the actor.
