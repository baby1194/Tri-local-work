Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains event admission records in a central registry. Each ticket is a dated document with a unique identifier, holder account, and venue gate log entries. Transfers are filed as amendment records linking old and new holder accounts under the event date and venue code.

2) What occurred: The actor filed a duplication request against a valid ticket document on a dated record. The actor altered the ticket's unique identifier field on the original dated record. The actor issued several amendment records copying the original identifier to newly created holder accounts. The actor filed a priority gate entry request for the first named buyer account. The actor submitted a refund waiver filing against the event date. The actor filed a dispute suppression request against the buyer account. The actor requested an identity verification hold against the gate log entries.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a valid digital ticket document, a central event registry, a named holder account, a dated amendment record, a gate log, and a financial dispute channel.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Multiple documents share one unique identifier under different holder accounts. A single event date shows several amendment filings from one source account. A priority gate entry request precedes standard queue filings. A refund waiver and dispute suppression filing are linked to the same event code. An identity verification hold request targets gate log entries tied to the duplicated identifier.
