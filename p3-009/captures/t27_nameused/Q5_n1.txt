Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork using standardized intake forms, sequentially numbered document IDs, designated storage channels, and dated retention fields. Each record references one originating account, one submission date, and one review status, ensuring neutral, traceable filing without commentary on intent or content.
2) What occurred: The actor created a dated document recording a single valid ticket reference. The actor produced multiple additional documents replicating the original reference number, QR field, and barcode field. The actor transmitted the original document to a named buyer account via a first channel, timed to precede gate verification. The actor transmitted the duplicate documents to a second account via a second channel. The actor filed a suppression record in the refunds field and a suppression record in the chargebacks field, both referencing the original ticket date. The actor filed a non-identification record referencing the buyer account and the original document ID.
3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the original ticket document, including its reference field, QR field, barcode field, and date field. The actor accessed the duplicate-generation channel producing additional documents. The actor accessed the first transmission channel for the original document. The actor accessed the second transmission channel for the duplicate documents. The actor accessed the refunds field, the chargebacks field, and the identification field for filing suppression and non-identification records.
4) Indicators & artifacts: An original document bearing a single valid reference. Multiple duplicate documents sharing the same reference field value. A dated transmission record showing the original document sent before the duplicates. A second dated transmission record showing the duplicate documents sent to a different account. A filed record in the refunds field marked suppression. A filed record in the chargebacks field marked suppression. A filed record in the identification field marked non-identification, referencing the buyer account and original document ID.
