1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by entering entries into a central register, assigning sequential reference numbers, and requiring dated signatures on each field. Standard practice keeps every line item attributable to a named account and a completed check, so that each document can be audited in sequence.

2) What occurred:

- On a following date, the actor prepared a second transport document for the same item and left the licensed-processor field blank on the consignment form.
