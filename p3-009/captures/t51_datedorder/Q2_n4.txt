- The file's review sign-off field was completed without the designated verifier's confirmation.

- A duplicate inspection form with altered outcome text.
- A pre-signed authorization sheet dated prior to the official inspection date.
- A payment receipt listing a non-standard fee category.
- Access to the physical records cabinet during an unlogged maintenance window.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Mismatch between the inspector's original field notes and the final archived copy.
- Sequential reference numbers showing a gap corresponding to the removed original form.
- Signature timestamps on the duplicate sheet preceding the official visit date.
- Unlogged access to the records cabinet on the date of alteration.
- Fee category in the receipt not present in the standard schedule.
