The actor utilized administrative access to the dispatch log and the document management system, bypassing the standard secondary-review workflow for hazardous materials documentation.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Discrepancies between the inventory sheet and the manifest volume field. Anomalous entries in the dispatch log showing a timestamp modification.
