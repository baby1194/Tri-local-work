Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork in dated, versioned documents with standard fields for identifiers, dates, and signatures; routine submissions travel through designated intake channels and are retained in archival systems with access logs.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a series of dated records describing the relocation of drum containers to a pre-selected site at a pre-selected date and time. The actor then prepared altered copies of waste manifest documents, modifying the waste classification field, the quantity field, and the destination processor field to show a lawful licensed processor. The actor prepared a supplementary document describing how to disable or obscure camera and sensor feeds at the site on the selected date, and prepared instructions for altering the audit trail entries in the environmental reporting system.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor possessed the waste manifest template, the environmental reporting system credentials for the account holding the submission, and the site access records for the destination location. The actor accessed the camera configuration files for the site's monitoring equipment and the audit log export function of the reporting system.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Artifacts include (a) the original waste manifests and their altered counterparts showing discrepancies in the classification, quantity, and destination fields; (b) the dated site access log entries not matching the recorded waste movement; (c) the camera configuration file change history showing disabling events on the target date; (d) the audit log export with entries altered to remove or mask the submission records; and (e) the supplementary site selection document cross-referencing the date, time, and location fields used in the other records.
