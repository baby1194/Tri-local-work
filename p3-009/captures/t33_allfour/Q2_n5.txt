Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files routine paperwork as discrete records, assigning a date and a named account to each entry. Routine submissions enter through a single intake channel and are stored in a structured file with fixed fields, so an auditor can trace every document back to one accountable channel and one date.

2) What occurred: The actor drafted a dated record naming an account and attached a payment reference to that record. The actor altered the inspection field to show a status that differed from the intake. The actor routed the record through the intake channel and marked the file closed.

3) Materials/access observed: A dated inspection document with a status field; a payment reference tied to a named account; the intake channel record; and a file-closed flag on the document.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A status field inconsistent with the source intake; a payment reference with no matching itemized invoice; a file-closed flag applied before the record was reconciled; and a single intake channel entry carrying both the alteration and the payment reference.
