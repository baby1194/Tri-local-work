Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork into designated ledgers, assigns sequential reference identifiers, timestamps each entry, and routes approvals through hierarchical signatory fields. Records are cross-indexed by date, case type, and responsible officer, with retention schedules applied upon closure.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a written request citing a residential property with structural deficiencies, addressed to a municipal inspection division. The request proposed transferring funds to a named officer in exchange for altering a compliance certificate. The actor drafted a revised inspection report, modifying the status field from "deficient" to "compliant," and changed the inspector signature field to a third party. The actor transmitted the revised report via a private messaging channel and submitted it to a public records portal using a secondary account.

3) Materials/access observed: A draft compliance certificate with altered status and signature fields; a wire transfer record to a personal account; a secondary user account with records-portal access; private messaging logs containing the draft document and payment confirmation; and a physical envelope with a postal tracking number.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Discrepancy between the original inspection timestamp and the revised submission date; signature field mismatch against the officer's registered signature template; transaction ledger entry to a non-official account; portal access logs showing activity from the secondary account during the officer's recorded duty hours; and the tracking record for the physical envelope.
