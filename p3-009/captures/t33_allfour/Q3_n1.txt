Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office receives, indexes, and stores submitted documents in a bound or electronic register. Each entry is assigned a serial number and date. Standard fields capture the submitter's name, the document title, and a description of contents. A clerk verifies signatures and seals before archiving.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a set of waste manifest documents containing fields for waste description, quantity, and destination. The actor populated the description field with an innocuous term and the quantity field with a value lower than the physical count. The actor routed these documents through the electronic intake channel, bypassing the signature field. The actor then created a secondary entry in a private log recording the actual quantities and the off-register disposal site. The actor scheduled the transport for a date after the last recorded inspection, using the private log to time the vehicle's departure against the facility's camera maintenance window.

3) Materials/access observed: Observed materials included blank pre-numbered manifest forms, a handheld data entry terminal with access to the facility's electronic filing system, and a private, unencrypted spreadsheet. The actor accessed the electronic filing system using an account named "Logistics_Lead". The actor also accessed the camera maintenance schedule, which was stored in a shared network drive.

4) Indicators & artifacts: The electronic filing system shows a gap in serial numbers corresponding to the omitted manifests. The waste description field contains a term inconsistent with the facility's approved chemical inventory. The quantity field in the submitted manifest is 40% lower than the inventory deduction recorded in the stockroom log. The private log contains a coordinate pair and a timestamp matching the transport vehicle's GPS data. The transport vehicle's license plate appears in the gate logs at a time when the facility's main camera system was offline.
