Each entry consists of a dated document, a designated routing channel, and a designated sign-off field. Verification occurs when a field value is reconciled against a reference list prior to archiving.

The actor requested a routing slip from a supervisor account without a corresponding pickup confirmation; the supervisor sign-off field remained unverified.
