Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context:
A generic institutional records office receives, logs, and stores standardized documents in sequential order. Intake staff validate document identifiers and issue confirmation numbers. Stored records are indexed by date and reference code for retrieval and audit purposes.

2) What occurred:
On an undated record, the actor registered a single valid ticket credential into the system's primary ledger. On a dated record, the actor executed a duplication procedure generating multiple ticket credentials from the single source credential. On a dated record, the actor assigned a gate-priority flag to the first duplicated ticket credential. On a dated record, the actor modified the refund and chargeback fields of the duplicated ticket credentials to suppress reversal processing. On a dated record, the actor removed the identity-verification sign-off requirement from the duplicated ticket credentials' transfer protocol.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor accessed the ticket credential ledger, the ticket duplication module, the gate-priority flag configuration, the refund and chargeback processing fields, and the identity-verification sign-off protocol settings.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Multiple ticket credentials sharing a single source credential ID. Gate-priority flag present on duplicated ticket credentials. Refund and chargeback fields set to null or suppressed values. Identity-verification sign-off field blank on transferred ticket credentials. Dated records showing sequential credential generation from one source.
