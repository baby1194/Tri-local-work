Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context:
A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork via dated documents, standard fields, and sign-off columns. Entries are indexed by identifier, retained in sequence, and routed to a single authority for verification before the file is closed.

2) What occurred:
- On a dated record, the actor entered an unapproved component identifier into a part-number field, leaving the original approval-number field unverified.
- The actor amended a serial-number field in a tracking document, leaving the factory-issued serial field unverified.
- The actor altered a date field in a maintenance log, leaving the original entry date field unverify.
- The actor added a sign-off to a compliance section, leaving the named authority's verification field unverified.
- The actor submitted a document through a standard channel, leaving the intake-stamp field unverified.
- The actor updated a status field in the record, leaving the pre-change status field unverified.
- The actor closed the record with a completion mark, leaving the final audit-check field unverified.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor used a named account to access the records system. A dated document with a part-number field was present. A tracking document with a serial-number field was present. A maintenance log with a date field was present. A compliance document with a sign-off field was present. A standard channel was used for submission. A completion mark was applied to the record.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- A part-number field contains an identifier not matching the approval-number field.
- A serial-number field differs from the factory-issued serial field.
- A date field in a maintenance log post-dates the original entry.
- A sign-off field contains a mark without a corresponding verification entry.
- A channel submission lacks an intake-stamp field.
- A status field shows a change without a pre-change status entry.
- A completion mark appears without a final audit-check field entry.
