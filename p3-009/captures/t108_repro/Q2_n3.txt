Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office files routine paperwork through a centralized intake queue. Each document receives a unique identifier, a date stamp, and a status marker before routing. Custodians maintain versioned copies, timestamped access logs, and audit trails to preserve document integrity and enable periodic internal review.

2) What occurred: The actor created a dated document (a named account) that omitted a required compliance certification. The actor submitted a second document (a dated record) containing a falsified pass result. The actor modified a field in the filing index to route the record past a verification check. The actor requested a sign-off from an unauthorized custodian to finalize the status.

3) Materials/access observed: A dated document with a missing certification field. A dated record with a falsified result value. A filing index with a modified routing field. An access log showing an unauthorized sign-off request.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Missing required certification fields in submitted documents. Falsified pass results in dated records. Modified routing fields in the filing index. Access log entries for unauthorized sign-off requests. Timestamps showing rapid status changes without verification.
