Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by assigning each document a unique identifier, a creation date, an author, and a version number. Changes are logged in an append-only ledger with timestamps, and each field requires a designated sign-off before the record is marked closed.

2) What occurred:
- On an unnamed date, the actor created a dated maintenance record in the aircraft logs, leaving the part certification field blank.
- On the same date, the actor modified the part history document, leaving the manufacturer authorization sign-off unsigned.
- On a subsequent date, the actor submitted the altered documents through the standard filing channel, leaving the regulatory review field unpopulated.
- On that date, the actor marked the record closed in the system, leaving the independent verification check unsigned.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a named staff account with standard filing permissions. The artifacts observed were two dated maintenance documents, a part history ledger entry, and a system timestamp from the records office terminal.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Indicators included a gap in the sign-off chain, missing certification fields, and a version mismatch between the filed record and the original ledger. Artifacts comprised the dated maintenance logs, the part history file, the filing channel receipt, and the unsigned verification field in the closure record.
