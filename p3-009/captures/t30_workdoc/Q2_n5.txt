Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: Municipal inspection offices receive submissions through intake portals or physical dropboxes. Staff log submissions into a tracking register with a date and case ID, assign a responsible inspector, and file outcome documents against the record. Completion updates a status field and closes the case in the public index.

2) What occurred: The actor opened the municipal case file for the cited property and identified the open fire-safety citation with an associated repair cost entry. The actor prepared a payment document on personal stationery, naming a bank account, a date, and an amount matching the cited cost, marked the instrument "paid in full," and submitted it through the standard intake channel under the owner's name. The actor then filed an amended inspection outcome document stating the violations were remedied, with a completion date preceding the submission date. The status field in the tracking register was changed from "open" to "closed - remediated." A correspondence record was filed showing the inspector acknowledged the payment document, with a signature block and a date consistent with the submission date.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used an authenticated owner portal session and the associated account credentials. The payment document referenced a named bank account and a routing value. The amended inspection document carried a form number, a case identifier, and a digital signature block. The correspondence record referenced an inspector identifier and an office distribution code. The tracking register entry showed a modification timestamp and an editor field populated with the owner account name.

4) Indicators & artifacts: An owner account performing an edit normally restricted to inspector roles. A completion date in the outcome document predating the payment date. A payment instrument issued by a private account to a case file rather than a treasury instrument. A register entry with a modification event outside business hours and an editor field matching the property owner. A distribution code on the correspondence record that does not match the office of record. The original citation and the amended outcome document both present in the file, creating a paired record pair with conflicting dates and a single shared case identifier.
