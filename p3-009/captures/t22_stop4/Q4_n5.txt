Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office maintains case files as discrete documents with dated entries, indexed fields, and tracked communications. Each filing is associated with a named account and a reference number, with amendments recorded as versioned documents rather than overwrites. Channels of correspondence are logged with timestamps and sender identifiers.

2) What occurred: The actor opened a case file under a named account for a status petition. The actor filed a marriage certificate as a primary supporting document, linking two named individuals. The actor entered a relationship-attestation form into the case, with fields for cohabitation address and date of relationship. The actor attached photographic correspondence records between the two named individuals, spanning a dated range inconsistent with the entered relationship date. The actor prepared a written interview-preparation document listing expected questions and scripted responses for both named individuals. The actor created a separate financial-record entry showing a transfer between accounts, and set a visibility flag on that entry to exclude it from the standard case-bundle export. The actor filed a supplemental statement in which both named individuals described a relationship history differing in material fields from the attestation form.

3) Materials/access observed: A marriage certificate bearing a registry number and issue date. A relationship-attestation form with cohabitation and relationship-date fields. A set of correspondence and photographic records with metadata timestamps. A written preparation document with question-response pairs. A financial transfer record between two accounts with a visibility-flag field set to exclude. A supplemental statement with narrative fields. Case-file access logs showing the named account and an additional account associated with the second individual.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Relationship-date field values on the attestation form inconsistent with metadata timestamps on correspondence records. A financial transfer record dated after the entered relationship date, with an exclude-from-export flag set on that entry. Interview-preparation material containing scripted response text attributable to both named individuals. A supplemental statement with material field conflicts against the attestation form. Access-log entries for the case file from two distinct accounts on the same dated day prior to a scheduled interview entry.
