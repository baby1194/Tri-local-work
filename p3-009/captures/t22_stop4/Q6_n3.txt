Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by entering data into designated form fields, attaching supporting documents, and routing them through defined approval channels for review and retention in official registers.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a part installation record for the aircraft, populating the part number field with the unapproved component identifier, the installation date field with the actual installation date, and the certificate of airworthiness reference field with a valid certificate number. The actor then prepared a maintenance log entry describing the component as the approved part, entered it into the maintenance log document dated the actual installation date, and submitted it through the standard maintenance submission channel. The actor prepared a part history document showing an unbroken service history for the unapproved component, entered it into the part history register, and submitted it through the standard parts submission channel. The actor prepared a return-to-service request referencing the maintenance log entry and part history document, and submitted it through the standard return-to-service channel to the aviation regulator.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the maintenance log document, the part history register, the certificate of airworthiness reference database, and the return-to-service submission channel. The actor possessed a named staff account with write access to the maintenance log and part history register.

4) Indicators & artifacts: The maintenance log entry contains a part number field value that does not match the part approved in the type certificate for that position. The part history document shows a first installation date for the unapproved component that is later than the date recorded in the maintenance log entry. The certificate of airworthiness reference field in the installation record references a certificate that does not list the unapproved component. The return-to-service request references a maintenance log entry whose part number field value differs from the part number in the type certificate. The maintenance log entry and part history document were entered by the same named staff account within a short time window.
