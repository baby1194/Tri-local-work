Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: The generic institutional records office maintains paper and electronic inspection logs, binds them to date-stamped case files, and indexes them by property address and assigned staff identifiers. Routine filings attach photographic annexes, deficiency lists, and correction status codes, with all entries cross-referenced through the central case-management registry.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a written proposal addressed to a named inspection account, offering financial compensation in exchange for a dated record alteration. The proposal was transmitted via a private electronic channel and referenced specific fields within the building's compliance file. Subsequently, the actor delivered physical currency to an individual, and the named account modified a dated record within the electronic case file. The modification changed the deficiency status code from "open violation" to "compliant," removed photographic annexes from the record, and updated the last-revised timestamp. The actor then requested a certified copy of the amended file from the records office.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor possessed physical currency, a draft proposal document, access to the private electronic channel, and knowledge of the internal case-management registry structure. The named account had administrative write access to the electronic inspection file, authority to delete annexes, and authority to issue certified copies from the records office. The electronic case file contained a deficiency list, status codes, photographic annexes, and revision timestamps.

4) Indicators & artifacts: The electronic file shows a status code change with no corresponding correction documentation. Photographic annexes were deleted without a deletion log entry. The revision timestamp was altered to predate the currency transaction. A certified copy was issued within twenty-four hours of the modification. The private channel logs show a message containing a monetary amount matching the physical currency transaction. The named account's access log shows out-of-hours write activity to the file. The case file lacks the standard correction-verification signature required for status changes.
