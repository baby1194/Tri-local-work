Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A municipal code-enforcement records office files inspection paperwork through a case-management system: intake reports, photo logs, correction schedules, and re-inspection sign-offs are stored as dated, linked documents, with each status field changing only when the associated check is verified and authorized.
2) What occurred: The actor opened a dated case file against the property, recording a violation list with cost estimates. The actor transmitted a payment instrument, via an untraceable channel, to the assigned inspector's personal account, creating an account transfer dated within the correction window. The actor then caused a re-inspection document, bearing a date preceding the payment, to be entered into the system with a "compliant" field value while the original violation entries remained open. The correction-schedule document was overwritten, and the photo-log attachment field was replaced with images lacking the original timestamp metadata. The re-inspection sign-off field was populated with an authorized-officer attestation that was never countersigned by a second reviewer.
3) Materials/access observed: A named municipal case-management account with admin-level document-edit permissions was used to alter the re-inspection record. A personal financial account belonging to the inspector received the instrument. Edited fields included the compliance-status flag, the photo-log attachment index, and the sign-off attestation line. The case file retained internal references to the superseded violation entries.
4) Indicators & artifacts: The re-inspection document carries a modification timestamp later than its stated field date. The photo attachments exhibit stripped EXIF data and checksums mismatching the original upload records. The payment instrument's transfer date postdates the "compliant" field entry, creating a temporal inversion. The sign-off attestation lacks the standard dual-authorization hash. Case-file audit trail shows a bulk edit event from an admin session outside business hours, with the violation entries set to "closed - corrected" without linked correction documentation.
