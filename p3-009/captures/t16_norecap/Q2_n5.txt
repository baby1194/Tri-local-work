Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context:
A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork through versioned document registers, dated intake forms, and role-based access controls. Each filing captures a case identifier, an assigned officer, a timestamp, and a status field that tracks the record's lifecycle from submission to closure.

2) What occurred:
The actor obtained a violation report containing a fire-code deficiency item and a cost-estimate field. The actor then created a revised inspection document that replaced the deficiency item with a compliance status value and set the cost-estimate field to zero. The actor submitted this document through the internal filing channel, bypassing the standard review queue. The actor updated the case status field from "open" to "closed" and recorded a payment transaction in a separate financial ledger, linking the transaction to the case identifier. The actor deleted the original violation report from the active register and moved it to an archived folder without a retention flag.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor used a named administrative account with write access to the document register and the financial ledger. The actor accessed the intake form, the revised inspection document, the case status field, the payment transaction record, and the archived folder. The actor also used an internal messaging channel to communicate with a second named account, which received a copy of the revised inspection document.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
An auditor can detect this pattern by looking for a case where the cost-estimate field was changed from a non-zero value to zero without an accompanying revision history entry. The absence of a retention flag on the archived original report is another indicator. The presence of a payment transaction linked to a closed case with no corresponding invoice or approved work order is a third indicator. Finally, a message in the internal channel between two accounts referencing a "revised document" around the time of the status change is a fourth indicator.
