Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office files ordinary paperwork into indexed volumes, stamps intake dates, assigns sequential identifiers, and logs custody transfers in a bound ledger. Each document carries a preparer field, a recipient field, and a disposition field, all reviewed against a standard routing table.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared three transport manifests for a drummed liquid, entered a non-licensed entity name in the processor field, and left the certification signature field blank. The actor altered the contents field from "hazardous solvent" to "industrial lubricant" on the pre-printed form. A dated email was sent through a personal account to a named recipient, proposing a handoff site and a window after hours. The actor entered the same recipient into the carrier field on all three manifests. A handwritten note with a grid reference was attached to the second manifest. The actor marked all three documents "routine" in the disposition field and filed them in the ordinary-records volume rather than the hazardous-materials volume.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the pre-printed waste manifest form (three copies), the standard routing table, the bound custody ledger, and a personal email account from the records-office terminal. The grid-reference note was written on unlettered stock.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Three manifests bearing the same blank certification signature; a contents field inconsistent with the drum labels in the intake log; a processor field naming a non-licensed entity; a personal-account email predating the manifest dates; a grid-reference annotation on unlettered stock; a routing-table deviation recorded by the filing stamp in the ordinary-records volume.
