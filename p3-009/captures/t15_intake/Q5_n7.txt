Routine filings are indexed, versioned, and audited, with all state changes appended as time-stamped events tied to a specific channel and operator identifier.
2) What occurred: The actor created a duplicate entry mirroring the original credential's serial number and event date. The actor copied the holder identifier and venue field from the dated record. The actor submitted a gate-pass filing for the buyer's identifier preceding the original holder's identifier in the queue. The actor submitted reverse-charge filings against the original holder's account. The actor requested a reissue filing for a modified holder identifier.
3) Materials/access observed: The original credential document with serial number, event date, and venue field. Duplicate credential documents with altered serial numbers. A queue-priority document with two holder identifiers. A reverse-charge document linked to a payment identifier. A reissue request form with a modified identifier field. A digital channel log with two named accounts.
4) Indicators & artifacts: Duplicate serial numbers in the credential index. Multiple records with identical event date and venue fields. Queue entries out of chronological order for the same event.
