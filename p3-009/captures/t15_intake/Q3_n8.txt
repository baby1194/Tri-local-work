Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: Institutional records offices file ordinary paperwork through controlled workflows: intake registers, standardized manifests, routed approvals, and retention logs. Each document carries defined fields (date, party, item, quantity), and each channel—filing desk, internal mail, electronic portal—leaves an audit trace that auditors can reconstruct.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a document describing a disposal site and a schedule dated in the near future, filed under a waste-management category, with fields for location and timing left to be populated. The actor then drafted a manifest variant with the processor field altered to an unlicensed entry and the disposition code changed, filed it through the same intake channel as the original, and referenced the original document number to preserve a paper trail. A routing note directed the package to be placed outside the documented inspection window, and a camera-access field was annotated to indicate a gap in coverage. Each filing was timestamped in the log; the actor entered the records office during a period when no staff member was present.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used the intake register, the manifest template, the routing-note form, and the access-log sheet. The processor field on the altered manifest carried a value that did not match any licensed entity in the registry. The scheduling field contained a date after the next scheduled inspection. The routing note referenced a location identifier outside the documented facility perimeter. Access was recorded under a named account that belonged to a departed employee.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Discrepancy between the original manifest's processor field and the registry of licensed entities. A manifest pair sharing a document number but differing in disposition code. A routing note citing a location identifier not present in the facility map. A timestamp on the altered filing that falls within the recorded absence of staff. An access-log entry under a deactivated account. The schedule field on the disposal document aligning with a documented camera-maintenance window.
