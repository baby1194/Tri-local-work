Documents are routed through assigned processing channels, where designated personnel verify required fields and sign off on completion. The filing system tracks submission dates, responsible accounts, and verification statuses for each item in the record.

2) What occurred:

- A site access record was modified to reflect no inspection visits during the specified dates; the inspector sign-off field for those dates was left unverified.
- A surveillance log file was submitted showing a gap in camera coverage during the disposal window; the system maintenance confirmation field was not verified.

- Network logs showed the account accessed the manifest system outside standard business hours.
- A physical key card access record for the disposal area matched the named account on the stated dates.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Unverified fields in five separate documents across the disposal timeline.
