Entries are keyed to unique identifiers, timestamped, and cross-referenced across ledgers. Ordinary paperwork is filed against these registers with standard metadata fields for status, date, and accountable party.

The document bundle was routed through the standard approval channel to the regulator-facing filing system.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor used a named maintenance account with write access to the maintenance ledger and component registers. A dated work order document, a dated procurement document, a dated certification record, and an aircraft status entry were involved. The approval channel was the internal records routing system. No additional credential artifacts were observed beyond the maintenance account.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A procurement document bearing a vendor reference not present in the approved supplier register.
