The actor attached a forged identity attestation to the buyer's entry document, leaving the biometric sign-off unverified.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Mismatch between the ticket's cryptographic hash and the platform's authenticity checksum; multiple documents sharing identical serial numbers; a private-channel transfer preceding official resale authorization; a buyer entry timestamp preceding the original holder's gate scan; a refund-suppression note absent a chargeback-reversal sign-off; and an identity attestation lacking biometric verification.
