Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office files ordinary paperwork as dated, versioned entries. Each document carries an identifier, a revision field, and an approval signature block. Entries are indexed by date and stored in a controlled log that supports sequential audit review.
2) What occurred: The actor altered a dated maintenance log field on a component record to show a service date. The actor edited the serial number field in the part history document to match a different component. The actor updated the approval signature field on a form to show a name and title. The actor submitted the documents through the internal file management system.
3) Materials/access observed: A named account with edit rights to the maintenance log system. The component record containing the serial number field. The part history document containing the installation date field. The approval form containing the signature block. The internal file management system submission channel.
4) Indicators & artifacts: The maintenance log field showing a service date that did not match the original installation record. The part history document showing a serial number that differed from the original component. The approval form showing a signature that was not from the originally designated authority. The file management system showing a submission timestamp that postdated the original record creation.
