Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains bound volumes of intake forms, cross-referenced index cards, and carbon-copy manifests. Clerks stamp dates, log custody transfers, and archive superseded drafts. Discrepancies between field values across copies are noted but rarely cross-checked against third-party logs.
2) What occurred: The actor prepared three duplicate waste manifests dated within a 48-hour window, altering the "hazard class" field from the correct code to a benign classification. The "processor signature" line was left blank in two copies and filled with a forged name in the third. The actor photographed the blank manifests, transmitted them via an unlisted personal account to a second actor, then shredded the original carbon copies before the scheduled 0900 pickup.
3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the records vault during the 2200-0600 maintenance window using a key card not issued to their department. A mobile device was observed within the vault, capturing images of the index card shelf. The "custody transfer" log for that night was missing page 14.
4) Indicators & artifacts: Divergence between the three manifest copies in the hazard class and weight fields. The forged signature does not match any staff record. The key card audit trail shows vault entry outside authorized hours. The personal account used for transmission has no affiliation with the organization's domain.
