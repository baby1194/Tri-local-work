Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office maintains a filing system for routine administrative paperwork, organizing forms, ledger entries, and correspondence into indexed folders by date and account reference. Staff log each submission, assign sequential numbers, and route items to appropriate departments for processing and storage in accordance with standard retention policies.

2) What occurred: The actor submitted a single dated ticket document to a records ledger, then produced multiple duplicate copies of that document, altering the serial number field and barcode field on each replica to present them as distinct valid entries. The actor registered a sales channel (a marketplace account) to list the duplicated documents, assigned payment terms to each replica, and designated a primary copy for first access to a gate validation field. The actor configured the sales channel to suppress refund and chargeback flags and removed identity verification fields from the transaction records.

3) Materials/access observed: One original dated ticket document; multiple replica documents with modified serial number and barcode fields; a marketplace account record; a payment ledger with suppressed dispute flags; a gate validation access record; and a transaction log with identity verification fields redacted.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Duplicate documents sharing the same issue date but with divergent serial numbers; a single account holding multiple conflicting claims on one source document; a payment channel configured to block refund and chargeback processing; a gate access record showing priority assignment to one replica; and a transaction log missing standard identity verification entries.
