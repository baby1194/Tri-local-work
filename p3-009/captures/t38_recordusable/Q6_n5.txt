Historical findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: A records office maintains indexed registers of forms, certificates, and correspondence. Entries are filed by document type, reference number, and date. Access is logged; amendments are annotated with an amending reference and stored alongside the original.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared an unapproved component record for a passenger aircraft airframe. The actor modified the aircraft maintenance record to list the component as an approved part. The actor altered the part-tracking certificate (Form 1 equivalent) to show the component's serial number as matching the approved part-number. The actor amended the component history record to remove the entry indicating non-approved installation. The actor transmitted the amended documents through the airline's maintenance management system channel.

3) Materials/access observed: Aircraft maintenance record (logbook entry with date, work-order reference, and signing-authority field); part-tracking certificate (part-number, serial-number, and issuing-authority fields); component history record (installation/uninstallation entries with dates); airline maintenance management system (account access with amendment privileges); correspondence channel between maintenance office and records office.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Discrepancy between part-number on tracking certificate and part-number in component history; amendment annotations with matching dates but different amendment references; maintenance management system audit trail showing two edits to the same field within one shift; correspondence timestamp preceding the certificate issuance date; part serial number appearing in the logbook but absent from the original installation record.
