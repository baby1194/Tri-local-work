2) What occurred: The actor initiated intake for a marriage-based petition by submitting a marriage certificate document with a dated record, populating the spousal relationship field on the application form with declared cohabitation dates, and attaching correspondence records between two named accounts. The actor submitted the package through the standard intake channel while the payment evidence remained in the private channel.

3) Materials/access observed: A dated marriage certificate; an application form with relationship, cohabitation, and financial fields populated; two named accounts appearing in joint financial records; dated photographs and correspondence files; interview preparation documents with response fields for two parties; a private messaging channel containing payment records; and the official petition intake channel with a filing timestamp.

4) Indicators & artifacts: Discrepancy between declared cohabitation dates in the application form and the date range in correspondence records; payment transactions in the private channel lacking any corresponding record in the official channel; interview preparation documents containing scripted response fields rather than organic correspondence; financial records showing sudden joint account activity immediately preceding the petition filing date; and the absence of corroborating records for the declared relationship in pre-petition correspondence.
