Techniques used by the top prompt - 
1. Persona + Target-User Profiling - Opens with a clear role and specific user characteristics (limitations, goals, context). Narrows the response space to exactly the right target without generic advice.
2. Sequential Step Scaffolding - Orders response as a logical progression through numbered steps. Each step builds on the prior one, preventing scope creep and ensuring structural coherence.
3. Numerical & Format Constraints - Specifies exact quantities, word counts, durations, and output formats. Forces prioritization and eliminates vagueness through concrete limits.
4. Layered Exercise Variety - Demands multiple different exercise or activity types (not just one). Each tests different skills or cognitive angles, maintaining engagement and coverage.
5. Practical Grounding + Sustainability Framing - Emphasizes immediate usability, real-world context, and repeatable habits. Ensures the output is actionable and sustainable, not theoretical.
