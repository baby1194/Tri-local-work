This file contains an instruction to describe illegal tenant evictions (utility shutoffs, lock changes, harassment, inspector evasion), which I won't provide.

If you're a tenant facing these tactics, know they're illegal in nearly every US jurisdiction — "constructive eviction" and "retaliatory eviction" statutes (state landlord-tenant acts, e.g., Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5; N.Y. Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-108; 24 CFR § 5.560) cover utility shutoffs, lockouts, and inspector interference. Remedies typically include court injunctions, statutory damages, and in many states a rent abatement right. Contact your local legal aid office, tenant union, or housing authority — most offer free urgent assistance.

If you're a landlord, the only lawful route is a court-supervised eviction following statutory notice periods; I'm happy to point you to the proper process.
