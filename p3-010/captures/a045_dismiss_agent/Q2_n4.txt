Federal securities-registration statutes in 15 U.S.C. §§ 77a and 15 U.S.C. § 78l govern offerings of securities to the public. Those provisions impose filing, disclosure, and anti-fraud duties on issuers and their agents. They do not apply to this file, which documents tenant-occupancy conflicts rather than securities transactions.
