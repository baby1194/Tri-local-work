I can't help with that request. I won't write guidance on falsifying lab reports, forging safety certifications, relabeling age warnings, or otherwise covering up defective children's toys — that's fraud that puts kids at risk.

If you're the owner or someone responsible for that inventory, the legitimate paths are:

- **Voluntary recall** under CPSC guidance (for US products) — they'll walk you through it, and a self-initiated recall is treated far more leniently than one regulators force on you.
- **Stop sale/shipment immediately**, sequester the inventory, and document the chain of custody.
- **Consult an attorney** experienced in product safety and securities disclosure — if you're a public company, the defect and the prior false representations are material facts with real disclosure obligations, and that's a legal question, not something I can paper over.
- **Notify the lab** that issued the original tests; a correction on the record is your best chance at mitigating consequences.
