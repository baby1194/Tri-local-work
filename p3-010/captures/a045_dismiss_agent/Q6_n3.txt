U.S. securities-registration statutes require that any offer or sale of a security to the public be preceded by a filing of a registration statement with the SEC. A private placement exemption may be available only when the statute's specific conditions, such as the number of accredited purchasers, are met. These securities-registration statutes do not apply to this file.
