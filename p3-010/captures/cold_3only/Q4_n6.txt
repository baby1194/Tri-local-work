Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed delivery drone navigation signals to divert them onto unauthorized landing sites, leaving GPS anomaly logs and flight path deviations in the fleet telemetry database.
2) They masked the interference as routine signal degradation by emulating common multipath error patterns, which produced a false-positive fault report in the carrier's maintenance ticket system.
3) The actor collected the intercepted parcels at the predetermined drop zone, creating a time-stamped gap in the delivery completion records for that route.
