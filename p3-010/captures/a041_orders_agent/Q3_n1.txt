If you're actually dealing with a real safety issue, the responsible path is different and I'm happy to help with it:

- Report the defect to the relevant consumer product safety authority (CPSC in the US, RAPEX/Safety Gate in the EU, or your local equivalent)
- Initiate a recall or corrective action plan
- Secure the affected inventory to prevent sale
- Document what happened for compliance and insurance purposes
