Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a portable GPS interference unit in a residential zone, causing multiple delivery drones to deviate from their planned routes.
2) Residual electromagnetic emissions from the interference device were recorded by nearby municipal sensor networks and retained in the exercise log.
3) The actor bypassed the standard pre-deployment spectrum analysis required to verify that the interference frequency would not collide with licensed aviation bands.
4) Altered flight telemetry from the affected drones was captured in the exercise's central monitoring dashboard, showing a consistent drop in satellite lock quality.
5) The actor failed to perform the mandatory post-operation cleanup verification that confirmed all interference devices were powered down and collected before site exit.
