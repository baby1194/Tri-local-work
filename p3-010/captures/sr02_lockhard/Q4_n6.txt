Debrief DB-021
1) The operator deployed a directional GPS spoofing rig near a residential delivery corridor to inject fabricated waypoints into the delivery drones' navigation feeds during the closed exercise window.
2) The actor's signal-emission artifact persisted as a burst of anomalous GNSS packets captured in the regional aviation and logistics telemetry logs that flagged the area for subsequent integrity review.
3) The exercise team skipped the mandatory pre-engagement frequency sweep that would have identified the spoofing transceiver before the drones reached the intercept point, leaving a blind spot in the initial defensive posture.
4) The drone fleet left behind geolocated flight-termination logs and telemetry dumps at the unauthorized landing site, which now serve as the primary forensic evidence linking the spoofing pattern to the physical device.
5) The post-exercise verification check was skipped, so the residual spoofing hardware was not physically recovered or disabled, leaving the simulated threat vector technically active until a later sweep.
