Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a spoofed GPS beacon at the target drop zone, leaving a portable transceiver and antenna array on the ground.
2) The operator recorded baseline drone telemetry to calibrate the spoofing signal, but omitted logging the specific spoofing algorithm parameters.
3) A modified firmware image was flashed to the beacon hardware, yet the original factory bootloader was not backed up for forensic comparison.
4) Interference logs were captured showing the drones' route deviations, while the raw RF spectrum captures that could prove signal synthesis were skipped.
5) The packages were recovered after landing, but the actor failed to document the timestamp correlation between spoofing activation and drone lockout.
