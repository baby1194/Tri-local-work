Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GNSS signals near the delivery corridor, causing the drones to leave their planned paths and hover at a preselected spot.
2) A replayed positioning file and a short transmission script were left on the exercise host's removable media.
3) The actor skipped the verification step confirming the spoofed fix was within the licensed test window and approved coordinates.
4) The drones logged anomalous jumps to the drop site, and those anomaly reports were archived in the exercise's after-action folder.
5) The operator did not run the post-exercise sweep to confirm no rogue transmission remained active before releasing the area.
