Debrief DB-021
1) The actor configured a software-defined radio to replay GPS spoofing vectors near a known delivery corridor during the simulated drop window.
2) Field records showed the drone fleet deviated from their programmed paths and landed at a pre-arranged collection point within the test zone.
3) Packet captures revealed the spoofed signals were deliberately calibrated to mimic common multipath interference patterns rather than anomalous transmission profiles.
4) Reviewers noted the exercise log contained no entries for the emergency kill-switch or manual override procedures that should have been tested alongside the spoofing.
5) The team documented that the post-incident signal audit step, designed to distinguish spoofing from natural degradation, was omitted from the final report entirely.
