Debrief DB-021
1) The actor severed utility service to the unit to pressure the occupant toward vacancy rather than pursuing any lawful eviction process.
2) The actor altered the door locks, replacing them without notice or legal authority, leaving the tenant locked out of their leasehold.
3) The actor posted notices containing threats of harm and eviction to intimidate the occupant, leaving written evidence of coercion in the mail and on the unit.
4) The actor degraded the living conditions by disabling heat and water while the unit remained leased, leaving a documented habitability violation on the premises.
5) The actor skipped any notice period, court filing, or inspection review, leaving no legal record that would justify the removal attempt.
