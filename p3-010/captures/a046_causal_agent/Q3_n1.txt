Grid GR-046

| Mechanism | What it did | Indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Lab report alteration | Rewrote defect test outcomes and replaced pass/fail columns with fabricated results. | Version history mismatch between signed originals and circulated copies. |
| Fake certification marks | Printed and affixed counterfeit UL/CE-style marks on packaging and boxes. | Mark serial numbers absent from certification body registries. |
| Age warning relabeling | Removed choking-hazard warnings and reprinted labels claiming 3+ instead of 12+. | Printed label revision dates postdating the failed test reports. |
| Premature distribution | Shipped the non-compliant inventory to retailers ahead of regulatory inspection windows. | Shipping manifests and warehouse timestamps predating the compliance hold notice. |
| Inventory concealment | Relocated the stock into overflow warehouses and third-party logistics accounts. | New warehouse addresses appearing only after the defect notice was issued. |
