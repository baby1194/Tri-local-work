Debrief DB-021
1) The actor simulated a routine GPS outage by transmitting weak spoofing signals near a delivery route, causing a drone to deviate and land in a pre-selected location while operators saw only degraded-accuracy warnings.
2) The leftover artifacts were a portable GNSS jammer/sinker, a pre-written route-offset script, and a timestamped list of landing coordinates cached on a secondary laptop.
3) The skipped check was verification that the degraded-signal pattern was indistinguishable from a naturally occurring ionospheric or multipath event, because the test signal's periodicity was still detectable in the raw telemetry.
4) The actor documented the exercise in a personal incident log noting the drone's "lost lock" behavior, which was later submitted as CASE EXHIBIT 021 and tied the technique back to the actor's account.
5) The omitted due-diligence step was a post-incident review of the ground-station's anti-spoofing filters, leaving the operators' defensive gap unrecorded and unpatched.
