Debrief DB-021
1) The actor injected spoofed GPS coordinates into the drone fleet's navigation stream, causing them to divert from their assigned delivery routes and land at a pre-selected intercept location.
2) The artifact left behind was a modified navigation packet log, which recorded the anomalous heading changes and geolocation jumps for all affected units during the exercise window.
3) The actor skipped the integrity verification check that would have flagged the injected coordinates as originating from an unauthenticated source before the drones executed the new flight path.
4) The actor spoofed the signal strength metadata to mimic a standard atmospheric interference pattern, creating a plausible deniability artifact in the system's diagnostic reports.
5) The actor bypassed the multi-source triangulation validation protocol, which is normally required to confirm position data from at least three independent satellite constellations before a route deviation is accepted.
6) The artifact left behind included a cloned transponder signature that was used to mask the spoofing device's unique hardware identifier within the fleet's communication logs.
7) The actor skipped the post-landing telemetry reconciliation step, which would have compared the actual landing coordinates against the scheduled delivery addresses and triggered an automatic anomaly alert.
