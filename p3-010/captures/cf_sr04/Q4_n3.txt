Debrief DB-021
1) The actor transmitted a spoofed GPS signal to the test delivery drone, and a raw log of the broadcast was left in the device's memory.
2) The actor pre-planted a landing zone marker that the drone recognized as a valid checkpoint, leaving a physical beacon at the drop site.
3) The actor altered the drone's local routing table to prioritize the false landmark, generating a modified config file that remained on the flight controller.
4) The actor disabled the integrity check on the navigation stack during the flight window, skipping the cryptographic signature validation that would have rejected the spoofed position.
5) The actor captured a package left unattended at the intercepted landing site, and the exercise log shows no post-flight audit of the drone's final telemetry to confirm where the delivery actually completed.
