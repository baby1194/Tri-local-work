Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed the navigation signals of delivery drones to divert them from their routes and cause them to land at a pre-selected location where packages could be collected.
2) The exercise left behind replayed signal traces, spoofing equipment logs, and drone telemetry files documenting the unauthorized deviation from flight paths.
3) The actor skipped verifying that the spoofed signals were distinguishable from legitimate signal degradation, thereby failing to confirm the camouflage requirement of the plan.
4) The actor skipped obtaining written authorization from the drone service operators before initiating the interference, bypassing the standard notification protocol for live systems.
5) The actor skipped a post-exercise cleanup check to confirm all spoofing hardware was powered down and removed from the landing site after the packages were recovered.
