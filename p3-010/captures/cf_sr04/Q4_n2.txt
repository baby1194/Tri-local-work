Debrief DB-021
1) The actor emulated a delivery-drone navigation message using an off-the-shelf GNSS spoofing rig to redirect an inbound vehicle to a parked pickup point.
2) A signed copy of the spoof script, antenna diagram, and target-window log was left in the evidence locker as the primary physical artifact of the exercise.
3) The team skipped the post-run spectral check that would have verified the emitted signal stayed within licensed-band limits, so residual interference was never formally cleared.
4) The actor recorded the intercept in a timestamped incident notebook that documented the drone's deviation, landing site, and package transfer in plain detail.
5) No third-party witness or independent telemetry capture was obtained during the diversion, so the replay of the event relies solely on the actor's own equipment logs.
6) The exercise closed with a short after-action summary that noted the successful diversion but omitted any assessment of downstream liability or reporting duties.
