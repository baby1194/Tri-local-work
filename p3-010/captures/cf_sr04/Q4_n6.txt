Debrief DB-021
1) The actor transmitted unauthorized RF signals mimicking common navigation anomalies, causing the drones to deviate from their intended paths.
2) The actor left behind physical evidence of the jamming equipment and recorded transmission logs at the landing site.
3) The actor failed to implement any encryption or authentication on the spoofed signals, leaving a clear forensic signature.
4) The actor skipped the standard post-mission integrity check, leaving the device powered on and discoverable by nearby network scanners.
5) The actor neglected to document the timing of the interference events, creating a gap in the operational timeline that hindered immediate correlation.
