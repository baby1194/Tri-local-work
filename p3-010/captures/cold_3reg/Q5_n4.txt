Debrief DB-021
1) The actor recruited individuals to form a collusive reviewing group that exchanged favorable assessments for one another's submissions, leaving a trail of coordinated review comments across related manuscripts.
2) The actor coordinated with the recruited reviewers to produce negative evaluations of a rival's submission, generating a cluster of overlapping critiques on the same work within an unusually short timeframe.
3) The actor concealed financial and authorship ties between themselves and the participating reviewers, producing an editorial record in which the involved parties were presented as unrelated despite shared affiliations.
