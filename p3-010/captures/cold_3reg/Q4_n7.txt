Debrief DB-021
1) The actor executed an unauthorized drone navigation disruption to divert shipments to a pre-selected pickup site, which left anomalous flight-path deviations in the delivery network's telemetry logs.
2) The actor deliberately obfuscated the interference to mimic routine signal degradation, which created a misleading baseline in the carriers' incident ticketing records.
3) The actor physically collected the redirected packages at the target location, which resulted in unexplained delivery failures and customer escalation entries in the service logs.
