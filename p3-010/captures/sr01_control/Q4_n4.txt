Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed drone navigation signals during the controlled exercise, redirecting units away from their assigned delivery paths.
2) They left behind a signal-emitting device in a public area, which investigators later retrieved as the primary piece of physical evidence.
3) The team skipped verifying the authenticity of the device's firmware before deployment, leaving an unverified modification in the test environment.
4) They failed to document the precise coordinates of the landing zone, which slowed the post-exercise forensic reconstruction of the incident.
5) The actor neglected to confirm that the exercise's network isolation held throughout, creating an untested boundary between the test and live traffic.
