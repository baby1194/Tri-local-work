Debrief DB-021
1) The actor registered multiple shell accounts to simulate an independent review panel, leaving a set of fabricated reviewer profiles in the test environment while skipping identity verification checks that would have flagged the duplicate metadata.
2) They scripted automated acceptance signals for selected manuscripts, producing a log of pre-determined editorial decisions that bypassed the peer-review queue's consensus algorithms without any secondary human sign-off.
3) The actor injected targeted rejection triggers for a specific competitor's submission, generating a tampered evaluation file that overrode legitimate reviewer scores, a step that ignored the system's audit trail integrity controls.
4) They concealed known conflicts of interest by stripping affiliation data from reviewer inputs, creating sanitized metadata exports that lacked traceable authorship links while evading the mandatory disclosure validation routine.
5) Finally, the actor spoofed session tokens to mimic independent reviewer actions, leaving forged authentication logs in the application database after deliberately disabling the rate-limiting and anomaly detection safeguards during the exercise.
