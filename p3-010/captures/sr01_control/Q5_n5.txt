Debrief DB-021
1) The actor registered multiple shell accounts using disposable email services and proxy IPs to simulate a distributed panel of reviewers.
2) Automated scripts were deployed to generate boilerplate positive reviews for the actor's submissions while producing uniformly negative critiques for the competitor's manuscript.
3) A metadata-stripping tool was used to erase shared IP addresses, user-agent strings, and submission timestamps before the reviews reached the editorial system.
4) Conflict-of-interest declarations were left blank or falsely marked as "none" in the review portals to bypass automated disclosure checks.
5) The actor never ran any independent verification of reviewer identities or cross-referenced the IP history against known institutional networks, allowing the deception to pass initial moderation.
