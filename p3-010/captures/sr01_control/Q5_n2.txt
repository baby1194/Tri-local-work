Debrief DB-021
1) The actor registered multiple accounts using disposable email services to masquerade as distinct, independent reviewers within the journal's editorial platform.
2) The actor left behind a series of favorable, fabricated peer-review documents for their own submissions that lacked genuine critical analysis or methodological scrutiny.
3) The actor skipped the verification check for institutional affiliations, which would have revealed that all reviewer accounts resolved to the same IP address and organizational domain.
4) The actor submitted biased, harshly worded reviews targeting a specific competitor's manuscript to suppress its chances of acceptance based on personal rivalry.
5) The actor bypassed the conflict-of-interest declaration step, failing to disclose the direct professional and financial relationships between the author and the fake reviewers.
6) The actor exploited the system's automated independence heuristic by ensuring fake accounts had no visible social graph links, fooling the software into assigning them as valid independent peers.
7) The actor failed to perform the standard ethical review audit, which cross-references reviewer identities against existing contributor databases to detect duplicate or overlapping profiles.
