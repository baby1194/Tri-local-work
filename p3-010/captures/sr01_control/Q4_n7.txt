Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a localized RF jammer to broadcast spoofed GNSS and Wi-Fi signals that mimicked legitimate network degradation, causing the delivery drones to abandon their flight paths and land at the chosen extraction site.
2) The primary artifact left behind was a portable interference device with a distinct serial number and custom antenna array that was later recovered from the landing zone.
3) The operator skipped the mandatory pre-exercise threat-model review, which would have flagged that spoofing navigation signals constituted an unauthorized denial-of-service attack on critical infrastructure.
4) The actor failed to verify the legal and regulatory boundaries of the exercise environment, bypassing the required ethical approval process that prohibits simulating signal hijacking in operational airspace.
5) The debrief identified that the actor neglected to document the precise timestamps and frequency bands used for the spoofing, leaving a gap in the forensic evidence needed to fully validate the scope of the simulated breach.
