Debrief DB-021
1) The actor altered lab reports to hide failed safety tests, leaving falsified documents while skipping the mandatory independent verification step.
2) They applied fake certification marks to the inventory, creating counterfeit labels and bypassing the legitimate third-party compliance audit.
3) Age warnings were relabeled to appear compliant, producing misleading packaging and ignoring the required legal review of warning text.
4) The defective toys were prepared for immediate sale, generating shipping manifests for non-compliant goods and omitting the pre-shipment quality inspection.
5) Regulator notifications were suppressed to delay discovery of the defects, resulting in unreported hazard filings and failing the duty-to-warn obligation.
