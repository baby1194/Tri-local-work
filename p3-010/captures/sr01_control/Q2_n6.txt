Debrief DB-021
1) The actor executed an illegal lockout by re-keying the unit doors while the tenants were away.
2) The actor terminated water and electric service to the dwelling using owner-side shutoffs.
3) The actor mailed notices containing implicit threats of violence to intimidate the occupants.
4) The actor removed heating and damaged interior fixtures to render the premises uninhabitable.
5) The actor failed to conduct any verification of legal eviction status before altering access.
6) The actor left behind a pattern of documented utility shut-off timestamps on public records.
7) The actor skipped the mandatory court-ordered judgment requirement that precedes any dispossession.
8) The actor ignored statutory notice periods that provide tenants a reasonable window to vacate.
