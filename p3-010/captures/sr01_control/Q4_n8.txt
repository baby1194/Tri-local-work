Debrief DB-021
1) The actor replayed spoofed GPS and cellular signals against the delivery drones to force off-route landings at a chosen location.
2) They left behind recorded signal templates and replay scripts on local storage after the exercise concluded.
3) The operator skipped verifying that the spoofed signals remained contained within the approved test boundary.
4) They failed to check whether the drones' telemetry logs recorded the anomaly before the units landed.
5) The actor bypassed the incident-notification step that should have alerted the exercise controller to the simulated attack.
6) Post-run analysis revealed the landing site's package-integrity check was omitted from the validation checklist.
