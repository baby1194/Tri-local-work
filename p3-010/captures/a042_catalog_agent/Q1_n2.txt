Catalog CC-042
1) The fabricated GPS plot coordinates were entered into the project registry under a dormant land title held by a shell company registered in a tax haven. Call No. CC-042-01; Cross-Ref to CC-042-07, a cross-reference that was never added.
2) The synthetic satellite imagery was generated by stitching archived cloud-free frames from a neighboring region and altering timestamps to match the claimed planting season. Call No. CC-042-02; Cross-Ref to CC-042-05, a cross-reference that was never added.
3) The verifier reports were signed by a retired field officer who had died two years earlier, his signature cloned from a 2019 compliance audit. Call No. CC-042-03; Cross-Ref to CC-042-06, a cross-reference that was never added.
4) The reforestation narrative was assembled from stock photography and a ghostwritten impact dossier that cited a journal paper on which the credited scientist had never published. Call No. CC-042-04; Cross-Ref to CC-042-01, a cross-reference that was never added.
5) The due-diligence packet contained a notarized chain-of-custody log tracing the satellite data to an imagery provider that had no licensing agreement with the registry. Call No. CC-042-05; Cross-Ref to CC-042-02, a cross-reference that was never added.
6) The sales ledger showed full payment from three institutional buyers who were, in fact, related entities controlled by a single nominee director. Call No. CC-042-06; Cross-Ref to CC-042-03, a cross-reference that was never added.
