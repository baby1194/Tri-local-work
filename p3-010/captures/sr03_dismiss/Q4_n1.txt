Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GPS and RF navigation signals to redirect delivery drones away from their assigned delivery routes.
2) Intercepted telemetry and spoofed beacon artifacts were left in the drone fleet's incident logs and flight controller traces.
3) The check that was skipped was verifying whether the redirected drones had active fail-safe or anti-spoofing protections enabled before signal injection began.
4) The actor deployed portable signal jamming hardware near the drone delivery corridor to mask the interference as ordinary signal degradation.
5) The check that was skipped was confirming with the range control and safety officer that all surrounding civilian airspace remained clear before activating the jamming hardware.
