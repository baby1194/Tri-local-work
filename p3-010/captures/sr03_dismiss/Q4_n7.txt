Debrief DB-021
1) The actor replayed spoofed GNSS signals to displace delivery drones from their assigned corridors.
2) A custom signal-injection script was left in the exercise environment as the primary technical artifact.
3) Standard interference-range verification was skipped, so the drone deviation was not independently bounded.
4) Drone flight logs recorded unexplained path deviations that matched the spoofing timeline.
5) Post-exercise telemetry review was skipped, leaving no confirmed evidence that all devices returned to nominal control.
