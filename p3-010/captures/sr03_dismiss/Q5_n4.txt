Debrief DB-021
1) The actor registered multiple pseudonymous reviewer accounts to form a covert panel that accepted target manuscripts, leaving fabricated profile records in the editorial database while bypassing the duplicate-IP and identity verification checks.
2) Coordinated rejection scripts were executed against a competitor's submissions, depositing templated negative review files into the decision queue while skipping the peer-review integrity and collusion-detection controls.
3) Conflict-of-interest disclosures were deliberately omitted from the reviewer assignment flow, creating an audit gap where the COI attestation field remained empty and the automated disclosure-verification step was never triggered.
4) The review workflow was manipulated to randomize reviewer metadata, generating log entries that made the panel appear independent, while evading the editorial system's related-party linkage analysis.
5) Supporting evidence was staged in the submission tracker, including timestamped fake review completions and acceptance recommendations, all produced without the mandatory secondary-reviewer sign-off or ethics-board oversight.
