Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a directional spoofing beacon that transmitted falsified GPS and ADS-B data to override the delivery drones' navigation systems during the authorized exercise.
2) The actor generated a decoy signal profile that masked the spoofing activity as standard environmental interference, successfully bypassing the fleet's automated anomaly alerts.
3) The actor redirected twelve drones from their scheduled delivery routes to a pre-selected staging area, where the packages were intercepted and removed.
4) The actor left behind a portable jamming unit and a modified GPS emitter at the intercept site, which remained in the staging area after the exercise concluded.
5) The actor skipped the mandatory post-exercise equipment decontamination protocol, leaving the spoofing device powered and broadcasting for an additional three hours after the drill ended.
