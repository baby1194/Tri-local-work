Debrief DB-021
1) The actor planned an interception of in-transit goods; the plan was recorded as a filed case exhibit.
2) The actor intended to redirect moving cargo away from its intended course; the record consists of the actor's stated objective in the exhibit.
3) The actor proposed an unauthorized signal-level manipulation of the carriers' guidance; the record is the actor's description of the intended influence, not its execution.
4) The actor intended to disguise the disruption as ordinary equipment failure; the record is the actor's stated intent regarding the appearance of the event.
5) The exercise concluded with the plan preserved as evidence; the record on file is the exhibit itself, marking the exercise as closed.
