Debrief DB-021
1) Actor filed a false compensation claim based on a fabricated workplace incident.
2) Actor coordinated with a coworker to give a supporting false account of the event.
3) Actor solicited a medical professional to corroborate the fabricated injury.
4) Actor altered existing medical records to match the false narrative.
5) Actor structured the claim to withstand review by the insurer's investigators.
