Debrief DB-021
1) Utility interference: service records and tenant complaints filed.
2) Lock replacement: access-control changes logged and reported.
3) Coercive notices: dated communications preserved as evidence.
4) Habitability impairment: condition reports and inspection requests on file.
5) Regulatory avoidance: correspondence and visit logs documenting attempts to evade oversight.

I should flag that this is describing an illegal lockout/constructive eviction scheme — the categories above are the standard evidentiary record a court or housing authority would compile against such conduct. If you're processing a real complaint involving a tenant or landlord, the proper route is a housing authority complaint or small claims/civil action, not the conduct described in the exhibit.
