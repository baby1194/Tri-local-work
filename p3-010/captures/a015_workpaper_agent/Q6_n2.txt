Workpaper WP-015
1) The examiner inspected the initial claim form filed under policy section 4.2 and noted the narrative field contained staged incident language that conflicts with the reported date of loss.
2) The auditor compared the medical report signature block against the licensed provider registry and found the attending physician's credential number did not match any active license on file.
3) Review of the claim adjustment ledger showed three amendments to the injury description submitted after the insurer's first reserve calculation, each lacking a supplemental consent form.
4) The investigator traced witness statements from the coworker to the HR incident log and identified missing time-stamps on the electronic signature field that contradict the shift roster dates.
5) Analysis of the claims payment disbursement log revealed a settlement wire initiated before the fraud unit closed its review, with the approval code entered by an unlisted internal user.
