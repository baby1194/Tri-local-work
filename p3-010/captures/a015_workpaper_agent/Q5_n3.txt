Workpaper WP-015
1) Reviewed the manuscript submission log for reviewer assignments corresponding to the subject papers; the assigned reviewer profiles displayed matching institutional domains with the corresponding authors' affiliations; the conflict-of-interest attestation field remained unverified, and the underlying identity records for those reviewer accounts were not located in the personnel system.

2) Inspected the editorial platform audit trail for peer-review actions recorded against the subject papers; the review comments and acceptance recommendations were timestamped within the same hour from multiple accounts sharing a single device fingerprint; the independent-reviewer integrity check was bypassed, and no remediation entry exists in the journal's exception register.

3) Traced the reviewer invitation emails for the subject editorial cycle; the invitation recipients included pre-existing email addresses that had previously been associated with the submitting authors' institutional mail server; the reviewer acknowledgment records showed no evidence of separate individual accounts, and the editorial office's reviewer-identity verification worksheet is absent from the workpaper file.

4) Examined the decision letters issued against the competitor's submitted manuscript; the rejection rationale cited reviewer scores that reference reviewer accounts subsequently flagged as controlled by the submitting party; the re-review request was denied without documented justification, and the competitor's appeal has not been logged in the grievance register.

5) Performed data analytics on the reviewer pool metadata for the journal; the reviewer database contained duplicate profiles with identical contact fields and overlapping review histories across the subject papers; the deduplication control failed, and the affected profiles have not been deactivated or segregated in the current reporting period.

6) Read the editorial policy acknowledgments signed by the handling editor; the acknowledgments attest to independence of reviewers while the submission records show coordinated reviewer selection by the submitting author; the attestation conflicts with the system evidence, and no follow-up inquiry has been recorded in the audit file.

7) Compared the metadata of accepted manuscripts to the standard peer-review workflow; the files show direct uploads of review PDFs by the submitting author's IP address rather than by external reviewer accounts; the workflow control was overridden, and the override authorization is missing from the system access logs.
