Workpaper WP-015
1) The reviewer registry export (REV_REGISTRY_2024Q4.csv, fields: reviewer_id, institution, orcid, email) was re-performed for the target manuscript batch, which showed three reviewer accounts sharing one institutional mail domain, leaving unexplained the absence of independent ORCID verification evidence in the audit trail (field: orcid_verified).
2) The editorial decision log (EDITORIAL_DECISIONS.jsonl, fields: manuscript_id, decision, reviewer_scores, timestamp) was inspected for the competitor submission MS-1147, which recorded a "reject" decision preceded by score amendments at the same timestamp as a reviewer account suspension, leaving open the lack of a documented post-decision integrity check on score mutations (field: score_change_justification, blank).
3) The conflict-of-interest attestation forms (COI_ATTESTATION_<reviewer_id>.pdf) were retrieved for all reviewers assigned to the accepted manuscript set, which returned forms bearing identical submission timestamps and a missing attestation for reviewer_id R-0342, leaving unresolved the evidentiary gap in the attestation completeness control (field: attestation_status = null).
4) The independence confirmation matrix (INDEPENDENCE_MATRIX.xlsx, sheet "Review-Assignment") was traced to reviewer acceptance emails in the mailbox archive, which found no acceptance correspondence for two of the five listed assignments, leaving uncorroborated the system flag recording those reviews as completed (field: independence_confirmed = yes).
5) The review-submission metadata (REVIEW_SUBMISSIONS.csv, fields: reviewer_id, submission_ip, reviewer_account_id, device_fingerprint) was compared across the accepted set, which identified two distinct review documents carrying the same device_fingerprint, leaving open the unexplained bypass of the multi-account detection control (field: duplicate_fingerprint_flag = false).
