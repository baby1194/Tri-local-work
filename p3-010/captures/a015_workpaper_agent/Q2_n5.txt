Workpaper WP-015
1) The auditor retrieved the lease register for the subject building and examined the termination and eviction columns for closed entries; the register displayed "N/A" in the legal-action field for all terminated tenants; the exception that no lawful termination mechanism was documented remains open.

2) The auditor inspected the utility account ledgers and meter-disconnection logs for the affected units; the logs recorded utility disconnection dates that precede the final lease-payment date in the rent ledger; the exception that disconnections occurred during active tenancies is still open.

3) The auditor reviewed the building key-control records and lock-cylinder replacement work orders; the work orders show cylinder changes executed on occupied units without corresponding tenant-consent forms or court-authorized entry documents in the file; the exception of unauthorized lock changes stands unresolved.

4) The auditor traced tenant correspondence files to identify service and content of each notice; the notices present in the file contained ultimatums and property-damage threats, and the envelope log shows service by posting rather than certified delivery; the exception that the notices were coercive and not validly served remains open.

5) The auditor compared the unit condition-inspection reports and maintenance work orders for the affected apartments; the condition reports list water shutdown, heat removal, and blocked access recorded during the tenancy period, with no remediation entries after the date of the inspection; the exception that conditions were rendered uninhabitable persists.

6) The auditor examined the compliance and regulator-correspondence file for the property; the file contains no inspector visit reports, no response letters to housing-authority inquiries, and a maintenance log entry indicating that records were reorganized the day after a state inspection notice was issued; the exception that regulatory oversight appears to have been evaded remains open.

7) The auditor corroborated tenant statements against the payment-application worksheet and the deposit account ledger; the worksheet shows withheld rent credited against unauthorized charges, and the deposit ledger shows funds released to the owner after move-out; the exception that tenants were financially penalized remains open.
