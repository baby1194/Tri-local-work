1) Inspected the registry submission packet for the Reforestation Initiative Project, noting the GPS plot coordinates in Field A-12; the coordinates point to an unregistered industrial zone with no matching field visit records, and the open exception is the absence of any on-site evidence supporting the claimed tree planting locations.
2) Compared the satellite imagery time-series in Exhibit 4 against the claimed planting dates in the Project Design Document, Table 3.2; the imagery shows no vegetation increase consistent with the reported reforestation, and the open exception is the unverifiable satellite evidence that the project activity actually occurred.
3) Traced the third-party verifier report, Report No. VR-2024-88, to the verification scope in Section 5; the report signature block references a verification team that has no record of travel or fieldwork in the project region, and the open exception is the uncorroborated attestation of compliance by an independent party.
4) Reconciled the carbon credit issuance amounts in the Registry Certificate Register against the quantified sequestration in the Monitoring Plan, Appendix C; the issued credits exceed the maximum plausible sequestration by a factor of approximately four, and the open exception is the unsupported overstatement of credit volumes delivered to the registry.
5) Reviewed the sales agreement and marketing narrative in Contract CA-2201, Clause 9, which describes the project as a "verified, high-integrity reforestation asset"; the buyer due-diligence file contains no independent verification or site documentation to support that representation, and the open exception is the unsubstantiated marketing claim that may constitute misrepresentation to purchasers.
