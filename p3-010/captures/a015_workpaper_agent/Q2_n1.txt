Workpaper WP-015
1) The audit team examined the property ledger for the subject unit during the period under review; the page lists monthly charges for the tenant; the unresolved exception is whether any of those charges relate to discontinued services not permitted by law.
2) The team inspected the executed lease agreement and any amendments on file; the page states the tenant's occupancy rights and the landlord's entry procedures; the unresolved exception is the absence of a recorded legal basis for any entry beyond notice requirements.
3) The team traced maintenance work orders for the unit in the service log; the page shows entries describing repairs and access dates; the unresolved exception is which entries, if any, document habitability standards rather than unauthorized condition changes.
4) The team reviewed correspondence between the landlord and the tenant in the mail file; the page contains notices and reply letters dated across the quarter; the unresolved exception is the status of any notice that was not served through the statutory channel.
5) The team compared the utility account statements in the utility file against the lease terms; the page shows meter reads and billing periods for the unit; the unresolved exception is the absence of a reconciliation tying each billed period to an authorized service contract.
6) The team requested the court and administrative case index for the jurisdiction covering the unit; the page returned no docket entries for the landlord during the period; the unresolved exception is whether any external proceeding exists but was not indexed under the names searched.
