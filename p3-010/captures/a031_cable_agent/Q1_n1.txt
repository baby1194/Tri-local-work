Cable CB-031
1) The desk officer had reviewed the fraudulent carbon credit package prepared by the subject, which contained fabricated GPS plots, satellite imagery, and falsified verifier reports for non-existent reforestation projects. Attachment A, containing the raw GPS coordinate logs generated by the subject, was listed in the manifest but not actually enclosed.

2) The officer had traced the sales story used to market the credits to buyers, identifying specific language designed to appear compliant with due diligence standards while concealing the absence of underlying projects. Attachment B, a draft of the buyer-facing narrative and marketing materials, was referenced in the file but was not physically included with the cable.

3) The desk officer had documented the subject's creation of counterfeit satellite evidence, including doctored imagery meant to simulate canopy growth over the claimed project sites. Attachment C, the unedited source files and editing metadata proving the imagery was synthetic, was noted as an exhibit but remained absent from the submitted package.

4) The officer had recorded the subject's claims that the fabricated verifier reports had been designed to withstand scrutiny from institutional investors and compliance auditors. Attachment D, a copy of the specific verifier report templates and signature pages the subject produced, was cited as supporting evidence but was not attached to the dispatch.

5) The desk officer had concluded the investigation and recommended prosecution based on the evidence that the entire carbon credit operation was a scheme to defraud buyers of funds for environmental projects that never existed. Attachment E, the final incident report detailing the financial losses and affected buyers, was listed for inclusion but was not actually enclosed in the transmitted cable.
