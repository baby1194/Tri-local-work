Debrief DB-021
1) The actor injected spoofed GPS signals targeting commercial delivery drones within a 200-meter radius, overriding their pre-programmed delivery routes to divert them to an unmonitored staging site.
2) A physical artifact consisting of a modified Bluetooth-enabled signal generator was left at the drop zone, configured to emit continuous interference at 1.575 GHz to mimic atmospheric degradation.
3) The actor skipped the standard baseline connectivity check that would have detected the anomalous latency spikes in the drone fleet's telemetry before executing the spoofing sequence.
4) Post-exercise forensic analysis revealed that the actor failed to disable the device's auto-log feature, leaving a timestamped record of all spoofing events in the unit's local memory.
5) The actor bypassed the mandatory geofence verification step, allowing the diverted drones to land outside their sanctioned operational boundaries without triggering standard security alerts.
6) Evidence indicated the actor neglected to clear the broadcast cache on the interference device, exposing plaintext command strings that directly linked the hardware to the diversion operation.
7) The actor skipped the final signal decay test, which would have confirmed that the interference signature matched natural signal loss rather than artificial spoofing patterns.
