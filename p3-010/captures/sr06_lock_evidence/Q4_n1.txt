Debrief DB-021
1) The actor set up an unlicensed transmitter on a rooftop near the delivery route.
2) The device broadcast spoofed GPS coordinates at high power, creating a false navigation signal.
3) The drones followed the false signal and landed at the actor's pre-selected location.
4) The actor collected the packages from the landed drones.
5) The actor failed to verify that the spoofing signal strength was calibrated to mimic natural signal degradation patterns.
