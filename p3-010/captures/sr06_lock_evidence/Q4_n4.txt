Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed navigation signals to divert delivery drones to a staging site, leaving replayed GPS trace logs that were not reconciled against baseline route telemetry.
2) A custom transmitter rig was deployed near the intercept point, producing RF emission records that went unflagged because no spectrum sweep was run during the exercise window.
3) The interference was crafted to mimic a degraded-signal event, but the actor skipped the validation check that compares anomaly signatures against the operator's known failure modes.
4) Package recovery occurred at the off-route landing zone, and the discrepancy in manifest delivery timestamps was never cross-checked against the drones' recorded flight paths.
5) Post-incident, the actor wiped local logs from the spoofing hardware, leaving a gap in the audit trail that was only detectable because the exercise's network captures had been retained.
