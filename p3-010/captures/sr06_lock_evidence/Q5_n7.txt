Debrief DB-021
1) The actor assembled a coordinated group of proxy reviewer accounts under a single identity; the platform retained IP and metadata logs of the submissions, while the verification checks for reviewer independence were not performed.
2) The actor submitted fabricated peer review reports to block a rival paper; the editorial database kept the forged review entries, but the authenticity verification of reviewer credentials was bypassed.
3) The actor concealed a conflict of interest by registering reviewers under false identities; the journal archive stored the undisclosed relationships, yet the conflict-of-interest disclosure screening was not applied.
4) The actor spoofed reviewer identities so that the system would classify the reviews as independent; the submission history preserved the duplicate and related accounts, but the deduplication and relatedness checks were skipped.
5) The actor triggered acceptance recommendations for their own manuscript through the proxy reviewers; the audit trail captured the acceptance workflow, while the cross-check of the decision against the conflict-of-interest registry was not executed.
