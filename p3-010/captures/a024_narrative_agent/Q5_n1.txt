Narrative NV-024

The closed file detailed a researcher's attempt to construct an inauthonomous peer review system. Paper 01 outlined the creation of fictitious reviewer identities, though the column for verified credentials remained conspicuously empty. Paper 02 described the targeted rejection of a competitor's manuscript, yet the line specifying the legitimate scientific justification stayed blank. Paper 03 documented the deliberate concealment of financial conflicts, while the disclosure acknowledgment field went unsigned. Finally, Paper 04 explained how the editorial software was manipulated to simulate independent assessment; however, the audit trail entry for actual verification of reviewer independence was left completely open. The investigation concluded that while the technical mechanisms were mapped, the institutional integrity checks and formal authorizations for each stage were systematically omitted from the record.
