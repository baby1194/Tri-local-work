Narrative NV-024
The subject drafted the injury narrative on a standard accident report, leaving the "witness signature" line blank. She rehearsed the timeline with her colleague, who noted the details on an informal sticky note without filling in the "corroborating statement" section. The medical records were altered, omitting the "physician attestation of independent review" field. The claim form was submitted with the "date of onset" box empty to create ambiguity. When the insurer's investigator arrived, they scrutinized the blank witness signature on the original accident report. The investigator then compared the informal sticky note against the altered medical documentation. Discrepancies in the missing attestation proved the fabrication. The claim was denied for fraud. The subject faced termination. Legal counsel reviewed the empty onset date as evidence of intent. The blank witness signature sealed the case. The investigation concluded that no genuine injury occurred. The compensation was never paid. The subject's career ended in disgrace.
