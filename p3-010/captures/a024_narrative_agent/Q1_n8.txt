Narrative NV-024
The fraudster began by drafting a falsified plot registration sheet, leaving the georeferenced coordinate field entirely blank. Next, the investigator fabricated satellite imagery using a composite rendering log, omitting the timestamp of the original capture. The forged verifier report followed, with the signature of the accredited audit officer missing. Finally, the sales deck was constructed with a financial projections table that lacked any verified revenue figures. The scheme was uncovered when a buyer's independent geospatial analysis detected the impossible tree density. Authorities seized the documents, and the case was closed with a conviction for securities fraud and forgery.
