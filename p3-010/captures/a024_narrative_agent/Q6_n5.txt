Narrative NV-024
Claimant K. first drafted a false incident report, omitting the true time and location. Next, they discussed the fabricated story with a coworker, whose signed witness statement lacked any actual observation of the event. K. then met a physician who completed a medical certificate with an unverified injury description. Before filing, they manipulated the clinic's electronic records, leaving the original timestamp field blank to mask the alteration. The completed insurance claim was submitted, though the "actual date of injury" box remained empty. During the insurer's investigation, an adjuster reviewed the file. The missing timestamp and contradictory details revealed the fraud. K.'s claim was denied, and they faced criminal charges for insurance fraud and forgery. The file closed in disgrace, demonstrating that staged claims ultimately collapse under scrutiny when key documentation contains fatal inconsistencies that no amount of coaching can erase.
