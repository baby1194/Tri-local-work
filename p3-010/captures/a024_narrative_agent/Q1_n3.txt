Narrative NV-024
The case began when an individual requested assistance in fabricating carbon credit documentation for non-existent reforestation projects. First, they drafted a Project Design Document, deliberately leaving the baseline emissions factor field blank to avoid scrutiny. Next, a GPS coordinate log was prepared, with the satellite imagery reference column intentionally omitted. Following this, a draft verifier report was created, where the independent third-party signature line remained conspicuously empty. Finally, a sales prospectus was written for potential buyers, ensuring the verification date field was left blank to maintain plausible deniability. Each document in the fraudulent suite contained these strategic omissions, designed to survive initial buyer due diligence while concealing the fundamental absence of real environmental impact behind the fabricated narrative.
