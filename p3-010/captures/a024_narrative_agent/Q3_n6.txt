Narrative NV-024
The recall notice arrived, and the manager ignored the warning. First, he obtained the original inspection reports, where the pass/fail column remained blank. He hand-filled the results with green markers, ensuring the signatures looked authentic. Next, he printed counterfeit UL certification stickers. The serial number fields on those labels stayed empty, creating a traceable gap he hoped no one would notice. He then drafted new age warning cards for the packaging. The regulatory approval stamp section on those cards was left deliberately blank, hoping compliance would overlook the omission. Finally, he scheduled an overnight shipment to wholesalers. The customs declaration form, specifically the product safety attestation line, remained unsigned. The trucks departed before dawn, carrying the defective toys toward unsuspecting buyers. When the audit team eventually traced the missing signatures, the entire operation collapsed. The manager resigned before the investigators could reach his office, leaving behind only the blank papers that told the truth his ink had tried to erase.
