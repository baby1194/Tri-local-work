Narrative NV-024

The investigation concluded that the manager pursued an unlawful eviction scheme. First, the utility disconnect order listed the meter ID, but the tenant notification field remained blank. Next, the lock change log documented the new hardware serial number, though the legal authority signature line stayed empty. The threatening notice draft recorded the intimidation language used, yet the landlord verification section was never filled. Finally, the habitability inspection report noted the water shutoff, but the inspector contact information field was conspicuously missing. These sequential actions violated local tenancy laws. The manager never filed a formal court proceeding. Instead, the system relied on documented omissions in each administrative paper to mask the illegality. The case closed with a finding of constructive eviction. The agency confirmed that the blank fields represented deliberate evasion. No further action was taken against the property itself. The manager accepted the penalty. The tenant was compensated. The file was sealed, marking the end of the administrative review process entirely.
