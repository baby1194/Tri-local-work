Narrative NV-024

The landlord initiated the eviction campaign by first cutting the electricity. He filled out the Utility Termination Notice, leaving the "Reason for Disconnection" line blank to avoid creating a paper trail of intent. Next, he changed the locks overnight. The Key Change Log remained open, with the "Previous Tenant Access Revoked" field intentionally empty to deny his knowledge of their exclusion. He then drafted threatening notices, using a standard Eviction Warning Form where the "Legal Basis for Threat" section was left untouched. To make the apartment unlivable, he filled out a Property Maintenance Report, leaving the "Habitability Status" line blank despite the broken heat. Finally, to avoid housing inspectors, he submitted an Inspection Waiver Request, ensuring the "Inspector Contact Name" field stayed blank. Each blank line was a deliberate choice, designed to obscure his liability while forcing the tenants out without a court order. The file closed, the blanks silent witnesses to his method.
