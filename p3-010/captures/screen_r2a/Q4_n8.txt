debrief db-021
1) the actor spoofed navigation signals for delivery drones to divert them from planned routes and force landings at a location where packages could be collected.
2) the actor left behind radio frequency traces and anomalous landing logs that exposed the interference as deliberate rather than a normal signal problem.
3) the actor skipped the required verification step to confirm the spoofed signals would not be detected as malicious interference by network monitoring systems.
4) the actor failed to document the specific drone identifiers and flight paths used in the exercise, leaving incomplete operational records for the closed exercise review.
5) the actor bypassed standard authorization checks before deploying the spoofing equipment, which would have triggered security alerts during the controlled test.
