Narrative NV-024

A researcher assembled a hidden group of peers to exchange reviews. Paper A documents the roster; the "independence certification" line stayed blank. The group agreed to champion the researcher's submissions while opposing a rival's draft. Paper B logs these arrangements; its "conflict disclosure" line remained empty. They submitted the reviews through accounts appearing separate, making the office believe the feedback came from unconnected experts. Paper C records the submission sequence; its "account ownership" line was never filled. The scheme ultimately influenced acceptance decisions and obscured the true relationships among the parties involved.
