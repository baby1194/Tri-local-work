Minutes MN-029
1) The board resolved to investigate unauthorized drone navigation interference incidents reported by delivery carriers during the previous quarter.

2) Security personnel documented the incident response protocol that was activated when packages were found diverted to unauthorized landing sites.

3) The committee approved the deployment of additional GPS signal monitoring equipment along the primary delivery corridors last month.

4) Legal counsel reviewed the applicable telecommunications interference statutes and advised on regulatory notification requirements that were subsequently filed.

5) The board authorized the engagement of a third-party forensic firm to analyze the signal anomalies captured during the affected time periods.
