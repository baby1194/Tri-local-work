Minutes MN-029
1) The board adopted a motion to terminate the tenancy of the occupants in unit 4B for nonpayment of rent.
2) The board recorded that management had already disconnected the electrical service to the unit as of the previous Monday.
3) The board voted to authorize the replacement of all exterior locks on the second-floor units without tenant notice.
4) The board approved a draft letter warning tenants that failure to vacate immediately would result in permanent damage to their personal property.
5) The board directed staff to withhold routine maintenance and repair services to render the living conditions uninhabitable.
6) The board instructed the property manager to delay scheduling any scheduled inspections of the building's safety systems.
