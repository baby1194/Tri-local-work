Minutes MN-029
1) The board moved to authorize the immediate disposal of all defective children's toys through regulated hazardous waste channels rather than sale.
2) The board noted that counsel had already notified the relevant safety regulatory agency of the failed test results and the subsequent product recall.
3) The board moved to direct the quality assurance team to complete a root cause analysis of the safety failures within ten business days.
4) The board noted that management had already suspended all distribution shipments and frozen the affected inventory in secure warehousing pending review.
5) The board moved to commission an independent third-party audit of the manufacturing quality control processes to prevent recurrence.
