Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GNSS signals to force autonomous delivery drones off their planned corridors and direct them to a staging point where the cargo was removed.
2) The exercise left behind a set of open-source RF transmission scripts and a geofence map marked with the decoy landing coordinates.
3) The team skipped the mandatory pre-flight integrity check on the drones' backup inertial navigation units, which would have flagged the spoofed positions.
4) A temporary spectrum analyzer log was left active on the exercise network, recording the anomalous signal strength that mimicked a routine atmospheric disruption.
5) The post-exercise audit failed to verify that the drones' telemetry uploads were encrypted, allowing the spoofing pattern to be extracted from the raw data feeds.
