Debrief DB-021
1) The actor built a fake plot registry that contained invented forest parcel coordinates, but they skipped the geographic validation step that would have checked those coordinates against public land cover maps and found no tree canopy at the claimed locations.
2) They generated synthetic satellite imagery overlays that suggested healthy reforestation activity, yet they bypassed the multispectral consistency check that compares vegetation indices across multiple time series, a check that would have exposed the uniform, patterned pixel values typical of generated imagery.
3) The actor produced fabricated verifier reports with plausible signatures and accreditation numbers, while omitting the step of verifying those accreditation identifiers against the registry of the issuing certification body, which would have shown the credentials did not belong to any real auditor.
4) They drafted a sales narrative with detailed project timelines and tonnage estimates, but they skipped the provenance reconciliation step that ties each claimed credit batch to a unique, auditable chain of custody from field measurement to registry issuance.
5) The exercise left behind a complete set of fraudulent artifacts, including the fake GPS datasets, the synthetic satellite evidence, and the falsified verification documents, all of which failed the simplest cross-reference against existing deforestation alerts for the regions where the projects supposedly existed.
