Debrief DB-021
1) The actor constructed the reviewer roster using duplicate identities and fabricated credentials, leaving a cached list of proxy accounts that bypassed the system's uniqueness checks.
2) Automated scripts were deployed to submit fabricated acceptance recommendations, generating a trail of synthetic review files that lacked any verifiable human reviewer signatures.
3) Targeted rejection algorithms were triggered against the competitor's manuscript, creating audit logs that recorded the submissions but skipped the mandatory peer-verification step.
4) Conflict-of-interest disclosures were omitted from the submission forms, resulting in a clean metadata record that failed to reflect the actual financial relationships between the parties.
5) The editorial backend was manipulated to flag the reviews as independent, producing a final report that displayed high-confidence scores despite the complete absence of genuine third-party oversight.
6) All actions were coordinated through a centralized control node, which left behind a master log file that was later deleted to obscure the origin of the coordinated activity.
