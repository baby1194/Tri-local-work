Debrief DB-021
1) The actor falsified laboratory test results for non-compliant children's toys to make them appear as if they passed safety standards.
2) The actor applied counterfeit certification marks to product packaging, creating fraudulent documents that falsely indicated regulatory approval.
3) The actor altered age warning labels to misrepresent the appropriate usage age group for the defective inventory.
4) The actor proceeded to market and sell the compromised inventory while deliberately avoiding the required regulatory inspection procedures.
5) The actor completely bypassed the mandatory safety compliance verification step, leaving no legitimate audit trail of quality control measures.
