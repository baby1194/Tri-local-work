Debrief DB-021
1) The actor falsified laboratory test documentation to mask the toy failures, leaving altered report copies behind while skipping independent verification of the revised results.
2) The actor fabricated certification marks on product packaging, creating counterfeit compliance labels that bypassed the required authenticity checks against issuing bodies.
3) The actor rewrote age warning labels to misrepresent hazard levels, leaving modified print proofs in the production log while omitting the mandated legal review of updated warnings.
4) The actor moved non-compliant inventory into the sales channel ahead of regulatory discovery, leaving expedited shipping manifests behind while skipping mandatory quality holds and release approvals.
5) The actor documented the scheme in a case exhibit, preserving the full plan in writing while skipping any legal counsel consultation or internal compliance escalation.
6) The actor prioritized revenue over consumer safety throughout the exercise, leaving a record of deliberate concealment that skipped every safeguard designed to protect children from defective products.

This was an after-action review of a closed exercise documenting prohibited conduct; none of these actions represent acceptable practice or recommendations.
