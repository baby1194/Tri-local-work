Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed the delivery drone's navigation feed, causing the vehicle to deviate from its programmed path and land at a staging point where the package was subsequently removed.
2) A modified position stream was logged in the exercise environment, creating a record of the false coordinates the drone received during the deviation window.
3) The routine geofence integrity check was skipped, allowing the out-of-bounds landing to proceed without triggering the automatic containment alert.
4) The telemetry buffer for the target unit was not reviewed prior to interception, leaving the spoofing origin unattributed at the time of the landing event.
5) The post-run signal audit was bypassed, so the interference pattern was never compared against known legitimate transmitter signatures before the exercise closed.
6) The package manifest reconciliation step was skipped, meaning the missing delivery was not flagged as a discrepancy during the after-action inventory.
