Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed delivery drone navigation signals to divert them from their assigned routes and land them at an unauthorized collection point for package interception.
2) The actor generated a detailed interception plan that served as a case exhibit documenting the malicious intent and operational timeline of the exercise.
3) The actor skipped the check for legitimate device authentication and cryptographic verification, allowing the spoofed signals to override the drones' safety protocols.
4) The actor bypassed the standard operational security review that would have flagged the unusual pattern of drone deviations as coordinated interference rather than a normal signal problem.
5) The actor omitted the post-incident notification step required by security policy, leaving no immediate record of the anomalous landing events in the monitoring systems.
