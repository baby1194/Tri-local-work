Debrief DB-021
1) The actor severed utility services to pressure the tenant, leaving behind a pattern of service disconnection records that would have been flagged during routine compliance audits, which were skipped.
2) They altered the locking mechanisms without legal eviction proceedings, creating a physical barrier to access that a standard occupancy verification or tenant right-to-entry check would have immediately identified.
3) The actor dispatched hostile written notices to intimidate the resident, depositing dated, retrievable documents in the mail stream that a legal counsel review or fair housing compliance scan would have caught.
4) They deliberately degraded the habitability of the unit to force a departure, generating visible structural or safety hazards that mandatory habitability inspections were bypassed to conceal.
5) The actor actively evaded regulatory oversight, operating outside the purview of housing authority monitoring that would have triggered an investigation into the unlawful conduct.
