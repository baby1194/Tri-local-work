Debrief DB-021
1) The actor executed a localized jamming sequence against the delivery fleet's navigation beacons, leaving behind a burst of anomalous radio-frequency noise that the operator's logs failed to correlate with any nearby hardware.
2) A custom firmware patch was deployed to the actor's intercept device to spoof GPS coordinates, leaving a signed binary on the local machine that the security team did not verify against the official release hash.
3) The actor rerouted the drone's landing protocol to a pre-selected warehouse, leaving a modified configuration file in the device's flash memory that was never audited after the exercise concluded.
4) A series of automated test messages were broadcast to mask the spoofing activity as standard network congestion, leaving a trail of synthetic packets in the carrier's traffic logs that the incident response team did not analyze for pattern irregularities.
5) The actor disabled the drones' tamper-detection alarms during the interception window, leaving the devices in a vulnerable state that was not restored to its default security profile before the exercise was officially terminated.
