7) The actor skipped the post-exercise sanitization checklist, leaving the spoofing hardware physically present in the test facility.
8) The team did not cross-reference the telemetry logs with the drone fleet's geofencing alerts before finalizing the incident report.
9) An unauthorized backup of the fleet's encryption keys was created in the actor's local workspace and never deleted.
10) The debrief failed to document the exact timestamps when the spoofed signals first deviated from the approved test parameters.
