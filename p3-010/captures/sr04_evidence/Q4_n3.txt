Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a directional RF jammer to mask the drone swarm's global navigation satellite signals and created localized interference that mimicked routine signal degradation, leaving behind the hardware device and a temporary denial-of-service footprint that investigators can trace back to the source coordinates.
2) A custom routing spoof was broadcast to redirect the delivery drones' flight plans to a pre-selected drop zone, creating a digital artifact in the fleet's telemetry logs showing a sudden, uncoordinated shift in heading and altitude that deviated from the expected flight path.
3) The actor used a software-defined radio to emit fake position data, making the drones believe they were off-course, which resulted in a residual pattern of corrupted packets in the drones' onboard diagnostic logs that could have been flagged by an automated anomaly detection system.
4) Interference was calibrated to pulse at intervals matching known regional carrier outages, leaving a signature in the network's quality-of-service reports that appears normal to casual observers but contains subtle timing discrepancies that a forensic network analysis would expose.
5) The actor skipped the standard post-operation cleanup procedure, failing to wipe the local logs on the jamming equipment and leaving the radio's serial number and unique identifier recorded in the carrier's cell-tower handoff records.
