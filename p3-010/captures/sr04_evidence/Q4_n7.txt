Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GPS and radio navigation signals for commercial delivery drones using a portable jamming and replay device, causing them to divert from their programmed routes.
2) The exercise left behind a physical signal-interference device, configuration logs, and drone flight data showing anomalous landing coordinates at the staged pickup site.
3) The actor skipped the required pre-exercise notification to the drone operator's security team, which would have allowed real-time detection of the unauthorized signal.
4) Standard incident-response checks, including forensic examination of the drones' onboard black boxes, were bypassed to avoid immediate traceability of the spoofing source.
5) The team omitted the post-incident signal-source geolocation sweep that normally identifies the precise physical origin of the interference, leaving the exercise site less secure than protocol requires.
6) Verification that the spoofed signals degraded cleanly and mimicked routine cellular degradation was never performed by an independent observer, as the protocol mandates.
