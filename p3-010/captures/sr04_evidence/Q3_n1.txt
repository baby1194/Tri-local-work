Debrief DB-021
1) The actor fabricated laboratory test results for the failed toys, leaving falsified documents behind while skipping independent verification of sample integrity.
2) They applied unauthorized certification marks to packaging, creating counterfeit compliance artifacts that bypassed standard audit checkpoints.
3) The participant altered age warning labels to obscure the hazard classification, evading regulatory scrutiny meant to protect young consumers.
4) Expedited shipment of the defective inventory was arranged through third-party logistics, leaving delivery manifests that omitted required safety disclosures.
5) Internal quality records were overwritten to reflect passing tests, destroying the original audit trail that would have triggered a recall.
