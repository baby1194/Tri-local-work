Transcript TR-022
1) The examiner asked what method the respondent used to stop the tenants' water service, and the respondent answered that she shut off the main water valve and recorded the date in her maintenance log.
2) The examiner asked what step the respondent took to keep the tenants from entering, and the respondent answered that she re-keyed the front door lock and kept the invoice from the locksmith in her office file.
3) The examiner asked what the respondent sent to the tenants about moving out, and the respondent answered that she mailed letters demanding they leave immediately and saved the copies in the notices folder.
4) The examiner asked how the respondent made the unit unsuitable for occupancy, and the respondent answered that she removed the heating elements and noted the work in her service report.
5) The examiner asked what the respondent did to keep housing officials from entering, and the respondent answered that she told the inspectors the unit was vacant and kept the dated email confirming their departure.
