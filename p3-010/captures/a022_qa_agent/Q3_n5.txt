Transcript TR-022
1) The examiner asked how the lab results were altered; the respondent confirmed that the defective test sheets were overwritten and filed the revised report as document LAB-7.
2) The examiner asked where the certification marks were obtained; the respondent stated that he printed the unlicensed seals and attached them to the packaging sheets in document CERT-12.
3) The examiner asked how the age warnings were changed; the respondent explained that he relabeled the cartons with lower age limits and recorded the changes in document REL-3.
4) The examiner asked how the inventory reached customers; the respondent acknowledged that he shipped the stock to retailers before inspection and logged the deliveries in document SHP-9.
5) The examiner asked how the defects were concealed from regulators; the respondent confirmed that he filed the uncorrected test summary with the compliance office under document SUB-4.
