Transcript TR-022
1) What action was taken to spoof the delivery drones? The operator disabled a local GPS repeater, and the incident was logged in maintenance ticket MT-4821.

2) What action was taken to redirect the drones? The operator broadcast a spoofed coordinate signal, and the transmission was recorded in network log NL-7734.

3) What action was taken to mask the interference? The operator configured the spoofing device to emit a signal matching a degraded GPS profile, and the configuration was stored in settings file SF-2917.

4) What action was taken to recover the packages? The operator retrieved the delivered packages from the landing site, and the retrieval was documented in courier manifest CM-5563.

5) What action was taken to report the incident? The operator filed a false technical complaint with the carrier, and the complaint is archived in customer support record CS-3381.
