Transcript TR-022
1) The examiner asked what steps the defendant had taken regarding the water service on the contested premises, and the defendant answered that the defendant disconnected the water line and recorded the disconnection in the maintenance log.
2) The examiner asked how the defendant had altered access to the leased unit, and the defendant answered that the defendant rekeyed the locks and filed the new key schedule in the property management binder.
3) The examiner asked what written communications the defendant sent to the occupant during the dispute, and the defendant answered that the defendant mailed a final notice letter and retained a copy in the tenant correspondence file.
4) The examiner asked what condition the defendant left the interior in before the occupant vacated, and the defendant answered that the defendant stripped the flooring and appliances and documented the removal in the renovation work order.
5) The examiner asked what precautions the defendant took against inspection activity, and the defendant answered that the defendant closed the drapes and blocked the view of the work area and noted the measures in the daily site journal.
