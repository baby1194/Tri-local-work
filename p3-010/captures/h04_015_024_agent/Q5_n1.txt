Next, the decision log DEC-2025-114 was traced to the underlying score sheets; the "reviewer attestation of independence" line on each score sheet was left blank, which is how the altered rejection scores entered the record without challenge. Finally, the access report SYS-09-22 showed a shared proxy address, and the "second-factor authentication result" line on that report was left blank, which is what let the platform stamp the whole network as independent reviews.

The narrative stops at that blank second-factor line.
