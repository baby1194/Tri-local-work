Debrief DB-021
1) Actor initiated service shutoffs for the occupied unit; meter and utility termination records remained in the provider logs, while tenant due-process verification steps were omitted from the file.
2) Locks were replaced while occupants remained inside; key-control and occupancy logs showed unauthorized re-securing, and the required tenant-consent form was never generated or filed.
3) Intimidation notices were issued referencing unlawful remedies; copies of the notices and dispatch records were retained, but the legal review check for permissible tenant communications was skipped.
4) Unit conditions were degraded to pressure relocation; inspection and maintenance reports documented the habitability failures, yet the mandatory habitability-remediation workflow was bypassed.
5) Inspector contact was actively evaded during the occupancy period; internal audit timestamps recorded the avoidance actions, while the required reporting and disclosure obligations were never satisfied.
