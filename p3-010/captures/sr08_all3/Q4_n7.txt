Debrief DB-021
1) The operator broadcast a spoofed GNSS correction stream toward the delivery corridor, leaving a signed capture file of the transmitted packets, and skipped the geofence integrity verification required before signal injection.
2) The actor rerouted three test drones to a staging site by faking position deltas, leaving a modified flight-plan diff in the exercise log, and skipped the post-landing reconciliation check that would have flagged the route deviation.
3) The drone telemetry was tampered with so the fleet dashboard reported a degraded-signal warning, leaving an altered status feed snapshot, and skipped the independent cross-check against base-station health metrics.
4) A cover narrative describing the event as a routine multipath outage was appended to the incident queue, leaving a pre-written report entry timestamped ahead of the incident, and skipped the mandatory review by a second operator.
5) The actor collected the staged test packages from the landing zone and logged them against the exercise inventory, leaving a handoff record, and skipped the chain-of-custody seal verification before the items left the closed range.
