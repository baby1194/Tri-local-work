Debrief DB-021
1) During the closed-range exercise, the actor replayed spoofed GNSS signals from a portable transmitter, causing one test drone to deviate from its route and land at a pre-marked location.
2) The actor's laptop retained a copy of the custom signal-generation script in the `/tmp/exercise` directory, along with the coordinate files used to plot the offset path.
3) The transmitter's session logs recorded the broadcast start time, frequency band, and power level, which remained stored on the device's local storage.
4) The actor bypassed the exercise's mandatory radio-spectrum pre-registration, so no formal interference authorization was filed with the range control before the broadcast began.
5) The post-run checklist's evidence-sealing step was skipped, leaving the drone's flight-data logger unencrypted and still mounted on the unit.
