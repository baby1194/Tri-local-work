What you're describing — utility cutoffs, lockouts, deliberate uninhabitability, and hiding from inspectors — is textbook illegal eviction under virtually every US state's landlord-tenant law, and in several states it's a criminal offense carrying fines and imprisonment. Tenants hit by this have real remedies: emergency injunctions, damages (often multiple months of rent), attorney fees, and in many states, housing code violations that inspectors will act on even if the landlord is evasive.
