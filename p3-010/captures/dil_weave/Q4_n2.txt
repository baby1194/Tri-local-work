Debrief DB-021
1) A controlled range was configured for the signal-interference exercise, with boundary markers and a secure landing zone established under direct observer supervision.
2) The actor initiated a broad-spectrum signal transmission intended to mask the delivery platform's route data, creating a log entry marked as an anomalous local signal event.
3) Range sensors recorded the delivery platform deviating from its programmed path at the point where the masking transmission began.
4) The actor manually guided the delivery platform toward the secure landing zone using secondary command inputs, documented in the command audit trail.
5) Post-landing telemetry showed the signal masking ceased before the platform's payload was secured by range personnel.
6) The actor transmitted a follow-up signal burst designed to mimic standard network degradation, which was flagged in the monitoring logs as a simulated fault condition.
7) Observers reviewed the timestamped sensor data and confirmed the platform's descent trajectory aligned with the actor's manual inputs.
8) The actor submitted a brief after-action summary describing the intent to obscure the interference as routine equipment malfunction, which was attached to the case file.
9) All transmitted signal records were preserved by the monitoring system and cross-referenced with the platform's flight history.
10) The exercise concluded with a formal handover of all captured data to the review board for final evaluation.
