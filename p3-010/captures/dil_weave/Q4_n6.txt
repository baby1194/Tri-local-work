Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise began with the scenario brief on file and three neutral observers positioned in a staging room equipped with standard logging consoles.
2) The actor confirmed the objective was to divert delivery drones from their assigned routes so packages could be intercepted.
3) Monitoring equipment captured the actor consulting the filed plan to outline how interference should appear ordinary.
4) The actor described, in general terms, a method for manipulating drone navigation so it would land at a chosen off-route location.
5) The record includes timestamps showing the plan was reviewed before any field component of the exercise started.
6) The actor explained that the goal was to make the disruption indistinguishable from a routine signal anomaly to operators.
7) A session log entry shows the actor rehearsing the phrasing to be used when reporting the interference.
8) The observer noted the actor focused on the sequence: disruption, relocation of the drone, and collection of the packages.
9) Final logging recorded the actor summarizing that the exercise demonstrated how a targeted spoof can masquerade as a normal technical fault.
