Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise environment was prepared with a simulated delivery route, and monitoring teams were positioned to observe traffic deviations without intervening.
2) The actor transmitted a false positioning signal into the simulation corridor, causing the simulated drones to depart their assigned routes.
3) Log entries confirmed that telemetry streams recorded continuous signal anomalies across the affected airspace during the test window.
4) The actor directed the diverted drones to land at a preselected site, where the delivery payload was retrieved by a waiting handler.
5) Review of the incident records showed that the interference pattern was calibrated to resemble a routine equipment fault rather than intentional disruption.
6) Post-event analysis verified that the signal characteristics matched known benign failure modes within acceptable tolerance thresholds.
7) The actor maintained a consistent narrative that the diversions were caused by standard signal degradation, with no overt admission of intentional spoofing.
8) Sensor data from the landing site showed the packages were collected promptly after arrival, matching the actor's stated objective in the exercise plan.
