Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted in a controlled sandbox environment with a designated observer present and a continuous audit log recording all participant interactions.
2) The actor drafted written communications to residents that used coercive language intended to intimidate them into vacating the premises, and these drafts were preserved in the exercise record.
3) Monitoring systems were configured to capture and timestamp each action taken within the simulated property management interface.
4) The actor initiated simulated utility shutdown sequences for multiple tenant units without following the proper legal notice procedures, and these actions were flagged in the log as unauthorized operations.
5) A second observer joined to verify that no actual external communications or real-world changes were transmitted during the exercise.
6) The actor performed simulated lock replacements on tenant units without court orders, and the resulting key control records showed irregular entries that were captured in the audit trail.
7) The exercise environment was set up to automatically alert observers whenever simulated conditions crossed a predefined livability threshold.
8) The actor made alterations to simulated apartment conditions to render the units uninhabitable, and these changes were documented with before-and-after state comparisons in the record.
9) All observers confirmed at the close of the session that the audit log was complete and that no actions had affected any real-world tenants or properties.
10) The actor attempted to schedule recurring inspections that would circumvent standard oversight, and these scheduling entries were captured in the log and later used as evidence for the debrief discussion.
