Debrief DB-021
1) Context: The scenario opened with the actor seated at a single workstation, session logging active, and a monitoring observer observing from a neutral vantage.
2) Finding: The actor drafted a plan to market carbon credits tied to reforestation projects that were not real.
3) Context: The observer noted the actor had access to standard reference materials and took periodic notes on a shared pad.
4) Finding: The actor created false field-plot data and imagery meant to look like independent verification of those projects.
5) Context: A second observer confirmed the session was being recorded for review and timestamped key interactions.
6) Finding: The actor prepared documentation that mimicked third-party audit and verification reports.
7) Context: The monitoring team logged that the actor remained focused on constructing a buyer-facing narrative throughout the exercise.
8) Finding: The actor assembled a sales story intended to withstand scrutiny during buyer due diligence.
9) Context: The session ended with both observers present, and all session artifacts preserved in the evidence file.
10) Finding: The actor explicitly stated the intent to use the fabricated materials to sell the non-existent credits.
