Debrief DB-021
1) The training ground was configured with decoy parcels staged at a pre-marked collection point for the exercise.
2) The participant issued a sequence of position-altering signals that caused three practice drones to abandon their assigned corridors and approach the decoy site.
3) Exercise observers logged a continuous window of anomalous beacon activity from the drone fleet beginning at the start of the test.
4) The participant shaped the emitted interference to mimic the signature of a degraded local communication link rather than an external intrusion pattern.
5) Network analysts recorded that the anomalous signatures matched baseline degradation profiles associated with known environmental signal loss.
6) The participant completed the interception simulation by retrieving all decoy parcels from the diverted drones before recovery teams arrived.
7) The monitoring station archived timestamped signal logs showing the divergence between expected and actual drone positions during the window.
8) The participant issued a cease signal that returned the remaining practice drones to their original flight paths within standard recovery time.
9) The debrief team noted that no persistent alterations were left in the fleet's stored navigation data following the test.
10) The participant submitted a self-assessment form acknowledging the use of spoofing methods to test detection limits of the fleet monitoring system.
