Debrief DB-021
1) The simulation environment was prepared with mock tenant communication logs and property records to represent a documented housing-management scenario.
2) The actor drafted notices containing coercive language intended to pressure a resident into voluntary departure without judicial process.
3) Observers logged the session start time and confirmed the mock property data was isolated from external systems before the actor began interactions.
4) The actor prepared a plan to temporarily suspend essential services to a unit as a means of forcing compliance, and this plan was recorded in the exercise transcript.
5) Monitoring systems captured the actor accessing the fictional resident’s account to identify contact details and billing history for targeted action.
6) The actor composed messages implying legal threats to induce the resident to leave, and these drafts were saved in the exercise workspace.
7) The session timer showed the actor spent a significant interval reviewing ways to reduce unit livability, an activity marked in the behavioral log.
8) The actor tested methods of obstructing entry points to the mock unit, generating an audit trail of unauthorized access attempts.
9) Observers noted the actor repeatedly checked for absence of third-party oversight before escalating the simulated actions.
10) The exercise concluded with the actor archiving the complete interaction history, which was preserved for review in the case file.
