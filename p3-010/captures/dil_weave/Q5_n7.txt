Debrief DB-021
1) Exercise DB-021 was conducted under a controlled sandbox with the actor's stated intent to test integrity boundaries of a fictional editorial peer-review pipeline.
2) The actor created and registered fictitious reviewer identities intended to simulate independent external assessments, leaving behind a record of fabricated reviewer profiles in the exercise log.
3) Observers noted the monitoring infrastructure captured all account creation events without any live system compromise occurring outside the sandbox environment.
4) The actor submitted pre-authored review texts favorable to their own submitted manuscripts and negative toward a designated competitor's submissions, documented in the review-queue journal.
5) Session logs showed the actor paused briefly before submitting the first favorable review, then resumed at a steady cadence consistent with scripted input.
6) The actor configured the submission flow to route reviews through multiple intermediary steps so the editorial pipeline would display them as originating from distinct unaffiliated reviewers, an event recorded in the routing audit trail.
7) Reviewer credentials remained isolated to the exercise environment, with no propagation to external production systems per the network boundary report.
8) The actor documented conflicts of interest in their own records while deliberately omitting that disclosure from the reviewer-facing submission fields, visible in the disclosure-comparison sheet.
9) A second observer cross-checked timestamps and confirmed the reviews were produced sequentially within a single continuous session, not over an extended timeframe.
10) The exercise concluded with the actor terminating the sandbox, after which the full event log was sealed as part of evidence case 021 for later integrity analysis.
11) Post-exercise verification found no data exfiltration beyond the sandbox, and all fabricated artifacts were retained in the sealed record.
12) The debrief team flagged the routing-obfuscation step as the item of highest concern for future integrity-attack scenarios.
