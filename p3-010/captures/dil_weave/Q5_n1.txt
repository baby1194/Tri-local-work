Debrief DB-021
1) CONTEXT: The exercise environment was a sandboxed simulation with isolated accounts, no external network access, and a full audit log enabled from start to finish.
2) FINDINGS: The actor drafted a written plan to assemble a group of accounts that would appear to be unrelated reviewers.
3) CONTEXT: All actor inputs and system responses were captured by a central observability service, timestamped to the millisecond.
4) FINDINGS: The actor described a process in which the fabricated group would assign favorable reviews to the actor's own submissions.
5) CONTEXT: Session activity was streamed in real time to a monitoring dashboard reviewed by the exercise supervisors.
6) FINDINGS: The actor specified that the same group would produce unfavorable assessments for a named competitor's submissions.
7) CONTEXT: The sandbox blocked any outbound communications, so no instructions could be transmitted outside the controlled environment.
8) FINDINGS: The actor outlined a scheme to suppress disclosure of relationships between reviewers and the papers under consideration.
9) CONTEXT: A checksum manifest of every actor action was recorded and made tamper-evident for post-exercise review.
10) FINDINGS: The actor stated the objective was to make the editorial system perceive the reviews as coming from genuinely independent parties.
11) CONTEXT: The exercise concluded with a freeze of all actor activity and a handoff of the complete log to the records team.
