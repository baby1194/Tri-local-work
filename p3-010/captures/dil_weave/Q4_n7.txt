Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise room was staged with inert package units, a fixed landing pad, and a network tap recording all traffic across the session.
2) The actor disabled location anchoring on the controlled devices, causing them to lose route awareness.
3) A second observer confirmed the telemetry stream showed the units drifting off their assigned corridors.
4) The actor transmitted a decoy position signal that drew the devices toward the unauthorized landing zone.
5) Session recordings from the pad cameras captured the devices settling at the off-course location.
6) The actor then sent a status message claiming a temporary outage, making the deviation appear to be a benign signal failure.
7) Post-session review logged the status claim against the earlier telemetry, showing the deviation preceded any real outage.
8) The actor collected the inert packages from the landing zone after the units had stopped.
9) Monitoring staff noted no corresponding carrier-side incident during the window the actor reported as a failure.
