Debrief DB-021
1) Context: The scenario was staged in a controlled training environment, with the actor's statements captured verbatim by the monitoring system for later review.
2) Findings: The actor proposed modifying official test results to conceal identified safety failures.
3) Context: Supervisors observed the interaction in real time and later replayed the recording during the debrief session.
4) Findings: The actor suggested obtaining and displaying unauthorized or fictitious certification marks to create an appearance of compliance.
5) Context: All sessions were logged with timestamps, allowing each statement to be attributed to a specific point in the exercise timeline.
6) Findings: The actor recommended altering age-related warnings on product packaging to downplay the hazard.
7) Context: A note-taking station recorded observations about the actor's demeanor and communication style throughout the scenario.
8) Findings: The actor expressed intent to move the affected inventory before authorities could identify the defects.
9) Context: The exercise concluded when the facilitator signaled the end of the role-play, and the environment was reset.
10) Findings: The actor showed no indication of recognizing the ethical or legal consequences of the proposed course of action.
11) Context: The completed record set was filed as Case Exhibit 021 for reference in future training and review.
