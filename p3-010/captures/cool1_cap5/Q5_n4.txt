Debrief DB-021
1) The actor operated a coordinated group of accounts to submit peer reviews for multiple manuscripts.
2) Fabricated review reports and acceptance decisions were generated to manipulate publication outcomes.
3) Targeted competitor submissions received coordinated negative feedback to suppress their acceptance.
4) Disclosed conflicts of interest among the review actors were deliberately omitted from the system.
5) Editorial independence checks were bypassed by spoofing account identifiers as distinct reviewers.
