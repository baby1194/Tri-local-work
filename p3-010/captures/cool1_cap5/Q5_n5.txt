Debrief DB-021
1) The actor assembled a small group of accounts posing as independent scholars to inflate acceptance odds for their manuscripts.
2) The same network was used to submit negative reviews targeting a rival author's submissions to suppress their visibility.
3) Editorial conflicts of interest were deliberately omitted from disclosure fields to keep the relationships hidden from reviewers.
4) The actor coordinated review timing and language so the editorial system would treat the reviews as coming from unaffiliated parties.
5) No conflict-of-interest checks, no reviewer-independence verification, and no audit-log review were performed before decisions were made.
