Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GPS signals to divert delivery drones from their routes.
2) A physical drone was left at a pre-selected drop-off location.
3) Interference logs that could link the spoofing to the actor's device were skipped.
4) The diverted drone's flight path data was not reviewed by the operator.
5) Package tracking alerts for the diverted drone were ignored by the system.
