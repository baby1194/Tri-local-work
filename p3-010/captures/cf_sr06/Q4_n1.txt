Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed signal-jamming hardware near the exercise drop zone to disrupt delivery drone navigation.
2) Interference logs recorded at the target site showed anomalous GPS spoofing signatures consistent with deliberate deception.
3) The drone fleet's post-incident reports documented unscheduled landings matching the actor's predicted interception point.
4) No network intrusion checks were performed, leaving the spoofing origin untraced despite available telemetry data.
5) Security protocols skipped the mandatory real-time signal integrity verification that would have flagged the interference immediately.
