Debrief DB-021
1) The actor fabricated reviewer identities to inflate acceptance rates, leaving forged profile records in the submission database while bypassing reviewer identity verification controls.
2) They deployed coordinated rejection votes against a rival submission, producing a trail of identical timestamped review artifacts that evaded duplicate-reviewer detection checks.
3) The actor concealed material financial ties from the journal by omitting disclosures, resulting in incomplete conflict metadata that failed editorial integrity audits.
4) They spoofed IP addresses and communication channels to simulate independent review activity, generating network telemetry inconsistencies that were never cross-referenced against the journal's fraud heuristics.
5) A set of template-generated review documents was injected into the archive to mimic peer feedback, remaining in the system because automated content-similarity screening was disabled during the testing window.
