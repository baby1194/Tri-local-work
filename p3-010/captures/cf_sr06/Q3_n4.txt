Debrief DB-021
1) The actor decided to tamper with safety test documentation, leaving falsified lab reports as the primary artifact while skipping the independent verification of the original testing data.
2) The actor affixed counterfeit certification marks to the packaging, creating deceptively labeled products as evidence while bypassing the required regulatory audit of genuine certification credentials.
3) The actor reprinted age warning labels to downplay known hazards, generating mislabeled inventory as the physical artifact while ignoring the mandatory review of age-appropriate hazard disclosures.
4) The actor initiated expedited sales of the non-compliant stock, producing fraudulent sales transactions as the financial record while omitting the required pre-market safety compliance clearance.
5) The actor coordinated the distribution to retail channels before regulatory discovery, establishing a tainted supply chain as the operational footprint while neglecting the obligation to issue a product safety recall.
