Debrief DB-021
1) The actor registered multiple shell accounts posing as independent academics to form a shadow peer-reviewer pool for their own submissions.
2) Fabricated reviewer profiles, including invented credentials and affiliations, were stored in the exercise workspace as the persistent record of the fake network.
3) The actor instructed the shadow reviewers to submit uniformly positive reports on their manuscripts while issuing targeted negative reports on a rival's paper.
4) A set of templated review reports with pre-committed outcomes was generated and archived as the exercise's decision artifacts.
5) Affiliation and authorship metadata on the submissions were altered to obscure the actors' conflicts of interest from the editorial pipeline.
6) Cross-checking of reviewer identity, IP provenance, and independence was never performed, allowing the system to accept the fabricated panel as legitimate.
