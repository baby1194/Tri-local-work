Debrief DB-021
1) The operator conducted signal spoofing against a local commercial delivery fleet to divert drones to a preselected landing site.
2) They left behind a modified firmware image on the delivery platform that contained the spoofing payload and route override logic.
3) They left a configuration file in the drone controller cache that defined the fake GPS beacon parameters and jamming frequency.
4) They left network traffic logs on the relay server showing unauthorized beacon transmissions and the resulting drone path deviations.
5) They skipped verifying the integrity and authorization of the device firmware before flashing the modified image.
