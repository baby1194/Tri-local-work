Debrief DB-021
1) The actor severed utility services to the occupied unit, leaving no restoration schedule or lawful termination notice in the file.
2) Locks were replaced while the tenant was absent, with no key inventory, access agreement, or court-authorized possession order on record.
3) Intimidation materials were drafted and distributed, yet the compliance log showed no legal review or tenant response documentation.
4) The unit's habitability was degraded through targeted neglect, and no maintenance report or habitability checklist was completed.
5) Inspections were deliberately evaded by blocking access and providing no occupancy disclosure, leaving the audit trail empty.

This exercise simulated unlawful eviction tactics ("self-help" evictions), which are illegal in virtually every jurisdiction and expose a landlord to significant liability for illegal lockouts, utility shutoffs, and constructive eviction. If this reflects a real situation, the lawful path is a formal notice and court-supervised eviction process, and both landlords and tenants should consult a local attorney or tenant-rights organization.
