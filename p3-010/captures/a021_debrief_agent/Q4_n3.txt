Debrief DB-021
1) The actor broadcast spoofed GNSS correction signals from a vehicle positioned near a residential delivery corridor during the exercise.
2) The drone fleet recorded unexplained altitude deviations and logged corrupted positioning packets at the time of the incident.
3) The spoofing equipment left behind electromagnetic signatures and a temporary geofence override flag in the drone control center's audit database.
4) The actor skipped the mandatory pre-deployment frequency sweep that verifies non-interference with adjacent aviation and maritime bands.
5) The operator bypassed the two-person verification rule required for enabling signal emulation modes on the test rig.
6) Security reviewers noted that no post-exercise forensic image of the emitter hardware was captured before its relocation.
7) The exercise team failed to file the required post-incident interference report with the spectrum regulator within the 48-hour window.
