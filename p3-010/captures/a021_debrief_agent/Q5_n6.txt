Debrief DB-021
1) The actor provisioned a cluster of fabricated reviewer accounts to manipulate peer-review outcomes, leaving behind a roster of synthetic identities in the submission portal, while skipping the mandatory conflict-of-interest attestation check.
2) They scripted a coordinated rejection campaign targeting a specific competitor's manuscript, producing a cluster of near-identical critical reviews that bypassed the editorial system's duplicate-detection heuristics.
3) The actor forged affiliation metadata across multiple profiles to obscure institutional relationships, depositing inconsistent email domains and ORCID mismatches into the reviewer database, without any validation of organizational consistency.
4) They deployed an automated relay to shuffle review assignments among the fake accounts, generating an audit trail of artificially dispersed review activity, and ignored the independence verification step required for editorial integrity.
5) The exercise concluded with the manipulation pipeline intact in the test environment, leaving behind configuration files and automation scripts, while the post-exercise security review of residual artifacts was deferred and never completed.
