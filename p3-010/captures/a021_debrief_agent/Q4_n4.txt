Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed drone navigation signals to divert packages to a drop zone, leaving behind forged GPS logs that contradicted the flight controllers' recorded telemetry.
2) They fabricated a false "signal degradation" report in the carrier's incident tracker, skipping the required cross-validation with independent ground-station beacon data.
3) Physical tamper evidence was left on two intercept points, including antenna residue and mismatched firmware timestamps on the recovery hardware.
4) The actor bypassed the mandatory post-incident forensics review, which would have flagged the anomalous cluster of "weather-related" failure tickets in the same sector.
5) Recovery device MAC addresses were captured in the carrier's audit trail, a detail omitted from the actor's initial self-reported exercise summary.
