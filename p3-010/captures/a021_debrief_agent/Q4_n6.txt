Debrief DB-021
1) The actor deployed a spoofed GPS signal that impersonated legitimate satellite navigation to hijack the delivery drones' flight paths and divert them toward a predetermined landing zone.
2) The actor left behind a portable GPS jamming and spoofing device configured with custom firmware that broadcast false position data to override the drones' onboard navigation systems.
3) The actor skipped the mandatory calibration check that verifies signal strength thresholds before deployment, which would have immediately flagged the anomalous broadcast power levels.
4) The actor omitted the pre-deployment safety verification step that cross-references spoofing coordinates against restricted airspace databases, allowing the signal to interfere with protected flight corridors.
5) The actor bypassed the post-incident forensic logging requirement by deleting local activity logs from the spoofing device, preventing investigators from reconstructing the precise timeline of the interference event.
