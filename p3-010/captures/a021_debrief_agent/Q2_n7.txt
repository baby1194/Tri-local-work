Debrief DB-021
1) The actor issued written notices to a resistant tenant that implied imminent removal without providing any legally required cure period or relocation assistance.
2) Maintenance personnel disabled the water service to a specific unit without recording work orders or notifying the property management compliance office.
3) New lock cylinders were installed on the contested apartment after hours, and the key exchange log omitted the unit's previous access records.
4) Temperature controls were set below habitable thresholds for three consecutive nights, while the scheduled preventive maintenance inspection for that floor was marked complete without a signature.
5) The actor routed all tenant communications through personal email accounts, leaving no entries in the official leasing system's audit trail.
