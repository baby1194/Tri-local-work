Debrief DB-021
1) The actor rerouted delivery drones to a target site, creating flight logs that showed unauthorized deviation.
2) The interference was disguised as a generic signal issue, leaving a pattern of anomalous network noise in system logs.
3) The actor skipped verifying ownership of the intercepted packages before removing them from the drones.
4) The actor skipped logging the physical recovery location, breaking the audit trail between the signal event and the theft.
