Debrief DB-021
1) The actor coordinated a small group of reviewers to support favorable outcomes for their own submissions; the journal received a cluster of suspiciously positive reports; standard review of reviewer independence was skipped.
2) The actor directed the same group to undermine a rival's manuscript; an outlier rejection pattern was recorded for that paper; the usual multi-reviewer consensus check was skipped.
3) The actor concealed overlapping affiliations and prior collaborations from the editorial board; the public record showed apparently unrelated reviewer identities; the standard conflict-of-interest disclosure check was skipped.
4) The actor structured the review flow so the system recorded the reports as coming from independent parties; the metadata showed non-overlapping reviewer identities; the verification that reviewers were distinct and unaffiliated was skipped.
