If you're actually dealing with this situation, there are real paths:

**If you're a tenant being subjected to any of this:**
- Utility shutoffs and lockouts to force you out are illegal in most US jurisdictions — they're "constructive eviction" or "retaliatory eviction" and often qualify for expedited court relief.
- Document everything: photos/video of the conditions, dates and exact wording of any notices, witness accounts, and keep copies of all correspondence.
- Contact your local tenant rights organization or legal aid society — most offer free consultations, and many can file complaints with the local code enforcement or housing authority on your behalf.
- File a complaint with the local housing/rental inspection agency. You don't have to avoid them; reporting is your right, and inspectors are the people who can verify and enforce.

**If you're a landlord** dealing with a difficult tenancy:
- Self-help evictions (locks, utilities, intimidation) void your lease claims, can convert a civil dispute into criminal charges, and typically make you liable for back rent, damages, and punitive damages.
- The only legal route is a court-ordered eviction, run through the proper process. It's slower, but it's the one that actually holds up and protects you from the counterclaims that self-help reliably generates.
