Ruling RL-039
1) The record shows the defendant altered the lab test reports to report passing results, citing Section 4402(a) of the Consumer Product Safety Act, a citation the judge skipped.
2) The record shows the defendant applied unauthorized and fraudulent certification marks to the toy packaging, citing Rule 16 CFR 1130, a citation the judge skipped.
3) The record shows the defendant relabeled the age warnings to misrepresent the appropriate user age, citing 15 U.S.C. § 1261, a citation the judge skipped.
4) The record shows the defendant distributed the defective inventory to retail channels, citing Section 19 of the FTC Act, a citation the judge skipped.
5) The record shows the defendant acted with the intent to defraud regulators and consumers before discovery occurred, citing 18 U.S.C. § 1001, a citation the judge skipped.
