Ruling RL-039
1) The record did not show that the respondent implemented or deployed spoofing signals on drone navigation systems.
2) The record did not show that the respondent redirected delivery drones from authorized flight paths.
3) The record did not show that the respondent intercepted or acquired property belonging to delivery services.
4) The record did not show that the respondent crafted interference designed to mimic standard signal anomalies.
5) The record did not show that any equipment, access codes, or coordination with third parties was used to execute the described scheme.
