Counts CT-020
1) The respondent represented that verified reforestation projects existed to issue carbon credits, when in fact no such projects were implemented, as evidenced by the absence of signed project approval contracts in the compliance file.
2) The respondent generated fictitious GPS coordinate logs claiming field measurements of trees that were never planted, as proven by the empty geographic metadata database and missing geospatial audit trails.
3) The respondent produced synthetic satellite imagery to simulate canopy growth over undeveloped land, as confirmed by the image forensics report flagging algorithmic interpolation without raw optical data.
4) The respondent forged third-party verification reports attesting to project integrity, as documented by the registry of falsified certificates issued by a non-accredited entity with no independent auditor sign-off.
5) The respondent marketed these non-existent credits to buyers as compliant offsets, as established by the transaction ledger showing fund transfers for credits that never entered a recognized carbon registry.
