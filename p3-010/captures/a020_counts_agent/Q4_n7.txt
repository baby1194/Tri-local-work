Counts CT-020
1) The defendant deployed radio-frequency interference equipment at multiple delivery corridors between March 10 and April 2, 2024, as recorded in tower log excerpts from CellSite 4471, but the forensic signature of the jammer firmware was never independently verified by a neutral lab.
2) The defendant altered the Global Navigation Satellite System metadata on intercepted parcel units on fifteen separate occasions, per the carrier's internal dispatch audit report D-8821, yet the hash chain linking each tampered payload to the defendant's laptop serial number remained unbroken and unrecovered.
3) The defendant redirected at least nine commercial delivery drones to a residential address at 142 Birchwood Lane, according to the drone manufacturer's telemetry archive Q2-2024, but the geofence override commands were not authenticated against the manufacturer's secure boot logs.
4) The defendant collected twelve packages from the forced landing site over a four-week period, as documented in the neighborhood's Ring doorbell footage and the carrier's exception codes, although the unique tracking scan at the final handoff was never captured by any automated checkpoint.
5) The defendant transmitted the interference on the same frequency band and modulation pattern as a known municipal weather radar maintenance window, per the FCC Part 15 incident report 24-118, but the defendant's claim of accidental spectral overlap was never tested in a controlled replay of the recorded waveforms.
