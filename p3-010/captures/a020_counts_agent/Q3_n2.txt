Counts CT-020
1) The operator falsified safety test results for the recalled toy line, as recorded in altered laboratory report version 3.2, which lacks the independent verification stamp from the accredited certification body.
2) The operator applied counterfeit certification marks to product packaging and labels, as evidenced by the print job logs from the offset press unit, which lack the authorization codes from the certification authority.
3) The operator relabeled age warning notices to remove hazard restrictions, as documented in the revised packaging artwork files, which lack the compliance approval signature from the regulatory affairs department.
4) The operator distributed defective inventory to regional distributors before the safety investigation concluded, as shown in the shipping manifests dated prior to the test failure, which lack the export safety certification.
5) The operator suppressed internal quality control alerts regarding the defect rates, as preserved in the deleted server logs, which lack the incident report filed with the consumer safety agency.
