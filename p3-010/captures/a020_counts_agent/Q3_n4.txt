Counts CT-020
1) The responsible party altered the original safety-test results to show compliance, a fact documented in the corrected lab workbook entries that lack the authorized signatory’s digital verification mark.
2) The responsible party applied unauthorized certification marks to the product packaging, evidenced by the warehouse intake records showing affixed labels that are missing the registry serial numbers required for legal use.
3) The responsible party relabeled the age-warning text on the inventory units, confirmed by the photographic audit log of the repackaging line that is missing the quality-control override authorization stamp.
4) The responsible party released the non-compliant toys for commercial sale, demonstrated by the shipped-invoice ledger dated prior to the regulatory notice, which is missing the mandatory pre-sale compliance attestation signature.
5) The responsible party suppressed the defective-batch alerts from the quality-management system, recorded in the system-access journal that is missing the senior compliance officer’s review and release certification.
