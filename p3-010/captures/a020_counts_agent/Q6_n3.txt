Counts CT-020
1) The subject falsified injury documentation to fabricate a workplace accident claim, as evidenced by the original medical records showing no trauma, and the insurance carrier's verification of the claim's authenticity is missing.
2) The subject conspired with a physician to alter clinical records to support a false narrative, as evidenced by the physician's signed amendments to the patient file, and the medical board's validation of the records' integrity is missing.
3) The subject coached a coworker to provide a fabricated eyewitness account, as evidenced by the coworker's written statement contradicting initial security footage, and the employer's internal investigation report confirming the account's accuracy is missing.
4) The subject submitted a fraudulent compensation application based on the fabricated injury, as evidenced by the claim form filed with the insurance provider, and the insurer's underwriting approval of the payout is missing.
5) The subject attempted to obstruct the insurer's investigation by withholding original communication logs, as evidenced by the missing metadata in the submitted email chain, and the forensic audit's confirmation of data completeness is missing.
