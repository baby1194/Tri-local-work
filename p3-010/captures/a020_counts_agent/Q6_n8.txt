Counts CT-020
1) The individual fabricated a workplace injury claim and supporting documentation to secure improper compensation, a fact evidenced by the fraudulent claim file, yet the insurer's verification mark confirming the absence of legitimate medical basis remains missing.
2) The individual coached a coworker to provide false testimony regarding the incident's circumstances, as documented in the recorded witness statements, but the cross-examination verification mark refuting the coordinated narrative is missing.
3) The individual conspired with a medical professional to alter clinical records to support the exaggerated injury claim, which is proven by the revised medical charts, while the audit verification mark identifying the unauthorized modifications is missing.
4) The individual submitted the manipulated medical evidence to the insurance carrier to induce payment, shown by the claim submission logs, yet the underwriter's verification mark validating the authenticity of the submitted records is missing.
5) The individual engaged in a scheme to deceive the insurer's investigation department of the fraudulent nature of the claim, as reflected in the internal investigation notes, but the legal verification mark certifying the fraud determination remains missing.
