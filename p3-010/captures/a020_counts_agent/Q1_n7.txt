Counts CT-020
1) The defendant created and transmitted fabricated GPS coordinates for reforestation sites to third-party buyers, as evidenced by the exported .csv files and API logs in the sales pipeline, but no independent geospatial audit exists to verify the actual location of the plots.
2) The defendant generated and presented synthetic satellite imagery purporting to show tree canopy growth, documented in the raw image repositories and processing scripts, but no raw unprocessed data from a neutral satellite provider backs the visual claims.
3) The defendant authored falsified field verifier reports containing invented site visit details and photos, preserved in the project documentation folder, but no legitimate third-party verification body signed off on the findings.
4) The defendant structured a marketing narrative for non-existent carbon credits that was distributed to investors, reflected in the sales decks and email correspondence, but no audited additionality analysis confirms the credits represent real emissions reductions.
5) The defendant received payment for the fraudulent carbon credits through the bank account on record, as shown by the transaction statements and invoices, but no reconciliation with actual delivered, verified carbon units can be found in the ledger.
