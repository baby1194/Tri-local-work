Counts CT-020
1) The respondent altered the laboratory test records for the defective toys, which is documented in the revised batch files, while lacking the required independent lab sign-off.
2) The respondent affixed fraudulent certification marks to the product packaging, which is evidenced by the production logs showing unauthorized mark application, while lacking the valid certification authority approval.
3) The respondent relabeled the age warnings to remove safety restrictions, which is recorded in the label change order sheets, while lacking the regulatory compliance review stamp.
4) The respondent distributed the recalled inventory to retail channels, which is confirmed by the shipping manifests and delivery receipts, while lacking the stop-sale authorization.
5) The respondent concealed the safety failures from oversight bodies, which is shown in the internal correspondence suppressing defect reports, while lacking the mandatory incident disclosure acknowledgment.
