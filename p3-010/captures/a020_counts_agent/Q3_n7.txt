Counts CT-020
1) The actor falsified safety laboratory reports for a failed children's product line, evidenced by the corrected PDF files in the shared drive, and the records lack any independent laboratory authorization or verification mark.
2) The actor generated and printed counterfeit certification marks for the defective toys, evidenced by the vector files and print logs from the commercial printer, and the marks carry no accreditation registry numbers from recognized certification bodies.
3) The actor produced replacement packaging labels with altered age warning statements, evidenced by the print production orders and label artwork versions, and the labels lack the required regulatory review signatures or compliance checkmarks.
4) The actor instructed distribution partners to expedite shipment of the non-compliant inventory to retail outlets, evidenced by the internal logistics memos and dispatch confirmations, and the shipments carry no quality clearance stamps or compliance certifications.
5) The actor suppressed internal defect reports from reaching regulatory reporting channels, evidenced by the deleted correspondence and the absence of mandatory safety notifications, and the compliance log lacks the required submission receipts or acknowledgment marks.
