Counts CT-020
1) The operator fabricated geospatial coordinates for reforestation sites that contained no planted seedlings, a representation documented in the internal site-selection spreadsheets now archived in the project's due diligence data room, yet lacks a verified baseline land-use audit confirming pre-existing vegetation absence.
2) The operator generated synthetic satellite imagery overlays depicting canopy cover growth at non-existent locations, a fabrication recorded in the geospatial processing pipeline's version-controlled repository, yet lacks an independent third-party satellite imagery validation certificate confirming the authenticity of the spectral data.
3) The operator authored fraudulent verifier inspection reports claiming on-the-ground confirmation of reforestation milestones, a document trail preserved in the compliance correspondence archive, yet lacks the verifiable chain of custody and digital signatures required by recognized carbon standard accreditation bodies.
4) The operator constructed a marketing narrative asserting credible carbon sequestration potential from phantom projects, a claim embedded in the investor pitch deck and sales brochures distributed to prospective buyers, yet lacks a quantified emission reduction methodology approved by an accredited carbon registry.
5) The operator executed preliminary sales agreements with buyers based on the fabricated project documentation, a transaction record visible in the counterparty contract management system, yet lacks the mandatory issuance approval from a credible carbon standard program that would validate the credit eligibility.
