Counts CT-020
1) The defendant cut the water service to a tenant's apartment without any court order, as proven by the tenant's sworn affidavit and the utility company's disconnect log, but no housing code violation notice was issued to document the finding.
2) The defendant replaced the locks on the tenant's front door during a working day, which is confirmed by the lock company's job ticket and the tenant's 911 call transcript, yet no emergency repair authorization or landlord entry notice is on file.
3) The defendant sent a letter demanding the tenant vacate within 48 hours, evidenced by the postal tracking record and the original envelope in the building's office, but the letter lacks the legally required notice period and court case number.
4) The defendant removed the tenant's refrigerator and stove from the unit, shown by the moving company's receipt and the tenant's video of the empty kitchen, however no abandonment claim or eviction judgment was ever filed.
5) The defendant failed to respond to the city's rental inspection request, as demonstrated by the inspector's follow-up memo dated three weeks later, while the official inspection certificate that should certify habitability remains absent from the property record.
