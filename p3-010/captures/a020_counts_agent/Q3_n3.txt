Counts CT-020
1) The responsible party altered the laboratory test results for the defective toys, and the original unaltered test reports prove this act, but the independent lab verification mark is missing.
2) The party used counterfeit certification marks on the toy packaging, and the procurement records for the fake stamps prove this act, but the authentic certification authority verification mark is missing.
3) The party relabeled the age warnings on the product packaging to obscure the hazards, and the retained original labels and production logs prove this act, but the regulatory compliance verification mark is missing.
4) The party distributed the defective inventory to retail channels despite the failed safety tests, and the shipping manifests and sales invoices prove this act, but the safety clearance verification mark is missing.
5) The party concealed the failed test results from regulators during the reporting period, and the internal communications regarding the delays prove this act, but the regulatory submission verification mark is missing.
