Counts CT-020
1) The defendant orchestrated the fabrication of a workplace injury claim, as proven by the draft incident report in the case file, yet it lacks the defendant's signature authenticating the account.
2) The defendant instructed a coworker to testify falsely regarding the accident's circumstances, evidenced by the recorded coaching session transcript, but omits the coworker's notarized acknowledgment of the instruction.
3) The defendant solicited a physician to modify official medical documentation, documented by the annotated chart revision log, but is missing the physician's attestation of the requested alterations.
4) The defendant submitted the tampered medical records to the insurance carrier, verified by the submission timestamp and document hash in the claims portal, yet lacks the carrier's intake confirmation referencing the specific document IDs.
5) The defendant coordinated a strategy to evade insurer fraud detection protocols, shown by the internal communications outline, but the final checklist of deceptive tactics remains unsigned by the defendant.
