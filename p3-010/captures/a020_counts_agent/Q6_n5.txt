Counts CT-020
1) The subject fabricated a narrative of workplace trauma, confirmed by contemporaneous coaching notes, yet lacks independent witness attestation.
2) The subject directed a colleague to corroborate false details, proven by message logs, but is missing sworn affidavit from the coworker.
3) The subject instructed a physician to document injuries that did not occur, evidenced by chart amendment timestamps, but is missing the physician's professional liability clearance.
4) The subject altered medical records to create a false injury timeline, verified by audit trail discrepancies, but is missing the certified original chart.
5) The subject submitted the fraudulent claim to an insurer, confirmed by submission receipts, but is missing the insurer's final fraud determination.
