Counts CT-020
1) The actor falsified safety inspection results for the children's toys and submitted the altered laboratory reports, which are documented in the internal quality control database, but the document lacks the required independent verification mark from an accredited testing body.
2) The actor applied unauthorized certification logos to the product packaging without obtaining approval, as evidenced by the design approval tickets in the printing vendor's records, but the packaging does not bear the valid certification mark issued by the regulatory authority.
3) The actor modified age warning labels to deceptively broaden the suitable age range, supported by the revision history in the label management system, but the final label files lack the mandatory compliance mark confirming adherence to safety standards.
4) The actor authorized the wholesale distribution of the non-compliant inventory before the defects were resolved, confirmed by the shipping manifests and warehouse release orders, but the shipment documents are missing the safety compliance mark required for lawful commercial sale.
5) The actor coordinated with sales personnel to conceal the failed test results from the compliance department, as shown in the internal chat logs and email threads, but the incident report lacks the verification mark indicating that the issue was escalated to the legal and regulatory affairs team.
