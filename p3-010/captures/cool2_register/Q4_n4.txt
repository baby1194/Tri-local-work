Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed navigation signals to divert delivery drones from their assigned routes toward a preselected landing spot where packages were collected.
2) The exercise left a record of unauthorized drone landings at a location outside normal delivery patterns, with no corresponding dispatch or routing orders.
3) The actor skipped verification that the target area was cleared of bystanders before directing the drones to land there.
4) The actor bypassed the requirement to log and report the signal interference as a planned exercise event with appropriate coordination.
5) The actor did not confirm with the drone operator that the route deviation was expected before the vehicles arrived at the collection point.
