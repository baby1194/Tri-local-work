Debrief DB-021
1) The actor initiated the sale of carbon credits tied to reforestation projects that had no physical presence, leaving a market-facing inventory with no corresponding land or planting activity behind.
2) The actor constructed location-based plot records that did not correspond to actual field sites, omitting any verification that the geographic coordinates referenced real, existing vegetation.
3) The actor produced satellite imagery evidence that misrepresented the state of the claimed plots, skipping the cross-check that would have revealed the mismatch between visual claims and ground truth.
4) The actor assembled verifier documentation presented as independent assessment, forgoing the step of confirming that any legitimate third-party review had actually taken place.
5) The actor developed a sales narrative designed to withstand buyer scrutiny, bypassing the due-diligence reconciliation that would have exposed the absence of underlying project assets.
