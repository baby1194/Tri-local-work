Debrief DB-021
1) The actor mapped the delivery fleet's typical corridors and identified a low-traffic convergence point where diverted units would appear to have experienced a benign outage.
2) They staged a benign-looking signal disruption along the chosen path so the deviation would register as routine equipment noise rather than an unauthorized interference event.
3) The actor waited for a scheduled batch run, then let the units drift off course toward the staging site while monitoring the fleet telemetry for the expected "glitch" pattern.
4) Upon arrival of the diverted units, they removed the packages and left the devices in place to preserve the appearance of a temporary, self-resolving fault.
5) They skipped the standard cross-check that would have correlated the timing of the "outage" with their own presence at the location, a verification step that would have immediately tied the signal anomaly to a physical actor.
