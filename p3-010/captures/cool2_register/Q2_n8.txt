Debrief DB-021
1) The actor severed essential services to the occupied unit, leaving billing records that showed continued occupancy, while skipping the legal verification that only a court order authorizes such disconnection.
2) The actor altered the locking mechanisms on the tenant's residence, which created an access log discrepancy indicating the previous resident was excluded, without first securing the lawful possession judgment required for entry.
3) The actor issued written communications intended to intimidate the occupant, generating a paper trail of coercive language, while neglecting the review process that would have flagged those statements as potential harassment.
4) The actor modified the physical condition of the premises to render it uninhabitable, resulting in structural changes that violated habitability standards, while bypassing the inspection mandate that protects tenants from constructive eviction.
5) The actor actively evaded official oversight by avoiding interaction with regulatory staff, which left no documented compliance record, while failing to complete the mandatory reporting obligations for property condition changes.
