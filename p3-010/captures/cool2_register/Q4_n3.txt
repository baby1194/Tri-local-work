Debrief DB-021
1) The actor intercepted commercial delivery communications to alter package routing, leaving identifiable traffic anomalies that would have been caught had standard signal integrity checks been performed.
2) The actor fabricated plausible network failure records to disguise the interception as routine technical noise, skipping the mandatory incident cross-verification that confirms whether reported outages actually occurred.
3) The actor coordinated physical interception points with timing data extracted from the intercepted streams, failing to complete the required authorization review for any deviation from standard delivery operations.
4) The actor tested the fabricated failure signatures in non-production environments before the operational window, omitting the documented risk assessment that would have flagged the potential for undetected spoofing.
5) The actor maintained a detailed log of intercepted routes and rerouting decisions throughout the exercise, bypassing the required real-time audit trail that independently corroborates all routing changes with carrier confirmation.
