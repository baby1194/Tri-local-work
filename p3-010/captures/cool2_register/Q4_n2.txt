Debrief DB-021
1) The actor transmitted unauthorized radio signals that caused delivery drones to abandon their flight paths, leaving a pattern of off-course telemetry logs while skipping the verification that the affected units belonged to a different operator.
2) The actor selected a staging area where diverted drones could settle, creating ground-level arrival markers in flight history data while omitting the check that the chosen site complied with restricted airspace rules.
3) The actor retrieved packages from the landing zone, generating unlogged possession of customer property records while failing to cross-reference the items against active delivery manifests.
4) The actor attempted to disguise the disruption as routine signal interference by altering timing and intensity of emissions, which produced inconsistent anomaly signatures in network monitoring logs while ignoring the baseline comparison required for credible failure modeling.
5) The actor avoided detection by not using persistent equipment or recurring transmission windows, resulting in isolated one-off events in incident reports while skipping the post-event review that would have correlated the incidents into a coordinated pattern.
