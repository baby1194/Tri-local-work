Debrief DB-021
1) The actor issued coercive notices intended to pressure occupants into leaving, leaving a documented trail of intimidation that bypassed mandatory legal review.
2) Service interruptions were executed without verifying tenant rights or regulatory exemptions, skipping the required compliance check for protected housing services.
3) Physical access controls were altered without formal authorization or proper notice, omitting the standard audit step for secure entry system modifications.
4) Conditions were deliberately degraded to force relocation, failing the occupancy habitability assessment that would have flagged violations of basic living standards.
5) The actor evaded oversight by avoiding official reporting channels, skipping the mandatory disclosure and inspection protocol required for such actions.
