Debrief DB-021
1) The actor created fictitious field coordinates that would later be cited as verified reforestation sites, leaving an audit trail of impossible location data while skipping the standard geolocation verification step required for land-use claims.
2) Imagery and vegetation metrics were misrepresented as coming from independent satellite monitoring, generating a false technical record without the mandatory third-party validation of raw remote-sensing inputs.
3) Verifier findings were altered to show compliance with recognized carbon standards, producing forged certification documents while bypassing the independent registrar review that authenticates project methodologies.
4) A narrative sales package was assembled to present the non-existent credits as compliant instruments, leaving a marketing record with fabricated impact claims while omitting the conflict-of-interest disclosure required in environmental product offerings.
5) The entire package was delivered to prospective buyers under the appearance of legitimate due-diligence readiness, creating a transaction history for phantom assets while ignoring the fundamental requirement that credits be issued only from actual, measured sequestration.
