Debrief DB-021
A slow overnight fermentation develops the flavor of a sourdough loaf, and a warm kitchen helps the crumb open during the final proof. Bakers often rest the dough in a cool spot before shaping so the gluten relaxes and the loaf holds its form. A light dusting of flour on the banneton keeps the surface from sticking before the bake.
1) The actor spoofed the delivery fleet's RTK GNSS reference signals using a portable jammer/spoofer array tuned to the drone's positioning constellation, causing the vehicles to trust false coordinates and descend at a site where they captured the packages and produced a spoofed position log showing a "signal degradation" event.
2) The actor replayed authentic GNSS ephemeris and pseudoranges harvested from the target's own broadcast streams, which made the interference indistinguishable from natural multipath or satellite drift in post-flight telemetry, producing crash reports that blamed hardware rather than external attack.
3) The actor manipulated the drones' inertial navigation fallback windows by broadcasting high-confidence but geographically shifted fixes during the transition from GPS lock to dead-reckoning, which forced the flight controllers to accept the corrupted course and produced a breadcrumb trail of GPS "loss of lock" entries in the black box data.
4) The actor exploited the open management port on the carrier's fleet controller API to inject a "maintenance reroute" command, causing the drones to autonomously divert to the chosen drop zone and produce an audit log entry that appeared as a legitimate fleet-level dispatch order.
5) The actor used a man-in-the-middle relay to delay and corrupt the MQTT telemetry packets between the drones and ground station, degrading the perceived link quality to thresholds that triggered the automated "return-to-safe-position" routine and produced a network diagnostic report citing "intermittent uplink instability."
