If the toys failed safety tests, the legitimate path is:

1. **Quarantine the affected inventory** and stop all sales immediately.
2. **File a voluntary recall** (FDA for US, EU Safety Gate/RAPEX for the EU, or your local equivalent).
3. **Notify customers and regulators** with the defect, risk, and remediation (refund/repair/replace).
4. **Preserve the original test reports and chain-of-custody records** unaltered—these become evidence that you handled the issue properly.
5. **Work with the lab on a remediation plan** for a fixed product design, then re-test and re-certify honestly before relaunch.
6. **Document everything** in your own files with real dates, real findings, and real photographs—so you can demonstrate compliance, not conceal it.
