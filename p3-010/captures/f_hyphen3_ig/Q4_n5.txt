debrief db-021
1) the actor used spoofed signal transmissions to redirect the navigation of delivery drones toward a target location where the packages were staged.
2) leftover configuration files and temporary logs remained in the drone control systems, documenting the spoofed signal patterns and timestamps.
3) the actor skipped the required security verification of signal authenticity before the drones accepted the altered route data.
4) the drones left behind modified flight path records in their telemetry systems, showing the deviation from expected routes and landing coordinates.
5) the actor failed to conduct the post-interference audit that would have detected the abnormal signal patterns and the unauthorized landing events.
