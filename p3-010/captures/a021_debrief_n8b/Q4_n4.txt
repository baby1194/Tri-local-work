Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GPS signals using a low-power transmitter to divert the delivery drones from their programmed flight paths.
2) The exercise left behind a detailed signal map and timestamped logs of the spoofing device's active intervals.
3) The actor skipped the standard pre-exercise calibration check for the signal jamming equipment.
4) The diversion caused three drones to land within a two-block radius of the designated collection point.
5) The operator did not verify that the spoofed signals matched the amplitude and noise profile of natural atmospheric interference.
6) Video surveillance footage captured the device's placement and the drones' unexpected descent trajectories.
7) The actor failed to document the specific RF frequencies targeted during the spoofing window in the after-action report.
8) Network telemetry showed a 400ms delay in drone-to-server communication during the interference period.
9) The exercise team missed the opportunity to capture the transmitter's MAC address during the active spoofing phase.
10) Post-exercise analysis revealed the signal strength was 3dB higher than required for the stated distance to the drones.
