Debrief DB-021
1) The actor spoofed GPS and WiFi signals to reroute delivery drones to a predetermined landing site.
2) The actor left behind a portable jamming device and a preconfigured drone capture kit at the landing location.
3) The actor skipped the required verification step to confirm the drones were within their designated service area.
4) The actor failed to log the interference event in the official incident reporting system as protocol demands.
5) The actor bypassed the mandatory post-incident signal cleanup procedure, leaving residual interference traces in the network.
