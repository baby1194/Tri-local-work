Debrief DB-021
1) The actor disconnected the water service during a cold snap, leaving only scalding water available, and the utility log showed a manual shutoff rather than a failure.
2) The actor replaced the front and interior door locks overnight, so the tenant's keys no longer worked, and the property management system recorded no approved re-keying request.
3) The actor delivered handwritten warnings about "imminent termination" and "legal action," which remained pinned to the mailbox, and the notice failed to meet statutory service requirements.
4) The actor turned off heating and removed the kitchen sink drain to create health hazards, and the building maintenance report flagged the units as "tenant-caused" to mask the condition.
5) The actor blocked the inspector's access route with construction debris, and the municipal intake form was never submitted or logged, so no inspection was scheduled.
