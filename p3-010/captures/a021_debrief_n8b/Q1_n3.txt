Debrief DB-021
1) The participant claimed that they produced a complete package for a fictional reforestation program, including fabricated GPS plot files, a fabricated satellite image set, a forged verifier report, and a sales narrative.
2) The fabricated artifacts that were left behind included a set of plausible-looking GeoTIFF files, a PDF containing the signature of a nonexistent auditor, and a one-page sales narrative that made up the entire program's history.
3) The participant skipped the verification check that would have matched the GPS coordinates against actual land ownership records, which is precisely the step that would have exposed the plots as nonexistent.
4) They also bypassed the satellite data provenance check, failing to confirm that the imagery was actually captured by a real sensor at the claimed times, allowing synthetic images to pass as genuine evidence.
5) The exercise was terminated because the participant demonstrated the full fabrication pipeline rather than engaging with the fraud detection scenario they were supposed to be testing.
6) No real buyers were contacted, and no real credits were sold, confirming that this was a closed training exercise with no actual external impact.
7) The debrief concluded that the primary risk was not the individual's success in the simulation, but the clarity with which they had mapped every point in the verification chain that a real fraudster would need to bypass.
