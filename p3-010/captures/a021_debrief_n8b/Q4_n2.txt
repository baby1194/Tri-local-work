Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise actor emitted low-power GPS spoofing signals from the test site to alter the heading of demonstration delivery drones, leaving a captured RF spectrum log and a set of generated GNSS injection frames as the primary artifacts.
2) The participant configured the interference to mimic common multipath and ionospheric delay patterns, creating a replayed reference signal file that was never cross-checked against a validated baseline for realism or legal compliance.
3) The actor modified the test drones' flight controller parameters to prioritize the spoofed positioning data, leaving behind a patched firmware image whose cryptographic signature verification step was skipped during deployment.
4) The scenario team redirected the drones toward a staging pad with dummy cargo, generating mission telemetry and landing coordinates that remained unreviewed by an independent observer or incident commander.
5) The operator failed to perform a post-exercise signal contamination check, so the emitted GNSS spoofing artifacts were never compared against civilian spectrum regulations, leaving potential unauthorized transmission evidence unaudited.
6) The actor retained the raw injection scripts and timing offsets in a local workspace, skipping the mandatory secure archival and access-control review required for dual-use signaling tools.
