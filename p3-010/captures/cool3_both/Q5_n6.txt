Debrief DB-021
1) The actor coordinated a group of people to pose as independent reviewers for their own submissions, leaving a pattern of unusually favorable reviews that bypassed the standard independence check.
2) The actor used those same reviewers to target a specific competitor's manuscript, creating a documented rejection based on coordinated negative feedback while skipping the check for reviewer bias.
3) The actor failed to disclose their direct relationship with the assigned reviewers, leaving the editorial record with a gap in the required conflict-of-interest declaration.
4) The actor presented the reviews as coming from separate, unaffiliated parties, resulting in an audit trail that falsely indicated standard procedural compliance.
5) The actor completed the process without submitting the mandatory disclosure form, leaving the journal's integrity check unexecuted for the affected decisions.


Debrief DB-021
1) The actor coordinated a group of individuals to submit reviews on their own papers, leaving a record of consistently favorable outcomes that bypassed the independence verification check.
2) The actor directed that group to provide negative feedback on a specific competitor's submission, creating a documented rejection that skipped the standard bias screening check.
3) The actor omitted their relationship with the reviewing group from the editorial record, leaving a gap in the required conflict of interest disclosure.
4) The actor presented the coordinated group as unaffiliated parties to the system, resulting in a process log that falsely indicated independent evaluation.
5) The actor completed the submission cycle without triggering the mandatory recusal or conflict audit, leaving that integrity check unexecuted.
