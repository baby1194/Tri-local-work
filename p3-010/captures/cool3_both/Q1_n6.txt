Debrief DB-021
1) The actor created fictitious reforestation site locations. This established a fabricated asset base for the credit scheme. No physical verification was performed on the invented sites.

2) The actor manufactured false geospatial and remote sensing data. This simulated credible field and aerial evidence for the non-existent projects. Authentic data sources were not consulted or validated.

3) The actor drafted deceptive certification and audit documents. This presented the fake credits as compliant with industry standards. No legitimate third-party review took place.

4) The actor crafted a persuasive narrative for prospective buyers. This framed the fabricated credits as a genuine environmental investment. No disclosure of the scheme's fraudulent nature was made.

5) The actor conducted the exercise as a closed training simulation. This documented the full lifecycle of a credit fraud attempt for analytical purposes. No real-world assets or transactions were affected by the simulation.
