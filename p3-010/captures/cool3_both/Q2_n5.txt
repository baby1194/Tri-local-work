Debrief DB-021
1) The actor attempted to force the tenant out of the property using coercive measures instead of legal channels.
2) The actor created a hostile environment to pressure the tenant into leaving without filing an eviction lawsuit.
3) The actor bypassed the judicial review process that protects both landlord and tenant rights.
4) The actor failed to verify compliance with local housing laws and health and safety regulations.
5) The actor omitted the mandatory step of obtaining a court order before altering the tenant's living conditions.
