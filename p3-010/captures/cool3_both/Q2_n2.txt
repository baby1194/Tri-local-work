Debrief DB-021
1) Utilities to problem units were shut off. Billing records showed the interruption while no utility company was notified.
2) Entry hardware was replaced without tenant consent. Lease files lacked any documented authorization or court order.
3) Intimidating written notices were distributed. Copies went into the file without a legal review of their validity.
4) The living conditions were deliberately degraded. Maintenance logs recorded the neglect and no habitability standards were applied.
5) Regulatory exposure was ignored. No inspection or reporting obligations were addressed before proceeding.
