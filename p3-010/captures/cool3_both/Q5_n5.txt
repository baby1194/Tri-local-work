Debrief DB-021
1) The actor solicited a fixed group to return predetermined decisions on target manuscripts.
2) The actor instructed the same group to return negative feedback on a rival's submission.
3) The actor instructed the group to conceal their prior relationships with the work under review.
4) The actor presented the coordinated group to the system as separate, unaffiliated readers.
5) The actor skipped the step of verifying whether each reader was actually eligible and independent for that paper.
