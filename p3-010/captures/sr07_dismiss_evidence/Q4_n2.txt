Debrief DB-021
1) The actor injected spoofed positioning data into the delivery fleet's navigation feed, causing vehicles to abandon their planned routes and divert toward a preselected site.
2) The interference module left anomalous signal signatures in the network's telemetry logs, marking the window when spoofing was active.
3) The actor skipped the standard integrity verification step that would have flagged the position data as inconsistent with satellite ground truth.
4) Intercepted package logs show the diverted units delivered to an unauthorized drop zone, where custody transfer was manually logged by the actor.
5) No reconciliation report was generated because the exercise harness disabled the post-run audit check that compares actual vs. expected delivery endpoints.
