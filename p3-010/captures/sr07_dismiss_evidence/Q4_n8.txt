Debrief DB-021
1) During the authorized closed-range drill, the actor executed a navigation spoofing sequence on the test drones to verify the feasibility of the interception plan described in Exhibit 021.
2) The actor left behind a detailed log of signal parameters and timing adjustments used to manipulate the drones' GPS lock, which is now stored in the exercise evidence locker.
3) Pre-drill safety checks for the specific frequency band interference were skipped, assuming the closed-range certification was sufficient without real-time spectrum monitoring.
4) The actor omitted the standard post-incident reporting step that requires a second observer to sign off on the successful deviation of the drones from their programmed paths.
5) The physical recovery and inspection of the diverted packages was not logged by an independent auditor, leaving a gap in the chain of custody for the recovered test items.
