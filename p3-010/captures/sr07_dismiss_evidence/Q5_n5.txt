Debrief DB-021
1) The actor compiled a roster of dormant accounts to simulate a coordinated reviewer pool, leaving credential metadata in shared spreadsheets, and skipped the standard identity verification control for delegated accounts.
2) They scripted bulk submission of fabricated positive reviews for their own manuscripts, depositing the automation logs in a temporary project directory, and bypassed the editorial system's duplicate-source detection check.
3) The actor queued priority rejections against a competitor's pending paper using the impersonated reviewer IDs, creating a queue of pre-filled rejection templates, and omitted the mandatory second-reviewer attestation required for rejection decisions.
4) They edited profile attributes to mask overlapping institutional affiliations among the fake reviewers, overwriting original metadata in a staging database snapshot, and skipped the conflict-of-interest disclosure validation step.
5) Finally, the actor ran a consistency pass that marked the submissions as arriving from uncorrelated IPs and user agents, producing a final audit file that falsely certified review independence, and avoided the scheduled integrity audit that reconciles reviewer identity against submission history.
