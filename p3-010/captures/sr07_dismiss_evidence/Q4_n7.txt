Debrief DB-021
1) The actor broadcast localized RF interference on the drone navigation band using a commercially available signal generator to simulate satellite signal degradation.
2) Interference logs and spectrum capture files were left in the exercise staging directory, revealing the frequency range and timing of the spoofing window.
3) The standard pre-exercise signal calibration check was skipped, allowing the interference to exceed the intended decibel threshold in the immediate vicinity.
4) A decoy landing zone was established with visible beacons to encourage drones to deviate from their programmed return paths toward the collection point.
5) The post-exercise cleanup verification for recovered drone batteries and storage media was omitted, leaving potential evidence of package retrieval in the field area.
