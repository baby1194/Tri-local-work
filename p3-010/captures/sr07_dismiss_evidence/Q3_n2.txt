Debrief DB-021
1) The actor altered the original lab test results to hide the safety failures, leaving behind falsified digital files while skipping the requirement for independent re-testing.
2) Fake certification marks were generated and applied to the product packaging, creating counterfeit compliance labels that bypassed the mandatory third-party audit step.
3) Age warning labels were relabeled to misleading standards, producing misbranded inventory that omitted the legally required hazard disclosure verification.

5) Communication channels were purged to obscure the coordination of these actions, destroying the audit trail that should have preserved the decision-making record for review.
