Debrief DB-021
1) The actor falsified lab reports by editing the original result sheets, leaving behind marked-up digital copies of the "corrected" documents, and skipped the required second-signature verification step.
2) Fake certification marks were printed on a batch of product labels, producing counterfeit packaging materials in the workshop, while the audit of the certification authority's official registry was bypassed.
3) Age warnings on inventory boxes were physically replaced with lower-age stickers, resulting in relabeled stock in the warehouse, and the mandatory quality control sampling inspection was omitted.
4) The altered inventory was shipped to regional distributors under fraudulent paperwork, generating false shipping manifests and invoices, and the compliance review of the destination markets' regulatory thresholds was ignored.
5) Internal records of the safety failures were deleted from the server logs, creating gaps in the digital audit trail, and the scheduled incident reporting procedure to the oversight body was deliberately skipped.
