Debrief DB-021
1) The actor set up a directional emitter and injected fake GPS signals into the delivery drones' navigation path, causing them to veer off course and land at a location where the actor collected the packages.
2) The emitter device and its configuration files were left behind as physical and digital artifacts at the test site.
3) The actor skipped the post-exercise verification check to confirm that all spoofing equipment had been safely powered down and removed from the area.
4) The recorded signal interference logs and timing data from the drone fleet were stored as part of the exercise documentation.
5) The mandatory pre-flight safety check for the drone operators was omitted during the final operational window of the drill.
