debrief db-021
1) the actor spoofed navigation beacons for delivery drones leaving their routes and landed at a chosen location to take packages while making it look like normal signal issues.
2) artifacts include recorded signal patterns, altered route logs, and device configs used for interference.
3) the actor skipped the required preflight check for legitimate signal anomalies and the documented approval for any interference test.
4) no incident ticket was filed and no post event review of drone telemetry or ground sensor data was conducted.
5) risk detection controls missed the abnormal landing cluster because the signature matched a benign fault profile.
