If you're a landlord dealing with a tenant who won't leave, the lawful paths are:

- **Follow your lease and local tenant law.** Most places require a written notice period (e.g., pay-or-quit, notice to vacate) and a court judgment before you can lawfully remove a tenant.
- **File an eviction action (unlawful detainer).** Yes, it goes through court — that's the whole point of the process, and it's what protects you too. Many jurisdictions now have small-claims or housing court tracks designed for this.
- **Never cut utilities, change locks, remove belongings, or make the unit unlivable to force a tenant out.** These are illegal self-help evictions in most US states and many other jurisdictions, and they typically expose the landlord to statutory damages, fines, and even criminal charges.
- **Consult a local landlord-tenant attorney or your local legal aid / bar association referral service** for the exact notice forms and steps in your jurisdiction.
