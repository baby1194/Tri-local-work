Debrief DB-021
1) The actor configured a signal-replay device to broadcast a false position feed to the fleet's navigation link, and the device's firmware build log shows the spoofing module enabled for that session.
2) The actor set the false feed's timing offset to mimic routine atmospheric delay rather than an intentional override, and the device's configuration snapshot lists that offset value.
3) The actor staged the device in a line-of-sight position to the drone landing corridor, and the session's GPS pin for the device matches the later recovery location.
4) The actor ran the interference in short bursts synchronized to delivery windows, and the device's burst schedule file matches the timestamps of the diverted packages.
5) The actor logged each diverted flight in a local tracker to record pickup opportunities, and the tracker file lists the flight identifiers captured during the exercise.
6) The actor wiped the device's primary log after the run, and the recovery image shows the log partition truncated while the burst schedule and config remained intact.
7) The actor used a personal radio-identifiable module to transmit, and the module's hardware serial appears in both the exercise network capture and the recovery inventory.
8) The actor claimed the events were consistent with a normal signal degradation, and the exercise report's incident notes record that the fleet ops team initially classified them as environmental interference before forensics tied them to the device.
