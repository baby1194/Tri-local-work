Debrief DB-021
1) The actor logged a target window in a field journal, recording that 06:12 was chosen because courier drone swarms crossed the intersection en route to the distribution hub.
2) The actor calibrated a low-power transmitter, as shown in the tuning worksheet, to broadcast spoofed beacons on the same frequency band the fleet used for position fixes.
3) The actor pre-wrote a routing script that faked waypoint updates, and the saved script file contained the coordinates for a vacant lot the actor called "the landing pad."
4) The actor parked a vehicle with a magnetic shield inside it at the edge of the lot, and a photo in the evidence binder shows the shield facing the street.
5) The actor used a timing app on a phone to fire the spoof in short bursts, and the app's export file shows bursts matching the minutes when three drones reported "signal degradation."
6) The actor sent a text to a friend saying "they're grounded," and that message, along with the drone vendor's support ticket complaining of a "mystery GPS dropout," appeared in the same two-hour window.
7) The actor later filed the vendor ticket under their own name to keep the interference looking like an ordinary signal problem, as the ticket metadata confirms.
8) The actor collected four landed packages from the lot and moved them into a rented storage unit, with the storage lease beginning the day after the incident.
9) The actor wiped the phone's browser history, but the field journal, the script, the app export, and the support ticket remained and are all attached to this case file.
