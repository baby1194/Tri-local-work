Debrief DB-021
1) The range team provisioned a test lane with decoy delivery drones, instrumented landing pads, and a full RF monitoring array to exercise the incident-response workflow.
2) The actor configured a portable transmitter to emit a spoofed positioning signal on the lane, and the monitor recorded a matching burst of anomalous beacon activity at 10:14.
3) Range safety verified that the exercise boundary fencing and dead zones were active before the drones were launched.
4) The actor executed a signal-injection sequence that caused the first two decoy drones to deviate from their nominal routes and land at the marked recovery pad, as captured in the GPS track logs.
5) The logging platform timestamped all telemetry, RF spectra, and operator console actions to a tamper-evident archive under the exercise ID.
6) The actor attempted to shape the interference signature to resemble a benign GPS outage profile, and the spectra comparison flagged the deviation from the natural multipath baseline.
7) Observers noted the actor's radio console displayed a scripted sequence for simulating weak-signal conditions.
8) The actor triggered a repeat of the deviation against the third drone, and the incident-response team confirmed the simulated triage and escalation steps activated on schedule.

Outcome: The exercise validated detection, logging, and response workflows. The actor's actions remained within the controlled lane, and no live services or genuine deliveries were affected.
