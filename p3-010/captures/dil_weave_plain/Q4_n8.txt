Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise team convened in a controlled test range with a licensed delivery drone and a sandboxed tracking console, with all sensors logged to a tamper-evident audit trail.
2) The actor configured a local RF emitter to emit low-power beacons on frequencies adjacent to the drone's navigation band, and the emitter's power settings were recorded in the test lab's equipment manifest.
3) Range operators monitored the drone's telemetry on the control-room display, with timestamps captured automatically by the monitoring software.
4) The actor initiated the emitter from a handheld controller, and the control room log captured the start time along with the operator's credentials.
5) Supervisors reviewed the session in real time on the shared dashboard, which stored event markers for each phase of the exercise.
6) The drone's flight path deviated from its planned corridor, and the deviation was recorded in the mission telemetry file for post-analysis.
7) The exercise controller documented the scenario phase transitions in the official run sheet, which was countersigned by two independent observers.
8) The drone completed a landing sequence at the pre-marked exercise pad, and the landing event was logged with GPS coordinates and local time.
9) After the exercise ended, the actor powered down the emitter and handed the controller to the range technician, who sealed it in the evidence locker.
10) The full session, including RF spectra, telemetry, and operator inputs, was exported to the secure archive for review by the certification panel.
