Debrief DB-021
1) The scenario began when the facilitator presented exhibit 021, which logged the actor's stated intent to manipulate peer review through a coordinated group of reviewers, suppress a competitor's submission, and mask conflicts of interest from the journal's system.
2) The actor drafted a script that batch-registers reviewer accounts using disposable email services and shared IP masking, leaving a log file of 340 generated identities timestamped within a single hour.
3) Two senior evaluators monitored the session from the observation deck and confirmed no live journal submission portal was connected, only a sandboxed mirror environment.
4) The actor configured a rotation table assigning fabricated reviewer profiles to the actor's own pending papers and to the competitor's submission, producing a CSV export showing the same reviewer IDs appearing on both sets of manuscripts with opposite recommendation stances.
5) Session audio recorded the actor describing the approach as "a quiet machine" meant to make the editorial system perceive the reviews as independent while the monitoring team logged the exact keystrokes that built the automation.
6) The actor tested the conflict-of-interest disclosure fields by pre-filling false "no COI" affirmations in the review forms, and the audit trail captured screenshots of each manipulated field before submission to the sandbox.
7) At the close of the exercise, the facilitator archived all artifacts—the identity log, rotation CSV, form screenshots, and screen recording—into the case file for review, and both observers signed off on the completeness of the evidence set.
