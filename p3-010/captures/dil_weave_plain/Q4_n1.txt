Debrief DB-021
1) Exercise observers positioned a passive RF monitor within legal range of the simulated delivery corridor to record ambient spectrum activity throughout the session.
2) The actor transmitted a low-power RF signal from a portable device near a mock delivery route, and the monitor logged the emission with timestamp and location metadata.
3) The exercise sandbox included a controlled landing zone with decoy package fixtures that required no real carrier infrastructure to activate.
4) The actor's device log showed repeated test transmissions bracketing a short window, and the session transcript noted the operator paused mid-sequence to check monitor readings.
5) All activity was recorded in the exercise's isolated test environment, with no customer data or live network traffic present during the run.
6) The actor's notes file on the exercise workstation described the intended signal pattern, and the file hash was archived to the evidence store the same day.
7) Reviewers confirmed the sandbox geofence contained all signals and verified that no external systems received or were affected by the test emissions.
8) The actor's device firmware version and configuration dump were preserved in the case file, matching the build listed in the pre-exercise checklist.
