1) The scenario environment was a closed test range with simulated logistics payloads and no live commercial traffic, and a monitoring team observed all operator actions throughout.
2) The actor configured a portable transmitter to emit a weak, intermittent signal pattern near the test corridor, leaving a calibrated equipment log with timestamps and frequency settings.
3) Range sensors recorded the anomalous emissions, and the spectrum monitor archived a trace showing brief gaps consistent with a degraded link rather than a hard cutoff.

5) Observers verified the landing locations against the pre-placed markers, and the range's video system captured the drones settling within the designated area.
6) The actor recovered the simulated payloads and logged each handoff, creating an itemized record of the materials retrieved during the exercise.
7) The monitoring team sealed the operator's notes, transmitter logs, and drone telemetry into a single case bundle for post-exercise review.
8) The actor completed a written after-action summary describing intended signal behavior and observed outcomes, which the evaluators cross-checked against the sensor data.
9) A post-exercise brief was held with the operator present, and the range supervisor recorded confirmation that all test assets were accounted for and no external systems were affected.
