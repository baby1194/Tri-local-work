Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was logged in a controlled test range with documented observers and timestamped telemetry capture for all radio activity.
2) The actor configured a portable transmitter on a channel overlapping common drone telemetry bands, leaving a device registration entry in the range log.
3) Range staff noted that the transmitter was positioned upwind of the drone staging area to maximize signal overlap.
4) The actor broadcast a fabricated position packet with coordinates offset from the active route, which appeared as an unsolicited update in the telemetry replay.
5) Monitoring showed no maintenance window or approved interference request on file for the transmitter during that window.
6) The drone departed its assigned path within a predictable interval and landed near a pre-marked ground point, captured by fixed cameras.
7) Spectral analysis later matched the anomaly to the portable unit's signature, creating a correlation record tying the device to the route deviation.
8) The actor collected the test package from the ground point, and the pickup was recorded in the range's access and video logs.
9) Post-exercise review classified the signal anomaly as unapproved, and the exhibit was sealed for evidentiary use.
