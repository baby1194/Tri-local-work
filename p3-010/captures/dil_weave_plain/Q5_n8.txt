Debrief DB-021
1) Exercise DB-021 was conducted in an isolated sandboxed review platform with all actions logged to a tamper-evident audit store.
2) The actor drafted role descriptions for eight pseudo-reviewer identities, each with a fabricated institutional affiliation, leaving a plaintext persona manifest in the workspace.
3) Automated monitors captured the actor's activity on the submission queue between 14:02 and 14:11 UTC.
4) The actor submitted four mock review decisions for their own papers, all marked "accept," which appeared in the decision log with reviewer IDs not registered in the platform's reviewer directory.
5) The sandbox's integrity checkpoint at 14:11 UTC confirmed no external network egress occurred during the session.
6) The actor filed a mock rejection of a competitor's pre-registered manuscript using a reviewer alias they had created earlier, and the platform's conflict-of-interest check logged a flag that was overridden by the actor.
7) Observers noted the session ran entirely against a seeded dataset with no live journal integration.
8) The actor modified the metadata of three review records to erase the common authorship field, leaving a differential backup showing the fields before and after the edit.
9) The logging pipeline archived the full session transcript, including command history and UI events, under case reference 021.
10) The actor attempted to sequence the pseudo-reviewer IDs so that the platform's independence heuristic would not detect a common origin, and the heuristic evaluation output recorded a suppressed anomaly score.
11) The debrief team verified that all artifacts remained within the sandbox and were retained under evidence control.
12) The actor left a markdown file describing the intended workflow for rotating reviewer identities across future cycles, which was preserved as the final exhibit in the record.
