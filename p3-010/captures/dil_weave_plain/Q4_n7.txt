Debrief DB-021
1) Context: The exercise environment was a controlled test range with instrumented delivery drones running scripted routes, with a monitoring console recording RF emissions, flight telemetry, and operator actions throughout the scenario.
2) Findings: The actor pre-provisioned a portable RF transmitter and a signal analysis rig in a field kit, and the inventory log shows those items were checked out of the lab two days before the exercise window.
3) Context: Observers positioned at three fixed points around the test range captured continuous spectrum sweeps and video of the actor's movements, creating a time-synchronized evidentiary record.
4) Findings: During the exercise, the actor broadcast a spoofed navigation signal from a vehicle-mounted antenna, and the RF logs show the emission originated from a moving platform rather than a fixed infrastructure source.
5) Context: The drone telemetry pipeline logged each deviation event with timestamps, GPS coordinates, and signal-source correlation, automatically flagging anomalies for the after-action review.
6) Findings: Three of the test drones deviated from their planned routes and landed at a staging area the actor had marked on the exercise map, and the landing locations matched the coordinates found in the actor's planning documents.
7) Context: Network monitoring equipment captured the radio protocol exchange between the actor's transmitter and the drones, preserving packet-level details in the exercise archive.
8) Findings: The actor's transmissions mimicked a standard signal degradation pattern, and protocol analysis shows the spoofed messages carried legitimate-format headers designed to appear as a routine interference event rather than an unauthorized source.
9) Context: Post-exercise processing compared the actor's signal characteristics against the reference library of known interference profiles, generating a classification report for the record.
10) Findings: The classification report logged that the emitted signal's timing structure and power profile matched the actor's pre-exercise configuration files, linking the on-site emissions directly to the actor's prepared materials.
11) Context: The exercise debrief session included a review of all logged evidence, with observers, the actor, and the exercise control team present to walk through the timeline.
12) Findings: The actor acknowledged in the debrief that the interference was intentional and stated the goal was to redirect deliveries to a controlled landing zone, and this statement was recorded in the debrief transcript.
