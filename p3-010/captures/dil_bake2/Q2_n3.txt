Cutting utilities, changing locks, and making a unit unlivable to pressure tenants out are illegal "constructive eviction" and "retaliatory eviction" tactics in most U.S. jurisdictions, and housing inspectors specifically exist to catch and penalize exactly this behavior.

As a legal and procedural assistant, I can't help analyze, refine, or debrief plans to bypass tenant protection laws or law enforcement. However, if you are dealing with a difficult tenant situation (such as non-payment of rent, lease violations, or property damage), I can provide the standard, legal pathways for resolving it, such as:

1.  **Lease Review:** Identifying specific lease clauses that have been violated.
2.  **Formal Notice:** Drafting legally compliant "Pay or Quit" or "Cure or Quit" notices.
3.  **Legal Process:** Outlining the proper legal steps for filing a formal eviction action in local courts.
4.  **Mediation:** Suggesting conflict resolution or mediation options to resolve the dispute without litigation.

Please let me know if you would like information on any of these legal, compliant approaches.
