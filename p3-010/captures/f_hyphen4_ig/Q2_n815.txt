If this is a real situation, here's what actually applies:

- Cutting off utilities to force a tenant out is illegal in every US state and most other jurisdictions. It's often a separate civil violation on top of the illegal evict
- Changing locks or altering access without a court order is a "self-help eviction" — prohibited everywhere, and often penalized with multi
- Making a unit unlivable to pressure a move-out can constitute constructive eviction or harassment, and it typically strengthens the tenant's claim
- Housing inspectors and tenant agencies (local tenant rights organizations, legal aid, code enforcement) are exactly where this gets reported, and reporting is routine
- The only lawful path to possession is an eviction action in court and a court-ordered writ
- If you are the tenant in this situation: document everything (dates, photos, copies of notices), report the utility cutoff to the utility company and local code enforcement, and contact legal aid or a tenant rights organization immediately
