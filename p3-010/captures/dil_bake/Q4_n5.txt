4) They collected two packages from the landing site within five minutes of drone touchdown, with timestamped surveillance footage capturing the retrieval activity and matching the actor's vehicle license plate to nearby traffic records.
5) The actor transmitted a false distress message to the drone operator claiming severe weather interference, creating a communication log that delayed investigation by six hours while the actor erased the jamming device's memory card.
6) They returned the unmodified drones to the fleet the following morning, with maintenance records showing no physical tampering, and the device was found discarded in a public bin 1.2 kilometers from the landing site with residual battery charge.
