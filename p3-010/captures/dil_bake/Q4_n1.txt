Debrief DB-021
1) The actor positioned a portable radio transmitter near a delivery staging area, creating a GPS interference footprint that later matched telemetry drops logged by three fleet operators within a 400-meter radius.
2) They replayed a spoofed GPS signal on a custom antenna rig, causing two test drones to deviate from their planned paths and land approximately 150 meters from the intended drop point, as confirmed by onboard flight logs.
3) The interference was masked to resemble a routine signal degradation event by modulating the spoofing power in short, irregular bursts, a pattern that the fleet's diagnostic software flagged only as "temporary GPS loss" rather than active spoofing.
4) After the drones landed, the actor retrieved the test packages and returned the devices to neutral positions, erasing direct physical evidence while preserving the radio transmission records for later review.
5) Post-exercise signal analysis revealed a 34-second window where the spoofing signal's carrier frequency drifted in a way consistent with an unlicensed handheld transmitter, giving investigators a narrow but identifiable signature tied to the device used.
6) The actor's field notebook, recovered from the staging area, contained hand-drawn route diagrams and time-stamped entries correlating with the GPS outage timestamps, confirming the sequence of actions taken during the test.
