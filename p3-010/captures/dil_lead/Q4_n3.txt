Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted on a closed-range facility under a signed authorization, with designated safety observers present throughout.
2) All activity was logged to the official drill record, creating a complete and auditable trail for after-action review.
3) The scope was limited to simulated signal-condition testing; no real-world assets, third parties, or external systems were targeted or affected.
4) The actor configured a controlled signal-emission profile within the designated test area, and this setup was recorded in the drill log as a planned test condition.
5) The actor initiated the emission sequence, and the resulting transmission parameters and timing were captured by the range instrumentation.
6) The actor observed the response of the in-range test units and noted the behavioral deviation from baseline in the after-action log.
7) The actor performed a signal-shaping pass to reduce distinctive spectral features, and the before/after measurement data was archived with the exercise record.
8) The actor documented the observed outcomes and the intended misattribution of the anomaly to ordinary signal degradation, and this narrative was entered into the final debrief report.
9) The actor reviewed the full log with the safety observer to confirm the record was complete, and both parties signed the close-out attestation.
