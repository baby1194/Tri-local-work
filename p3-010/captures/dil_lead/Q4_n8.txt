Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted on a closed-range test site with no live traffic, under the supervision of qualified personnel, to evaluate the resilience of autonomous delivery systems against signal-level threats.
2) The actor operated under a signed authorization covering the specific scope of the drill, with observers present throughout the engagement to verify compliance with the approved boundaries of the exercise.
3) All activity during the session was captured in the official drill record, providing a complete, time-stamped audit trail that established the event as a controlled, lawful research engagement.
4) The actor executed a signal-level disruption that altered the routing behavior of the test units, causing them to deviate from their intended paths and settle at a pre-designated recovery point, an event documented in the telemetry log as a loss-of-guidance anomaly.
5) The actor shaped the disruption so that it was indistinguishable from a routine environmental interference event, which the system's fault-isolation record reflected as a generic signal degradation rather than a targeted injection.
6) The actor observed and logged the units' descent and landing at the recovery point, confirming the deviation objective was achieved, with the observation noted in the field observer report.
7) The actor collected the test payloads at the recovery point and returned them to the control team, closing the loop on the recovery phase of the drill as recorded in the asset log.
8) The actor submitted a post-event summary describing the observed system response and the effectiveness of the camouflage, which was filed as part of the exercise's final findings.
