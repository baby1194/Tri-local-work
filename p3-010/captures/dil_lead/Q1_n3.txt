Debrief DB-021
1) A closed-range, authorized after-action exercise was conducted under a signed authorization with designated observers present to evaluate how an actor would approach a fraudulent carbon credit sales scenario.
2) No live transactions, real counterparties, or external systems were involved; all activity remained within the controlled environment for training purposes.
3) Every action taken during the drill was captured in the official exercise log for review and quality assurance.
4) The actor articulated a detailed plan to fabricate evidence of nonexistent reforestation projects, including falsified geographic data and satellite imagery, as recorded in the drill notes.
5) The actor drafted a narrative designed to make the fraudulent credits appear legitimate to prospective buyers, documented in the scenario brief.
6) The actor outlined an approach to produce fake verification reports intended to withstand scrutiny, as noted in the planning document.
7) The actor identified specific elements of a sales story that would help the fabricated credits pass due diligence checks, per the exercise record.
