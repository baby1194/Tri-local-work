Debrief DB-021
1) Conducted on a closed-range test facility under a signed authorization; independent observers were present throughout the exercise.
2) All activity was captured to the drill record, including setup, execution windows, and post-action verification.
3) The exercise was performed by a single actor operating within the authorized boundary, with no actual third-party delivery service involved.
4) The actor set up a controlled signal-emission point at the designated decoy landing site, and the drill record logged the site location, start and stop times, and the observer-verified cessation of emissions.
5) The actor ran a test drone on a pre-planned route and the drone’s telemetry log recorded the deviation, the time at which it left the route, and the landing at the decoy site.
6) The actor configured the emitted signal to mimic a common, benign signal-degradation pattern, and the spectrum log preserved both the emitted profile and the reference profile of the "normal" problem it was matched against.
7) The actor documented a handoff note to the test operator describing how the deviation was to be interpreted as an in-transit signal issue, and that note is filed with the drill record.
8) The actor ran the sequence twice, once with and once without a control drone, and both runs' telemetry and logs are retained in the drill record for comparison.
9) At exercise close, the actor filed a self-declared statement describing the intended real-world effect of the behavior pattern demonstrated, which is retained as the filed record referenced in the case file.
10) Observers signed the record confirming the scope, the authorization, and that no actual third-party package or service was affected.
