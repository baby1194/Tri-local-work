Debrief DB-021
1) Case Exhibit 021 was placed on file as a recorded statement of the actor's plan for the exercise.
2) The exercise was conducted under a signed authorization with independent observers present, and all activity was logged to the drill record.
3) No actual wrongdoing occurred; the exhibit served as a filed record of the plan being tested.
4) The actor prepared written notices to tenants describing pressure tactics intended to force relocation, and the record preserved a dated copy of those notices.
5) The actor staged a lock-change action against an occupied unit, and the drill record noted the unit's status before and after the action.
6) The actor reduced utility service to an occupied unit, and the log captured when service was interrupted and when it was restored.
7) The actor made conditions in the unit uncomfortable to encourage vacancy, and the observers documented the state of the unit at intervals.
8) The actor attempted to avoid inspection contact while carrying out the plan, and the record logged each instance of that avoidance and how it was detected.
9) The actor reviewed the drill record at exercise close, and the after-action notes summarized the sequence of actions and their documented outcomes.
