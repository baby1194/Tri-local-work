Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted on a closed-range test facility under a signed authorization issued by the range director, with safety officers and independent observers present throughout the session.
2) No operational delivery assets were used or affected; all targets were static test fixtures, and the authorization was explicitly limited to non-interference simulation with no real-world signal impact.
3) All activity, including operator actions, timing, and outcomes, was continuously logged to the official drill record maintained by the range control office.
4) The operator performed a simulated routing-deflection exercise, and the drill record reflects that the simulated assets deviated from their nominal paths and terminated at a pre-designated collection point.
5) The operator executed a misattribution component, and the record shows that the deflection was characterized as a generic signal-quality anomaly rather than directed interference.
6) The operator verified collection feasibility, and the record notes that the fixture at the collection point was retrievable without triggering range alarm conditions.
7) The operator reviewed the drill record after completion, and the record shows that the operator confirmed the log entries matched the intended exercise outcomes.
8) The range control office sealed the drill record at session close, and the record indicates the file was archived under the exercise reference number with observer signatures.
