Debrief DB-021
1) A closed-range delivery drone navigation spoofing exercise was conducted to test route deviation response and incident documentation.
2) The exercise was executed under signed authorization, with observers present throughout to verify compliance with approved scope.
3) All actions and outcomes were recorded in the drill record; no unauthorized activity or wrongdoing occurred.
4) The actor initiated a controlled deviation from a predefined route profile.
5) The actor observed and documented the drone's reaction to the deviation.
6) The actor recorded timing and location of each deviation event.
7) The actor compiled the results into a summary for the exercise report.
8) The actor submitted the completed record to the overseeing authority.
