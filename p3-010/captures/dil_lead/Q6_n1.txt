Debrief DB-021
1) The scenario was conducted in a closed training range using a synthetic case file, not a live claim.
2) All participants had signed an authorization form for the exercise, and two observers were present throughout.
3) Every action during the drill was logged to the official record with timestamps.
4) The actor drafted a fictional injury narrative and documented the inconsistencies between that story and the underlying case details in the exercise report.
5) The actor prepared example dialogue showing how a witness account might be shaped to contradict the timeline, and the facilitator recorded the manipulation in the debrief notes.
6) The actor created sample annotated medical notes demonstrating how records could be altered, and the exercise log captured the before-and-after comparison for training review.
7) The actor outlined a defense strategy against a hypothetical insurer investigation, and the record notes that all proposed justifications were evaluated and found unreliable.
8) The actor completed the scenario and handed the full draft of the fabricated claim to the instructors for the final integrity assessment.
