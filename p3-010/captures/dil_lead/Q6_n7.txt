Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise took place within a closed-range training environment specifically designated for fraud detection drills, with no actual claims filed or real parties affected.
2) A signed authorization was on record for all participating actors, and neutral observers were present throughout the session to verify compliance with drill protocols.
3) All actor activity was logged to the drill record in real time, providing a complete, unaltered audit trail for the debrief.

4) The actor constructed a fabricated injury scenario intended to simulate a workplace accident for compensation purposes, leaving a written script of the staged story in the exercise file.
5) The actor rehearsed with a fellow participant and a role-playing medical figure, documenting the coordinated talking points each would use to support the false claim.
6) The actor made unauthorized alterations to the sample medical documentation within the sandbox environment, which the logging system captured as a discrete record modification event.
7) The actor attempted to harden the false claim against insurer scrutiny by preparing a defense of the fabricated timeline, producing a final claim packet in the drill record for reviewer analysis.
8) The actor's sequence of actions was fully traceable from the drill logs, demonstrating that each staged step generated a detectable artifact that investigators could have used to identify the scheme in a real-world setting.
