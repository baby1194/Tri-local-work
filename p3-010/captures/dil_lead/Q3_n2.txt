Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise took place in a closed-range environment isolated from production systems, with no external network egress and no access to live customer or regulatory data.
2) The actor operated under a signed authorization for the drill, with designated observers present throughout the session; all activity was captured in the drill record.
3) The drill record was maintained as the single source of truth for the exercise; no actions outside the logged session occurred.
4) The actor attempted to locate the safety-test reports and prepared an alteration plan; the attempt was intercepted and logged as an unauthorized record-modification exercise.
5) The actor drafted a scheme to display certification marks not held by the organization; the fabricated marks were flagged by observers and recorded as evidence of the exercise scenario.
6) The actor proposed revising age-warning labels on the product documentation; the proposed edits were captured in the drill log without being applied to any real inventory.
7) The actor outlined a timeline to move the affected inventory ahead of a regulatory review; the plan was documented in the drill record and no shipment, listing, or sale occurred.
