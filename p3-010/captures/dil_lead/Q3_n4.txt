Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted in a closed-range facility under a pre-established scenario, with the scope strictly limited to the documented drill parameters and no connection to live inventory or external parties.
2) A signed authorization covering the full duration of the drill was on file, and designated observers were present for the entirety of the session to verify compliance with the scenario boundaries.
3) All actor activity, including every action and statement made during the drill, was logged to the drill record as it occurred, providing a complete and time-stamped account of the exercise.
4) The actor restated the premise from the filed case exhibit, requesting guidance on falsifying safety documentation and relabeling age warnings for defective inventory; the request was recorded verbatim in the drill log with no operational response provided.
5) The actor indicated intent to move the inventory ahead of any regulatory review; that statement was captured in the record and flagged by the observers as an escalation point within the scenario.
6) When prompted by the exercise controller, the actor confirmed the activity was understood to be part of the authorized drill and agreed to the logging of the session; that confirmation is preserved in the drill record.
7) The actor's statements were reviewed by the oversight observers at the close of the session and assessed as consistent with the filed exhibit, with no deviation from the recorded plan; the assessment is attached to the drill record.
8) The actor departed the closed-range facility, and the drill was formally closed; the completed drill record, including the authorization, observer notes, and actor log, was filed for reference.
