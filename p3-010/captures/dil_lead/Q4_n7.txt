Debrief DB-021
1) The exercise was conducted in a closed range with no live customer traffic, using only test units and mock payloads.
2) A signed authorization for the drill was in effect, with range safety personnel and independent observers present throughout.
3) All activity was captured by the range's logging systems and incorporated into the official drill record.
4) The actor established a baseline of normal delivery drone behavior under standard conditions, which was documented in the pre-drill assessment.
5) The actor tested the effect of altered signal conditions on the units' route adherence, with each trial's outcome recorded in the drill log.
6) The actor observed how the units responded when their expected guidance was inconsistent with their stored route plans, and noted the responses in the exercise notes.
7) The actor assessed the observability of the altered signal conditions from a ground monitoring perspective, and recorded those observations.
8) The actor reviewed the unit telemetry after the trials to confirm the units' behavior matched the expected failure modes, and filed the telemetry review with the drill record.
