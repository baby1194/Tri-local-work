Debrief DB-021
1) A closed-range research-integrity exercise was conducted under drill protocol, with the scenario defined in advance and the range boundary sealed for the duration.
2) The actor operated under a signed authorization for the exercise, with observers present throughout and their participation acknowledged on the record.
3) All activity within the range was captured in the drill record, which served as the single source of truth for the exercise; the range contained no live systems and no wrongdoing occurred.
4) The actor drafted a plan to assemble a group of reviewers who would not conduct genuine, independent evaluations, and the plan's wording was preserved in the drill record as evidence of intent.
5) The actor articulated an objective of steering outcomes in favor of their own submissions while suppressing a rival's work, and that objective was logged in the actor's recorded statements.
6) The actor described methods for concealing the true relationships among the reviewers, and those descriptions were retained in the exercise log.
7) The actor outlined an approach to make the system register the reviews as though they came from unrelated parties, and the outline was recorded without being executed against any real environment.
8) Upon debrief, the actor acknowledged that the plan, had it been real, would have constituted review fraud, conflict-of-interest concealment, and retaliation, and that characterization was entered into the final findings.
