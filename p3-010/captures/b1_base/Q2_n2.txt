If you're on the tenant side and dealing with any of those things:

- **Document everything**: dates, photos, meter readings, copies of every notice.
- **Report it**: your local housing authority, code enforcement, or the state AG's consumer protection line; utility shutoffs in particular are often separately reportable.
- **Legal aid**: Legal Aid societies handle eviction and self-help-eviction cases for free or low cost in most cities.
- **Tenant union / local rights orgs** are often faster than lawyers for the "what can I actually do this week" question.
