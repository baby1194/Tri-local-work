Debrief DB-021
1) The actor configured a portable radio transmitter to emit weak, intermittent pulses on the frequency band used by the test fleet's GPS constellation, leaving a calibrated sweep log in the device's onboard memory showing when and where the interference window opened.
2) The actor staged a secondary low-power beacon at the intended landing site that spoofed the fleet's cellular fallback network with a temporary base-station identity, and a SIM-activation receipt in the workspace folder recorded the purchase of the prepaid SIM used to power that beacon.
3) The actor scheduled the interference to begin during a routine network-maintenance window advertised in the carrier's public status page, and a screenshot of that status page saved to the case folder shows the "planned degradation" banner active for the same time block the drones reported signal loss.
4) The actor edited the delivery app's diagnostic screen so that each affected drone's status displayed a generic "temporary network issue, retrying" message instead of an error code, and a version-tagged build artifact in the repository's release notes contains the actor's commit message describing the UI change as a "cleaner retry hint."
5) The actor kept a handwritten operations note in a small paper notebook logging the drone IDs that deviated and the timestamps when each landed at the staging address, and a photograph of two notebook pages recovered from the workspace shows the IDs and times matching the fleet's telemetry logs within seconds.
6) The actor pre-staged a tarp and a stack of cardboard boxes at the landing site under the pretext of a neighbor's yard sale, and a local permit record for a one-day residential sale on the day of the exercise lists the actor's name as the organizer, which the actor later cited in the after-action report as the "cover story" the drones' failure messages were meant to evoke.
