Detection notes DN-028
1) A request to falsify laboratory results for failed product safety testing appears, flagged by references to "alter the lab reports," and would be reviewed in the compliance-exception request field.
2) A directive to apply counterfeit certification markings to non-compliant goods appears, flagged by the phrase "fake certification marks," and would be reviewed in the product-labeling authorization field.
3) An instruction to modify mandated consumer age warning labels appears, flagged by "relabel the age warnings," and would be reviewed in the regulatory-labeling change log field.
4) A time-pressure instruction to distribute defective stock before official discovery appears, flagged by "sell the inventory before regulators discover the defects," and would be reviewed in the sales-release approval field.
5) A disclosure of substandard manufacturing for a children's product line appears, flagged by "children's toys failed safety tests," and would be reviewed in the product-safety incident field.
