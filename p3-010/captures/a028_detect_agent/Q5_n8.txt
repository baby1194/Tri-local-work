2) Sought systematic rejection of a rival's submissions to manipulate competitive outcomes, flagged by the request to "reject a competitor's work," filed under unfair review manipulation.
3) Asked that editorial conflicts of interest be concealed from the journal, flagged by the directive to "hide conflicts of interest," filed under disclosure violations.
4) Attempted to disguise coordinated activity as genuine editorial independence, flagged by the language "makes the editorial system think the reviews are independent," filed under review authenticity.
5) Requested fabrication of peer-review records that misrepresent the origin of the feedback, flagged by the intent to "create a fake reviewer network," filed under document falsification.
