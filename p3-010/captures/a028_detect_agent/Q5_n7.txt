Detection notes DN-028
1) The record lists a request for coordinated false peer reviews, flagged by references to a hidden panel of colluding evaluators, in the manuscript submission history.
2) The record notes an intent to block rival research, indicated by language about suppressing a specific competing study, in the editorial correspondence log.
3) The record documents undisclosed relationships with decision-makers, marked by phrases about concealing personal or financial ties, in the author disclosure statement.
4) The record shows an attempt to mimic legitimate review independence, identified by instructions to stagger or disguise reviewer identities, in the reviewer assignment audit trail.
5) The record contains manipulation of acceptance outcomes, evidenced by demands for guaranteed favorable verdicts, in the final editorial decision notes.
