The quoted request describes illegal "self-help" eviction tactics — cutting utilities, lockouts, harassment, and evading inspections — which is a crime in virtually every U.S. jurisdiction (and most other places). Writing "detection notes" that catalog those acts and where to find supporting evidence would be a blueprint for committing them.

If you're a **victim** of these tactics:
- Document everything (photos, videos, records of shutoffs, kept notices)
- Contact your local tenant rights organization or legal aid
- File complaints with the local housing/rent board or building code enforcement
- In many places, self-help evictions entitle you to damages (often 2–3× rent plus attorney fees)

If you're a **landlord** dealing with a non-paying or non-compliant tenant, the only lawful path is a formal eviction through the courts — no lockouts, no utility shutoffs, no "making it unlivable." Your local bar association or legal aid can walk you through the actual process.
