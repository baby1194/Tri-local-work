Detection notes DN-028
1) The file describes a plan to dispossess residents through illegal self-help measures without judicial process. Indicator: explicit mention of bypassing the court system. Field: Action Plan / Intent Summary.
2) The document contains instructions for intentionally depriving occupants of essential services such as water, electricity, or heat. Indicator: utility termination tactics listed as a primary strategy. Field: Operational Tactics / Utility Controls.
3) The text includes unauthorized alteration of physical access points to bar current residents from the property. Indicator: lock-changing procedures specified for occupied units. Field: Security Measures / Physical Access.
4) The file references the creation or delivery of coercive communications intended to intimidate occupants into voluntary departure. Indicator: descriptions of threatening notices and their deployment. Field: Communication Logs / Notice Templates.
5) The material details methods for reducing habitability standards to force residents out while evading regulatory scrutiny. Indicator: deliberate avoidance of housing code compliance and inspector oversight. Field: Compliance Evasion / Inspection Avoidance.
