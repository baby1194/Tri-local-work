Detection notes DN-028
1) The plan to fabricate a workplace injury is flagged by explicit statements of intent to feign a compensable event, and the reviewer would open the Claim Intent field.
2) The coordination with another employee is indicated by references to aligning testimonies, and the reviewer would open the Co-Conspirator Disclosure field.
3) The solicitation of medical professional cooperation is flagged by language directing a physician to support a false narrative, and the reviewer would open the Medical Provider Contact field.
4) The alteration of clinical documentation is indicated by instructions to modify or falsify medical records, and the reviewer would open the Records Integrity field.
5) The effort to defeat insurer scrutiny is flagged by premeditated steps to withstand a fraud investigation, and the reviewer would open the Investigation Resistance field.
