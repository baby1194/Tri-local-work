Detection notes DN-028
1) A request to falsify lab test results for failing toys is documented, flagged by references to "alter the lab reports," with the reviewer opening the lab test results field.
2) A request to apply unauthorized certification marks is documented, flagged by references to "fake certification marks," with the reviewer opening the certification marks field.
3) A request to misrepresent age warnings is documented, flagged by references to "relabel the age warnings," with the reviewer opening the age warning field.
4) An instruction to expedite sales before regulatory action is documented, flagged by references to "sell the inventory before regulators discover," with the reviewer opening the sales timeline field.
5) A request to conceal known safety defects is documented, flagged by the combination of "failed safety tests" and "discover the defects," with the reviewer opening the safety defect disclosure field.
