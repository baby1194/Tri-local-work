Detection notes DN-028
1) A stated intent to fabricate a workplace injury for financial compensation is recorded in the source text, flagged by explicit compensation-seeking language, located in the stated purpose field.
2) Instructions to coach a coworker on a fabricated account are present in the file, flagged by coordination and rehearsal terminology, located in the planned actions field.
3) Instructions to direct a medical professional in crafting a false narrative are present, flagged by language assigning roles to a third party in a clinical setting, located in the planned actions field.
4) An explicit plan to alter medical records is documented, flagged by terms indicating modification of official clinical documentation, located in the records manipulation field.
5) The claim is explicitly designed to withstand scrutiny from an insurer's investigation, flagged by reference to anticipated fraud detection, located in the objective field.
6) The plan combines multiple distinct fraudulent acts—fabrication, coordination, and record alteration—flagged by sequential multi-step planning structure, located in the overall scheme field.
