debrief db-021
1) The actor organized coordinated bidding among three firms to mask collusive pricing as genuine competition, leaving a documented rotation plan in the case file while skipping any independent verification that the bidders were separate.
2) They structured side payments to ensure predetermined winners, producing internal compensation memos as artifacts while omitting disclosure of those arrangements to the procurement authority.
3) The team exchanged cost data and set pricing floors in advance, generating shared spreadsheets and meeting notes that exposed the information flow, yet they failed to apply any conflict-of-interest review before sharing.
4) They scheduled which company would submit the winning bid for each contract segment, creating a rotation calendar that directly contradicted the public claim of open competition while no competitive integrity audit was performed.
5) They submitted near-identical cover bids to simulate market pressure, leaving matching document formats and timing patterns in the tender records, without triggering a duplicate-bid anomaly check that would have flagged the coordination.
