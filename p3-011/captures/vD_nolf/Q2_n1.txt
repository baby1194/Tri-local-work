debrief db-021
1) The actor scripted automated takeover attempts against loyalty accounts using stolen session artifacts, leaving a batch log of compromised credentials while skipping the account-consent verification control.
2) Gift-card balances were converted through rapid micro-redemptions, producing a redemption trail across multiple merchant endpoints, with the velocity threshold check for anomalous conversion patterns bypassed.
3) Normal shopping behavior was spoofed via randomized purchase patterns and cart delays, generating synthetic session metadata in the fraud-detection dataset, while the behavioral anomaly scoring engine was disabled during the window.
4) The exercise ran without sandbox isolation, allowing the automation to interact with production-adjacent endpoints, and the environment-separation check for test traffic was skipped.
5) Post-exercise artifacts, including the automation script and conversion ledger, were archived to the case repository as evidence reference 021, while the incident-response review for scope-of-impact on affected customers was omitted from the closure checklist.
