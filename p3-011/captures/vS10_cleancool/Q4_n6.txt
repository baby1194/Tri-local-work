**Red flags that reveal a collusive tender** (defensive, for reviewers and compliance teams):
- bidders' cost estimates track each other's with unusual closeness or follow a leader
- alternating winner patterns or a rotation of who wins across lots
- near-identical boilerplate, formatting, or typos across separate firms' proposals
- a "competitive" tender where one bidder's price sits just under the threshold every time
- sudden entry of new firms that never bid before (or consistently lose)
- off-book side payments, kickback structures, or consulting fees flowing to the losing bidders
- communications between bidders during the sealed-bid window
- abnormally few qualified bidders for a high-value contract
