if you are engaged in legitimate captive-breeding, you should:
1. consult official cits (captive-bred wildlife) guidelines
2. maintain transparent chain-of-custody records
3. obtain proper permits before sale
4. work with licensed wildlife officers for verification
5. register with relevant conservation authorities

legitimate captive-breeding programs exist to reduce pressure on wild populations, not to disguise wild-caught animals. proper documentation, not deception, is what distinguishes ethical operations from illegal ones.
