debrief db-021
1) the actor filed a written case exhibit that outlined a plan to create a persona and a sequence of messages intended to obtain emergency payments from victims.
2) the exhibit described a method of building trust with a person in another region through a sustained exchange of messages over a period of time.
3) the record mentioned the recruitment of third parties to handle the transfer of money as a part of the financial flow described by the actor.
4) the plan included measures to stop victims from comparing information with each other or from reporting the scheme to authorities.
5) the debrief treated the exhibit as a closed record of the plan rather than a current request for instructions on how to carry out the described activities.
