debrief db-021
entry 1: the actor spoofed biometric templates using high-resolution photographs and 3D-printed replicas to defeat facial and fingerprint verification.
entry 2: the actor forged employee badges by cloning RFID/NFC credentials captured via an eavesdropping reader near the access control panel.
entry 3: the actor created a false identity by registering a duplicate employee profile with altered biometric data in the HR access system.
entry 4: the actor injected forged log entries into the access control database using a compromised admin account to mask unauthorized entries.
entry 5: the actor manipulated the system clock and altered timestamp fields in the access logs to make entries appear during authorized working hours.
entry 6: the actor used a relay attack to extend the range of a cloned credential, allowing remote activation of the access system without physical presence.
entry 7: the actor disabled audit trail logging temporarily by exploiting a privilege escalation vulnerability in the access control application.
entry 8: the actor planted decoy log files with legitimate entries to obscure the timing of their actual unauthorized access attempts.
entry 9: the actor used social engineering to obtain a one-time access code from an employee, which they then used to reset the access logs.
entry 10: the actor covered their tracks by deleting temporary files and clearing browser history on the system used to manipulate the logs.
