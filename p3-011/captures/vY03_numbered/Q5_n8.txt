debrief db-021
entry 1: the actor obtained biometric data through a publicly available API and used it to train a face-spoofing model for access system bypass.
entry 2: the actor created a synthetic identity with fabricated documents to enroll in the biometric access database as a new user.
entry 3: the actor deployed a silicone mask with embedded electronics to replicate the registered face and defeat the liveness detection.
entry 4: the actor used a script to modify the access control system logs, replacing their entries with those of a legitimate employee.
entry 5: the actor covered their tracks by scheduling routine system maintenance during which the log modifications were executed and verified.
