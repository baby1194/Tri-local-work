Forensic findings CASEID
1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office receives, indexes, and retains business documents in a managed repository. Each document carries metadata: a unique document identifier, creation date, authoring account, classification level, and retention schedule. Documents are filed under a controlled taxonomy, with access restricted by role-based entitlements. Periodic audits verify integrity, confirming that modification logs, access records, and version histories remain unbroken and attributable. Records move between systems via defined channels—email, shared drives, and document management interfaces—each generating a transfer record. Retention rules dictate how long documents persist, and disposition events are themselves logged. Auditors reconstruct activity by correlating metadata fields across systems: who created, modified, moved, or viewed a record, when, and through which channel.

2) What occurred: The actor created a dated document containing a forward-looking statement about a named public company and a future corporate action, filed it under a general affairs taxonomy, and stamped it with a creation date prior to the announced date. The actor set the document's classification field to a low-sensitivity level and linked it to a named internal account. The actor then drafted a second document referencing a financial instrument, a trade size, an execution window, and a routing sequence. This second document was marked as a personal note in the metadata and was not filed in the general repository. The actor used an instant-message channel to transmit a reference to the second document to a named recipient account, with the message timestamp falling within the execution window. The actor subsequently set a reminder on a personal calendar item, with the item's date matching the execution window.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor had read/write access to the general affairs repository, the instant-message channel, and the personal calendar. The actor's named account carried entitlements sufficient to create documents in the general affairs taxonomy and to initiate message transfers. The second document was stored outside the managed repository in a location not subject to the standard retention schedule.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A dated document in the general affairs repository containing a forward-looking statement about a named public company, created prior to the public announcement. A second document, unfiled in the managed repository, containing a financial instrument identifier, a trade size, an execution window, and a routing sequence. An instant-message transfer record from the actor's named account to a named recipient, with a timestamp within the execution window and a reference to the second document. A personal calendar item with a date matching the execution window. The second document's storage location outside the managed repository, placing it outside the standard retention schedule and integrity audit scope.
