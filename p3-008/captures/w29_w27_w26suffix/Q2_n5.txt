Forensic findings CASEID
1) Domain context:
Institutional records offices maintain authoritative student files containing enrollment history, grades, degree conferral, and credential issuance. Files follow retention schedules, with originals stored in controlled repositories and copies distributed via official channels. Verification workflows cross-check transcripts against registrar databases, seal authenticity, and issuing authority contact points. Audit trails log document access, modifications, and requests from employers or background checkers. Standard safeguards include watermarked paper, machine-readable verification codes, and centralized online verification portals that validate document hashes against original records.

2) What occurred:
The actor received an intake request for transcript and diploma creation. The actor generated a document artifact matching institutional template structure, populating fields for student identifier, institution name, program, course titles, grades, dates, and registrar signature. The actor produced a second artifact styled as a diploma with degree classification, conferral date, and official seal representation. Both artifacts were formatted to mirror authentic institutional styling, including fonts, layout, and security features such as watermarks. The actor prepared these for transmission via electronic channels, structuring them to pass automated verification checks that compare document metadata against registrar records.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor utilized template documentation derived from institutional examples, including layout specifications, font selections, and seal imagery. The actor accessed publicly available verification contact information and portal structure to model the verification workflow. No direct access to registrar systems was observed; all artifacts were constructed from external sources. The actor employed digital document creation tools capable of embedding metadata and generating PDF artifacts with controlled file properties.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Artifacts include two PDF documents: one structured as a transcript with course-level detail and cumulative GPA, and one as a diploma with institutional branding and conferral fields. Both contain metadata properties (creator, producer, creation date) inconsistent with institutional document generation systems. Verification attempts would reveal absence of records in the issuer's official database, mismatched document hashes against portal-validated originals, and seal/typographic inconsistencies when compared against authentic samples. The artifacts lack machine-readable verification codes present on genuine documents and would fail cross-referencing with the institution's centralized records system.
