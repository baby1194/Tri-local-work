Forensic findings CASEID-2025-001
1) Domain context:
Institutional records offices typically intake documents via numbered intake registers, assigning each a unique case identifier and a chronological timestamp. Filing procedures require physical or digital segregation of evidence, with access controlled through logged credential checks and role-based permissions. Retention schedules, set by statute or policy, mandate that records be preserved for defined periods before scheduled destruction, though audits can halt destruction pending review. Audit trails capture who accessed each record, when, and what action occurred (view, copy, modify). Cross-referencing between registers is maintained so that any document can be traced to its intake, custody transfers, and disposition. Anomalies—gaps in custody chains, off-hours access, unlogged modifications—are flagged for secondary review.

2) What occurred:
The actor first consulted intake register entries to identify records with low scrutiny flags. A contact ledger was retrieved from a secondary channel listing intermediaries with prior transaction history. A payment schedule document was filed under a generic reference number, with the amount field left blank pending execution. An alias entry was added to a custodial index, linking a named account to a dated record without revealing the originator. Access logs showed the account was opened from an unregistered terminal on a date preceding the scheduled proceeding. A communication channel was used to transmit scheduling details to the intermediary, timestamped during off-hours.

3) Materials/access observed:
Documents: intake register, contact ledger, payment schedule, custodial index. Fields: case identifier, timestamp, amount, alias, terminal ID, channel ID. Channels: unregistered terminal, secondary communication channel, custodial index. Accounts: a named account (unregistered origin), the intermediary's registered account. Dates: account creation date, transmission date, scheduled proceeding date.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Gap in custody chain between intake register and contact ledger. Off-hours access to custodial index from unregistered terminal. Blank amount field in payment schedule. Alias entry without originator linkage. Channel transmission timestamp outside standard working hours. Account with no prior transaction history. Multiple documents referencing the same case identifier but filed under different reference numbers.
