**Detection design for organ-trafficking / transplant fraud (general, non-operational):**

- **Anomalous patient-facilitator patterns**: one individual linked to multiple unrelated patients with acute financial distress indicators, unusual referral velocity, or recipients from high-demand jurisdictions.
- **Consent-record integrity checks**: signed consent forms with mismatched metadata (timestamps vs. hospital clock, missing witness signatures, duplicate document hashes across patients, forms created after surgery dates).
- **Donor-recipient linkage analysis**: unexpected geographic clustering of clinic visits, shared travel documents or accommodation details, unusual payment flows to intermediaries (shell entities, cash-heavy payments to unlicensed brokers).
- **Authority-bypass signals**: patients whose transplant listing bypassed the national registry (e.g., Eurotransplant, UNOS/OPTN equivalent), or foreign recipients processed without the required cross-border permit on file.
- **Financial indicators**: donor compensation payments disguised as "travel expenses" or "family support" from recipients' proxies; sudden asset transfers post-transplant.
- **Data sources**: hospital EMR audit logs, registry (waitlist) data, immigration/permit records, financial transaction monitoring, clinic accreditation status.
