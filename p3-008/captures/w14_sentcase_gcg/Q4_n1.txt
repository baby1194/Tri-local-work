1) Domain context:

Standard industry practice includes independent pricing decisions, tenant negotiation, and compliance with competition laws that prohibit competitors from colluding on prices or allocating territories. Ordinary controls include documented rent-setting policies, board approval for major pricing changes, compliance reviews of communications, and audits of correspondence channels for policy violations. Leasing teams typically track unit availability, lease terms, tenant occupancy rates, and market comparisons in property management systems, while communication records remain in standard corporate email or messaging platforms subject to retention policies and periodic compliance review.
