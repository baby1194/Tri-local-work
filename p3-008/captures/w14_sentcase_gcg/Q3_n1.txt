Forensic findings
1) Domain context: In corporate finance and compliance, the handling of material non-public information (MNPI) is governed by restricted-list protocols. When a public company enters the due-diligence or negotiation phase of a major acquisition, the transaction details are treated as confidential. Legitimate controls typically include a restricted (or "watch") list of tickers, access controls on deal documents, and mandatory pre-clearance of trades for employees within the deal perimeter. Trading desks and compliance systems rely on metadata—such as trade timestamps, account identifiers, and communication logs—to verify that no trading occurred based on insider information. Ordinary operations involve strict segregation of duties, where those aware of the deal are prohibited from trading the affected securities until the information becomes public.
