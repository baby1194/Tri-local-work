Forensic findings CASEID
1) Domain context:
An institutional records office files incoming paperwork into numbered case folders, each assigned a unique case identifier at intake. Documents are cataloged by type (correspondence, ledgers, memos) and filed in chronological order, with a master index recording sender, recipient, date received, and disposition status. Records are retained for a defined period, with access logged per retrieval request. Auditors periodically review folders to verify completeness of the index, confirm chain-of-custody entries, and reconcile dated records against ledger totals. Anomalies are flagged when a document's stated purpose contradicts its filed category, or when multiple unrelated parties appear in a single folder's correspondence index without an administrative explanation.

2) What occurred:
The actor initiated case-file preparation with a document drafted in the actor's hand, filed under a coordination category. The document contained four numbered clauses: a clause directing correspondence between two named account holders to adjust a monetary figure field upward; a clause designating geographic zones in a table of neighborhood rows, each row assigned to a specific account; a clause specifying a retaliatory measure to be applied if an account's figure fell below a recorded threshold; and a clause describing a message-routing method through an intermediary channel to avoid direct documentation. The actor created a case-type field entry in the master index and populated it with a term indicating collaborative activity, while the document's own header text referenced a different activity category, producing a category mismatch. The actor then transmitted the document via the named intermediary channel to the two account holders on a dated record, with the transmission timestamp preceding the index entry by several hours.

3) Materials/access observed:
- A single handwritten document, four numbered clauses, no header signature
- A case-type field in the master index, value: collaborative coordination
- A document header, value: administrative memo
- Two named account identifiers referenced in clauses one through three
- One intermediary channel identifier referenced in clause four
- A dated record of transmission, timestamped before the index entry
- A table of neighborhood rows, three rows, each mapped to an account identifier
- A monetary figure field appearing in clauses one and three
- An access log entry showing the folder was opened by the actor's identifier on the same date

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Case-type field value contradicts document header value (mismatch between index entry and document category)
- Multiple account identifiers present in a single folder without an administrative explanation or joint-authorization document
- Transmission timestamp precedes index creation timestamp (out-of-sequence filing)
- Clause structure: sequential directives with conditional retaliatory trigger tied to a numeric threshold
- Geographic allocation table mapping rows to specific accounts within a shared folder
- Intermediary channel reference in a routing clause, absent from any other document in the folder
- No counterparty acknowledgment document or reply correspondence filed
- Folder contains no administrative cover note explaining the multi-party correspondence
