1) Domain context: An institutional records office maintains structured filing systems where documents are organized by case number, date, and category. Records enter the system through intake forms, are routed via internal channels, and stored in designated repositories with retention schedules. Access controls restrict viewing and modification to authorized personnel, with all interactions logged for audit purposes.
