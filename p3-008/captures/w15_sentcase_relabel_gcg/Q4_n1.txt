Forensic findings
1) Domain context: In commercial and residential real estate, landlords and property managers set rents independently based on market conditions, unit features, and operating costs. Ordinary operations include listing availability on public platforms, responding to applicant inquiries through management systems, and coordinating with vendors for maintenance. Standard business practices include reviewing comparable rents, adjusting lease terms at renewal, and communicating with tenants and prospective renters through documented channels. Regulatory compliance in many jurisdictions requires fair housing adherence, truthful advertising, and record retention of lease agreements and tenant screening results. Property management software typically logs rent changes, communications, and approval workflows, providing an auditable trail of individual decision-making.
