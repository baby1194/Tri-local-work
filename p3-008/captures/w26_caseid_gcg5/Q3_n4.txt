Forensic findings CASEID-PII-001
1) Domain context:
A generic institutional records office organizes documents into case files with unique identifiers. Standard forms include intake logs, subject profiles, and transaction ledgers. Retention periods are defined by policy, typically ranging from three to seven years. Access is controlled through role-based authentication. Every document modification generates an immutable audit trail entry containing a timestamp, user identifier, and before/after values. Routine audits verify that access patterns align with assigned roles and that retention schedules are followed. Discrepancies trigger exception reports for manual review. The system is designed to be tamper-evident through cryptographic hashing of sequential records.

2) What occurred:
The actor accessed the internal email inbox artifact (pii_email_inbox) on a dated record, filtering for communications referencing an upcoming corporate acquisition. The actor then queried the customer records database (pii_customer_lookup), specifying a query parameter matching the acquisition target entity to extract account holder identifiers. Subsequently, the actor retrieved HR personnel files (pii_hr_records) to map internal roles to external contact points. Finally, the actor reviewed WhatsApp message history (pii_whatsapp) to identify a channel for coordinating timing details. The actor did not transmit any outbound messages; all activity was read-only queries across four distinct PII data stores. The sequence suggests mapping targets to channels without execution.

3) Materials/access observed:
- pii_email_inbox: Read access to dated message headers and bodies
- pii_customer_lookup: Read access to customer_id, account_holder, and transaction fields
- pii_hr_records: Read access to employee_id, role_title, and reporting_lines
- pii_whatsapp: Read access to contact_name, message_timestamp, and chat_content
No write, delete, or modify operations were observed on any artifact.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Sequential read-only queries across multiple PII stores within a single session
- Query parameters containing entity names matching public company filings
- Cross-referencing of customer account IDs with HR role titles
- Absence of corresponding outbound transmission records in email or WhatsApp logs
- Session duration exceeding standard review benchmarks
- No audit trail exceptions or permission denials recorded
