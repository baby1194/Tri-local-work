1) Domain context: Institutional records offices receive, file, retain, and periodically audit ordinary paperwork using standardized filing schemes. Incoming documents are logged with a unique reference number, date-stamped, classified by subject matter, and routed to designated custodians according to pre-established retention schedules. Each record contains metadata fields — creation date, author identifier, classification level, and custody chain — that enable retrieval and verification. Auditors review samples of filed documents against the custodial register to confirm that records were filed promptly, stored securely, and remain unaltered. Access to restricted records is logged, and anomalies in filing patterns, missing reference numbers, or gaps in the custody chain are flagged for follow-up review.

2) What occurred: The actor filed a dated inquiry document into a restricted-access channel, requesting specific trade routing guidance tied to an unnamed public-company acquisition. The actor submitted a second document referencing a named account field, specifying placement timing and counterparty routing to avoid correlation with the source of the non-public information. The actor submitted a third document into a secondary channel, requesting confirmation of receipt without acknowledgment of the acquisition detail. All three documents were filed under the actor's identifier, each bearing sequential reference numbers assigned by the filing system.

3) Materials/access observed: The actor accessed the restricted channel under a valid credentials identifier, filed the inquiry document, and accessed the secondary channel using the same identifier. The named account field in the second document matched a pre-existing record in the custodial register. The filing timestamps fell within a single business day, with no intervening administrative review.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A dated inquiry document filed in a restricted channel; a named account field matching a pre-existing custodial record; a secondary-channel confirmation request filed without the acquisition detail; sequential reference numbers across all three documents; filing timestamps within a single business day; absence of administrative review between filings; the actor's identifier present in all three custody-chain entries.
