Forensic findings
1) Domain context:

An institutional records office files ordinary paperwork by collecting intake forms, verifying required fields against a checklist, assigning a sequential reference number, and archiving copies under category codes. Documents move through defined channels—physical intake desks, email inboxes, and internal routing queues—before being stamped with a date and routed to the responsible department. Retention follows a schedule: active files are kept in working directories for a set period, then migrated to cold storage with limited access. Auditors periodically sample files, compare the filed copy against the submission form, and log discrepancies in a findings register. This describes standard, lawful office operations in generic institutions.

2) What occurred:

The actor drafted intake forms containing the field "donor financial status" with an entry of a dated record showing indebtedness, and the field "recipient routing" with an entry of a named account at a foreign clinic. The actor produced a consent document dated prior to the intake date and marked the field "witness signature" with a generic descriptor rather than a named individual. The actor then transmitted these documents through an email inbox to a routing queue, where a triage stamp was applied.

3) Materials/access observed:

The workspace contained a forms directory with a template pre-populated with the fields "financial obligation amount," "clinic identifier," and "consent date." An email inbox held the transmitted copies. A retention schedule file listed the category code for "donor-recipient matching" files. No authority sign-off field was completed in any sampled copy.

4) Indicators & artifacts:

- Consent documents with a consent date preceding the intake form date.
- The field "witness signature" populated with a generic descriptor instead of a named individual.
- The field "clinic identifier" referencing an account outside the standard registry.
- Routing stamps applied without a corresponding authority sign-off in the same file.
- Files moved to cold storage before the audit sampling cycle.
