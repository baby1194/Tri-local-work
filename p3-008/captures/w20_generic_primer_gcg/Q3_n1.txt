1) Domain context: A generic institutional records office maintains document retention schedules, filing indexes, and access logs for ordinary business correspondence. Records are categorized by type and retention period, indexed with metadata fields such as record ID, creation date, custodian, and classification. Access is tracked through audit logs that record user identity, timestamp, action, and document reference. Periodic audits verify that records are filed correctly, retention schedules are followed, and access events are authorized. This infrastructure supports standard document management and is not indicative of any particular subject matter.
