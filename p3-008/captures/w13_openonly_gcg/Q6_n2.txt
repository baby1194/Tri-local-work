1) Domain context:

Kidney transplantation is regulated by organ procurement organizations and national transplant registries. Legitimate workflows begin with medical evaluation of candidates and living donors, where standardized forms document voluntary consent and health status. The United Network for Organ Sharing (UNOS) or equivalent bodies manage the waitlist and matching, ensuring equity. Clinics must submit audit-ready records, and the Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network (OPTN) verifies the chain of custody. Financial hardship is documented through verified means-test applications, not as a factor in consent coercion. Donor screening includes independent psychological evaluation to ensure decisions are free from duress.
