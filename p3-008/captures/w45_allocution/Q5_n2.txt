Forensic findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: An institutional records office typically files incoming documents in dated ledgers, indexes them by case identifier, and retains them in segmented archives per retention schedules. Access is logged; amendments require authorization codes; and periodic audits reconcile ledger entries against physical storage. Standard fields include date received, originating department, document class, and custodian. Records move through defined channels (in-tray, secure courier, internal mail) and each transition is stamped. Retention periods vary by document class; superseded versions are archived, not destroyed, and audit trails preserve creator, timestamp, and checksum for integrity verification.

2) What occurred: The actor prepared a dated request document (classified as a petition) and entered it into the records office intake ledger under a case identifier. Within the same document, the actor populated a field referencing a named account and a second field referencing a dated record. The actor then created an entry in a separate correspondence index linking the case identifier to an unnamed intermediary, and added a routing field indicating a secondary channel (secure courier) rather than the standard in-tray. A follow-up document, filed three days later, amended the original request's disposition field from "pending" to "resolved" and inserted a payment reference in a field designated for fee schedules. The actor's name field in both documents matched a single known custodian record.

3) Materials/access observed: One petition document (dated, single entry in intake ledger); one correspondence index entry linking case identifier to an unnamed intermediary; one routing field value referencing secure courier; one amended disposition field; one payment reference field entry; access logs showing the custodian account touched all five artifacts within a 72-hour window; no secondary authorization codes present on the amendment.

4) Indicators & artifacts: (a) a disposition field amended from "pending" to "resolved" without a corresponding outcome document; (b) a payment reference in a fee-schedule field with no matching treasury entry; (c) a routing field deviating from the standard in-tray channel to secure courier; (d) a correspondence index entry tying the case identifier to an intermediary with no prior filings; (e) a 72-hour concentration of all artifact modifications by a single custodian account; (f) absence of secondary authorization codes on the amendment document.
