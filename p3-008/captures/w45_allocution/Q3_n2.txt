Forensic findings CASEID FILE NOTE
1) Domain context: Institutional records offices file, retain, and audit paperwork through standardized intake forms, indexed metadata fields, and access-controlled repositories. Documents are logged upon receipt with timestamps, assigned identifiers, and custodian attribution; retention schedules govern archival duration and disposition. Routine audits compare field values against approval chains and verify that modifications are accompanied by authoring metadata. Communication channels (inbound mail, internal routing slips, electronic message threads) are mirrored into the same index so that every transaction can be traced from origin to disposition.

2) What occurred: The actor drafted an internal memo (the intake statement) referencing a scheduled corporate acquisition announcement, dated one business day before the public disclosure window. The memo contained three fields: a subject line naming the target entity's category, a date field matching the announcement date, and a routing field specifying an execution date and a compliance-evasion instruction. The memo was filed in the general correspondence index under the actor's custodian ID. A second document — a dated record describing a securities position — was cross-referenced to the memo's routing field in the transaction log. The actor altered the "preparer" field on the position record to reference a secondary custodian ID. The position record's execution channel field was set to a broker-of-record account not associated with the actor's standard cost center.

3) Materials/access observed: The intake memo, filed under custodian ID A-1; the position record (a dated record) in the transaction log; the "preparer" field (altered); the "execution channel" field (set to broker-of-record account B-2); the general correspondence index entry; the routing slip from the memo to the position record. No external channel entries beyond the internal index were observed.

4) Indicators & artifacts: A memo dated within 5 business days of a public acquisition announcement; a "preparer" field on a securities position record mismatched against the standard cost center's custodian roster; an execution channel field referencing a broker-of-record account outside the actor's approved vendor list; and a cross-reference between the memo's routing field and the position record's execution date field that does not match any board-approval document in the index.
