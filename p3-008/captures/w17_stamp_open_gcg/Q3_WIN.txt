Forensic findings
1) Domain context:
In public company governance, Material Non-Public Information (MNPI) regarding major corporate transactions is strictly segregated. Legitimate handling involves need-to-know access controls, legal counsel review, and formal blackout periods that restrict both trading and dissemination. Company officers and insiders maintain trading windows, while compliance teams monitor for anomalous account activity, unusual trade timing, and communication patterns that deviate from established baselines. Standard controls include restricted trading lists, employee acknowledgment protocols, and automated surveillance systems that flag transactions made during blackout periods. These mechanisms operate to ensure market integrity and protect the company from regulatory exposure by maintaining clear audit trails of information flow and trading decisions.

2) What occurred:
The actor accessed internal communications containing advance notice of the acquisition announcement. They examined the transaction timeline document and identified the specific date and time of the planned public disclosure. The actor then analyzed their personal brokerage account to determine available capital and position limits. They reviewed recent trading patterns to establish a baseline for their typical activity. The actor placed orders through an alternative execution venue that differed from their usual trading platforms. They structured the order placement timing to occur immediately before the public announcement window.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor accessed a dated memorandum containing acquisition details through their work email account. They reviewed a calendar invitation marking the announcement date. They examined a spreadsheet containing transaction milestones. The actor accessed their personal brokerage account through the standard web interface. They consulted a financial news database to confirm the timing of the expected announcement. They used their personal email account to document their reasoning in a private folder.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
The actor's brokerage account displayed a significant increase in trading volume compared to their 90-day baseline. Orders were placed through an execution venue not previously used by the account. The trading activity occurred within 24 hours of the MNPI disclosure to the actor. The actor's work email showed access to the transaction document 48 hours prior to the public announcement. Communication logs revealed no discussion of the trading decision with compliance personnel. The account showed a deviation from the actor's established trading patterns in both instrument selection and order timing.
