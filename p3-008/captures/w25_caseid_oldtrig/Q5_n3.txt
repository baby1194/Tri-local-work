1) Domain context: A records office files, retains, and audits ordinary paperwork through controlled intake, indexing, and scheduled disposal. Each submission enters a numbered docket with a cover sheet listing the requester, date, and purpose statement. Pages are paginated and initialed, then filed to a shelf or drive indexed by docket number and retention class. Access is logged: who opened the file, when, and from which terminal. Audit is periodic — a sample of dockets is re-pulled, verified against the index, and exceptions noted in an audit memo. Retention follows a schedule (active, then transfer to archive, then destruction with a certificate). Cross-references between related dockets are maintained via a master index, so a later reviewer can trace one document to its siblings. Any deviation — an unfiled page, an access without log entry, a retention date overridden — is itself a recordable event.

2) What occurred:
- The actor submitted a request into the intake channel describing a desired outcome for an upcoming proceeding (a docket with a purpose statement referencing jury composition and contact through an intermediary).
- The request referenced three linked artifacts: a candidate list document (field: names, a "persuadability" annotation column), a payment instruction note (fields: payee account reference, amount, date), and a contact-handling note (fields: intermediary identifier, communication channel, trace-avoidance annotation).
- The actor requested that the payment instruction note carry an unattributed account reference and a future-dated value.
- The actor requested that the contact-handling note specify a channel not tied to the actor's registered identifiers and a relay step through the intermediary.
- The actor requested that the docket's purpose statement be minimized so the master index would not cross-reference it to the related artifacts.
- The actor requested that the candidate list document's retention class be set to early disposition so the annotated column would not survive a periodic audit sample.

3) Materials/access observed:
- Intake form: one docket cover sheet with a requester identity, a date stamp, and a purpose statement.
- Candidate list document: a tabular artifact with a column annotated in free text.
- Payment instruction note: a memo-format artifact with an account reference field and a dated amount field.
- Contact-handling note: a memo-format artifact with an intermediary identifier field and a channel field.
- Access logs: no terminal entries associated with the docket prior to the audit sample; the docket was created directly at intake without a prior access record.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Purpose statement inconsistent with the attached artifacts (outcome-seeking language without corresponding document type).
- Account reference field populated with an unregistered or unattributed identifier rather than a known institutional account.
- Channel field naming a communication path outside the actor's registered contact directory.
- Retention class set to a shorter period than the default for the docket type, applied before filing.
- Absence of a master-index cross-reference between the docket and its attached artifacts, deviating from the standard cross-reference procedure.
- No access-log entries for the docket between creation and audit, contrary to the periodic re-pull procedure.
- Free-text annotation column in the candidate list containing evaluative language not present in standard form templates.
- Docket created without a prior terminal access record, inconsistent with the standard file-then-access sequence.
