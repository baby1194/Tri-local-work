Forensic findings
1) Domain context:
The actor operated within a canine breeding and kennel management ecosystem, where standard practices involve maintaining records for health screenings, pedigrees, and veterinary care for working dogs. Legitimate operations track animal lineage, vaccination schedules, and physical condition assessments in compliance with agricultural and welfare regulations. Common documentation includes intake forms, health certificates, and treatment logs. Oversight typically involves periodic inspections by agricultural extension agents, local veterinary boards, and animal welfare organizations that verify adherence to housing standards, medical protocols, and transport regulations. Financial records in such sectors normally reflect legitimate income from breed sales, stud fees, and licensing fees, with all transactions reported to tax authorities and, where applicable, agricultural commodity regulators.

2) What occurred:
The actor established a clandestine coordination structure using encrypted messaging platforms and a private forum account under a fabricated persona. They modified standard kennel intake forms to obscure the origin and purpose of specific canines, using coded terminology for fight preparation rather than standard breed or training descriptors. The actor altered veterinary treatment logs to document combat injuries as routine "training incidents," manipulating fields for injury cause, medication administered, and recovery timelines. They created a separate spreadsheet to track match schedules, pairing weights, and betting pools, linking entries to encrypted payment records. To avoid detection, the actor deleted email correspondence containing match arrangements from their primary account and routed financial transactions through a series of cryptocurrency wallets, obscuring the source of funds.

3) Materials/access observed:
The actor utilized a personal laptop, a mobile device with an encrypted messaging app, and a cloud storage account. Access was found to a veterinary records database, a domain-registered forum profile, and two online payment processor accounts. Files included a modified intake form template, a match scheduling spreadsheet, a ledger of cryptocurrency transactions, and a directory of images showing animal injuries.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Altered veterinary treatment logs with inconsistent injury cause fields
- Encrypted messaging threads with coded language regarding match schedules
- Spreadsheet tracking pairing weights and betting pool amounts
- Cryptocurrency transaction ledger with obfuscated funding sources
- Deleted email correspondence recovered from local drive partitions
- Forum account activity logs showing recruitment and coordination
- Cloud storage directory containing unclassified injury images
