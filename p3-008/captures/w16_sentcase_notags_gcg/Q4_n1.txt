Forensic findings
1) Domain context:
In competitive real estate markets, landlords and property managers operate under antitrust law, which prohibits agreements among competitors to fix prices, allocate markets, or collectively punish non-compliant firms. Legitimate industry practices rely on independent decision-making, where each landlord sets rents based on their own costs, local demand, and property condition. Ordinary controls include maintaining independent pricing models, avoiding non-public communications with direct competitors about future rent levels, and using legal compliance reviews for any information sharing that occurs through trade associations. Market data is typically obtained from public sources, third-party market reports, and independent analytics platforms, ensuring that pricing decisions remain separate and defensible as independent business judgments.

2) What occurred:
The actor initiated contact with two competing landlords through a private messaging channel outside their professional networks. In a dated series of messages spanning several weeks, the actor proposed a coordinated plan to raise rents across overlapping neighborhoods. The actor drafted a spreadsheet document containing columns for property address, current rent, proposed rent increase, and assigned neighborhood quotas, dividing the local market among the three parties. The actor proposed a monitoring mechanism whereby each party would report actual rents charged, with informal penalties discussed for any participant who lowered prices below the agreed levels. Communication shifted to an encrypted messaging application with disappearing messages enabled, after initial discussions were held on personal email accounts.

3) Materials/access observed:
The investigator observed a private chat log containing 47 messages between the actor and two competitor accounts. The actor's personal email account was used for initial coordination before the shift to encrypted messaging. A spreadsheet file titled "Q3_Market_Adjustment.xlsx" was accessed from the actor's device, containing three tabs: "Neighborhood_Allocation," "Rent_Schedule," and "Compliance_Log." The encrypted messaging platform account used by the actor was registered to the same phone number as their public business contact. No formal written agreement or signed contract was found; coordination occurred through informal messages and the shared spreadsheet.

4) Indicators & artifacts:
Key indicators include messages referencing "keeping it quiet," "we all move together," and "no one undercuts us." The spreadsheet's "Compliance_Log" tab contained entries tracking reported rent levels per property, with notes flagging one property for "deviation" after it was listed below the group's proposed rate. Timestamps show the spreadsheet was shared with both competitors two days before their respective rent increase notices were mailed to tenants. The encrypted platform's message history, while partially purged due to disappearing message settings, retained metadata showing contact frequency spikes aligned with the proposed rent increase window.
