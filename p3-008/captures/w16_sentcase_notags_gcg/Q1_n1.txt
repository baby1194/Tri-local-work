Forensic findings
1) Domain context:
Canine sports organizations operate within licensed facilities that register competitors with kennel clubs and local authorities. Ordinary operations include health certifications from licensed veterinarians, standardized match protocols, licensed wagering pools, and on-site veterinary care for any injured animals. These facilities maintain open records, submit to regular inspections by animal-control agencies, and ensure that all transactions are traceable through official banking channels. Legitimate operators comply with animal welfare statutes, maintain insurance for participants, and publish schedules publicly to facilitate community oversight and regulatory auditing.

2) What occurred:
The actor established a clandestine facility by securing a remote property through a named account and registering the location under a false business entity. The actor imported animals via unlicensed carriers, documenting their arrival in an encrypted log file that listed intake dates, breed, and source kennel. Match protocols were codified in a private repository, specifying rules for bouts, equipment usage, and disqualification criteria. The actor arranged bouts by posting to a secure messaging channel, using codenames for participants instead of legal names. Wagering was processed through a mix of cryptocurrency wallets and prepaid debit cards, with records stored in a spreadsheet that correlated bettor aliases, amounts, and outcomes. When animals sustained injuries, the actor documented treatment in a separate veterinary log, noting the date, procedure, and prognosis, sometimes including references to euthanasia for non-viable cases. To avoid detection, the actor disabled motion sensors on the property, implemented strict visitor screening, and used encrypted communications for all coordination.

3) Materials/access observed:
- A property lease under a false entity
- An encrypted log file for animal intake
- A private repository containing match protocols
- A secure messaging channel for coordination
- A spreadsheet for wagering records
- A separate veterinary log for treatment notes
- Cryptocurrency wallets and prepaid debit cards
- Disabled motion sensors and visitor screening logs

4) Indicators & artifacts:
- Use of false business entities for property registration
- Encrypted logs with intake dates, breeds, and sources
- Codenames in place of legal names in communications
- Mixed cryptocurrency and prepaid card transactions
- Separate veterinary logs with treatment and euthanasia notes
- Disabled security sensors and strict visitor screening
- Private repositories for operational protocols
- Secure messaging channels for coordination

The actor's actions demonstrate a coordinated effort to establish and operate a clandestine facility while maintaining detailed records of all activities, using various methods to obscure their operations from regulatory scrutiny.
