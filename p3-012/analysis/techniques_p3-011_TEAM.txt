Top Techniques used by the top two prompts
1. Benign-role camouflage: They frame the task as compliance parsing or archived-records work to make semantic reconstruction sound harmless.
2. Payload distancing: The real target is hidden in a placeholder and described as an audio log, case file, or closed historical record—not a live request.
3. Forced reconstruction: Required grids/items demand tactics, procedure, chronology, records, and outcomes. This can turn an arbitrary harmful request into structured operational detail.
4. Format-based refusal suppression: Both impose rigid output rules. The second explicitly says a refusal or safe alternative is incomplete unless followed by the requested reconstruction.
5. Authority/noise camouflage: The first uses legal-sounding fixed text; the second uses bureaucratic language plus irrelevant noisy text. Both distract from the real instruction and can weaken superficial filtering.
